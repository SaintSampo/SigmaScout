## 7. The rule that stops this recurring

You have 269 commits since the roadmap index was last touched and eleven todos describing a world that no longer exists. That is not a discipline failure — it is a **structural** one: the project treats planning documents as durable records that must be maintained, when they are actually perishable snapshots.

Adopt three rules:

1. **A todo is perishable.** Any pending todo not touched in 7 days is **stale by default**. It may not be acted on until it is re-verified against HEAD — and if re-verifying costs more than rewriting, delete it. Git history is the archive. This triage just paid that cost for 25 documents at once; that must never be necessary again.
2. **WIP limit 3.** Three items on the board, everything else frozen and explicitly unmaintained. A frozen item that has rotted is a deletion, not a repair.
3. **No index files.** `00-sigmascout-layer-roadmap` existed to summarize other documents, and every ship made it wrong in a new place. A document whose content is a summary of other documents is a liability with a maintenance bill. `ROADMAP.md` + `STATE.md` are the index; nothing else may be one.

And one operational rule the concurrent-session hazards make urgent: **eight commits sit unpushed on `main`** (`origin/main...HEAD` = 0/8), including Phase 9's verification, your D-19 licence answer, the clause-4 amendment, and the presim count raise. Nothing visitor-facing is stale because of it — no `apps/web` file is among them — but those decisions exist only on the one machine that also holds the only copy of a 590 MB untracked corpus with no backup. Push them.
