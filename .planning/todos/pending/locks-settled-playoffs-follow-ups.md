# Follow ups from settling a knocked out team's playoff points

## Why this matters

Quick task 261008-26o made both Locks tabs settle a team's Playoffs cell grey as soon as its alliance's bracket placement is decided (`settledPlayoffPoints` in `apps/web/src/components/districts/districtLedgerRows.ts`, read by `districtLedgerStatus.ts` and `champLedgerStatus.ts` through `row.settledElim` and `ChampLedgerSource.settledElim`). The settled value replaces the whole Playoffs ceiling: TBA's own final number joins the floor, a placement table value joins only the ceiling. On the PNW fixture's FNC like stop rewound over the finished DCMP (Playoffs open, every alliance but the finalists decided) that locks frc5468 where the blunt rule locked nobody. Five things were left.

3. **The dropped unpicked rule, and a measurement before any revival.** The plan proposed settling a team left off every alliance at 0 once alliance selection is final. It was dropped by the orchestrator under Jacob's rule that a team is Locked only if it is impossible for it not to qualify: a backup robot is called from the unpicked pool during the playoffs and TBA pays it the alliance's placement points (`SuppliedAlliance` doc in `packages/core/districts/ledgerSimulation.ts`), so an unpicked team's playoff points are not settled until the playoffs are final. The 2026 PNW fixture holds a live example: frc7034 carries 0 alliance selection points and 12 DCMP playoff points. Before reviving it in any form, measure in the corpus how often a team absent from the final alliance list at selection earned playoff points, and whether any such team would have flipped a Locked display.
