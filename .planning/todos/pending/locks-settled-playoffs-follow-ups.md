# Follow ups from settling a knocked out team's playoff points

## Why this matters

Quick task 261008-26o made both Locks tabs settle a team's Playoffs cell grey as soon as its alliance's bracket placement is decided (`settledPlayoffPoints` in `apps/web/src/components/districts/districtLedgerRows.ts`, read by `districtLedgerStatus.ts` and `champLedgerStatus.ts` through `row.settledElim` and `ChampLedgerSource.settledElim`). The settled value replaces the whole Playoffs ceiling: TBA's own final number joins the floor, a placement table value joins only the ceiling. On the PNW fixture's FNC like stop rewound over the finished DCMP (Playoffs open, every alliance but the finalists decided) that locks frc5468 where the blunt rule locked nobody. Five things were left.

## What to do

1. **Retire the `placed` milestone arm.** `buildDistrictLedgerRows` now settles a decided placement as a grey `final` cell before `playoffMilestoneFor` is reached, so the `{ kind: "placed" }` arm of `DistrictPlayoffMilestone` is unreachable from the row builder. Remove it across `districtLedgerRows.ts`, `ledgerVerdict.ts`, `districtLedgerOutcomes.ts` and `LedgerParts.tsx`, with their tests (`districtLedgerOutcomes.test.ts` ~143, `ledgerVerdict.test.ts` ~278).
2. **The offline tenet sweeps cannot exercise the settled set.** `scripts/measureChampTenets.ts` and `scripts/measureLedgerTenets.ts` pass no distributions, and `data/local-publish` holds no event artifacts, so the new Locked displays at playoff Round stops are unswept. A sweep needs the as-of event artifacts (played sets per stop) and the bracket milestones derived from them, then the same tenet check.
3. **The dropped unpicked rule, and a measurement before any revival.** The plan proposed settling a team left off every alliance at 0 once alliance selection is final. It was dropped by the orchestrator under Jacob's rule that a team is Locked only if it is impossible for it not to qualify: a backup robot is called from the unpicked pool during the playoffs and TBA pays it the alliance's placement points (`SuppliedAlliance` doc in `packages/core/districts/ledgerSimulation.ts`), so an unpicked team's playoff points are not settled until the playoffs are final. The 2026 PNW fixture holds a live example: frc7034 carries 0 alliance selection points and 12 DCMP playoff points. Before reviving it in any form, measure in the corpus how often a team absent from the final alliance list at selection earned playoff points, and whether any such team would have flipped a Locked display.
4. **The publisher's own verdicts (observation, nothing changed).** `packages/harness/districtRankingsMerge.ts` builds `maxRemainingDistrict` from whole event `remainingEvents.maxPoints` and `maxRemainingChamp` from one whole hypothetical DCMP (`recomputeDistrictVerdicts`, ~449-462), never from category stages, so it does not share the blunt Playoffs rule this task fixed. But `applyDistrictRankings` (~618-621, the Worker's live refresh path via `apps/worker/src/districtRefresh.ts`, and `scripts/publishDistricts.ts`) drops an event from `remainingEvents` as soon as TBA posts any `event_points` row for it, and `maxRemainingChamp` adds no DCMP once a team has any dcmp row. If TBA posts a row mid event, that event's remaining ceiling is zeroed while its playoffs and awards are still to come, which could publish a `locked` or `eliminated` verdict that is not yet true. Confirmed in code; whether TBA posts rows mid event was not checked (no network in that task). Nothing on the site displays the published verdicts, so this is a data honesty item, not a display bug.
5. **Prorated playoff points: the Now display still prints the placement table value.** TBA pays a team that sat out part of its alliance's playoffs less than the placement's points: 2026pncmp frc3663 sat on the fourth place alliance (worth 21) and was paid 12, and the backup frc7034 (no alliance selection points) was paid 12. Across the local 2023 to 2026 district artifacts, 1 to 2.5% of rows per season and tier carry an `elim` off the placement table, every one of them below a table value. 261008-26o already made the LOCK MATH safe (fix commit 93cabd4d): a value off the placement table (`exact: false`) joins only the ceiling, and only TBA's own final `elim` (`exact: true`, every rewound stop over a finished event) joins the floor. Two things are left. (a) At Now mid playoffs the grey Playoffs cell still PRINTS the placement table value, which overstates for a team that sat out; options are to print it only when `exact`, mark it as at most, or read the played playoff matches' team lists to know who sat out. (b) The core simulation (`ledgerSimulation.ts`) pays every alliance member the full placement value, so the open Playoffs distributions slightly overstate for teams that rotate out. Learn TBA's proration rule from the corpus (played match counts per team against `elim`) before changing either.
