<objective>
**The two numeric foundations deliverable 4 rests on: a re-measured F3 mean deficit restricted to the population the RP layer actually predicts for, and `marginals.ts` — the exact, browser-safe, right-skewed marginal math D-01 and D-08 lock in.**

Nothing ships from this plan. No call site changes, no published byte moves, no config flips. What lands is (a) a number that decides whether 09-05's marginal swap is worth attempting, and (b) the module 09-04, 09-05 and 09-06 all consume.

Three things make this plan load-bearing rather than routine:

1. **Task 1 is a ROADMAP sequencing constraint, not a nice-to-have.** The ROADMAP states it outright: "Re-measure F3's mean deficit **restricted to fully-warm 3/3 rosters** before committing to the marginal swap — the probe that found predicted means low in 33 of 34 season-variables included partially-cold rosters, which the RP-producing population largely excludes." A marginal family chosen against a deficit measured on the wrong population is a fix aimed at the wrong cause.

2. **D-07 traded away the strongest available correctness evidence, and this plan is where the replacement gets paid for.** A one-time Monte Carlo equivalence cross-check was offered — the two forms must agree today, since the joint is fully diagonal — and declined. The required mitigation is hand-computed unit tests. Every expected value in `marginals.test.ts` must come from the closed form, computed independently; expectations lifted from the implementation's own output assert only that the implementation equals itself.

3. **Pitfall 3 is a real NaN path into a published pmf.** `r = mean² / (variance − mean)` divides by zero when `variance = mean` and goes negative when `variance < mean`. The population-level measurement says every real threshold variable is overdispersed (var/mean 1.27-102.3 across 15 variables and 7 seasons) — but that is a population fact, not a per-team, mid-season guarantee, and the accumulator's own arithmetic produces `variance ≤ mean` for a two-observation team on ordinary data. The fallback ladder is shipped, documented, tested, and — critically — **observable**, so a "negative-binomial" arm that quietly resolved to Gaussian half the time cannot pass itself off as one in 09-06's attribution table.

Purpose (D-01, D-08, F2, F3): F2 measured published bonus probabilities under-predicting 2.06× across all ten seasons and 488,002 observations. F3 located the leading cause — a predicted alliance mean systematically 8-15% low, which at a tail threshold costs far more than 8-15% of the probability. D-01's answer is a count-native, right-skewed marginal with an exact discrete CDF; D-08's answer is that the module doing it must be importable by a browser, because deliverable 8 runs the same pure function client-side.

Output: one credential-free measurement script and its committed record, one zero-runtime-import numeric leaf with its hand-computed test suite, and one new entry in the import-graph scan that makes "browser-safe" a checked property rather than a claim.
</objective>

**5. The negative-binomial CDF, log-space, exact at integers.** Compute in log space and exponentiate
per term, never by multiplying raw terms, so a seed that underflows does not zero the whole series.
Both logs are exact and free of cancellation: `logP = log(mean) − log(mean + r)` and
`logQ = log(r) − log(mean + r)`. Seed `logTerm(0) = r · logQ`; step
`logTerm(k) = logTerm(k−1) + logP + log(k + r − 1) − log(k)`.

  `probAtLeast(m, t)` for a discrete family: let `n = Math.ceil(t) − 1`; if `n < 0` return exactly `1`.
  Accumulate the lower sum over `k = 0..n`. If that sum is at most `0.5`, return `1 − sum`. Otherwise
  **continue the same recurrence upward from `k = n+1`** and return the directly-accumulated upper tail
  — this is what keeps a genuine 1e-9 bonus probability from being the cancellation residue of
  `1 − 0.999999999`. Terminate the tail loop when the term falls below `1e-18` **and** `k` is past
  `mean` (the NB mode is `floor((r−1)·mean/r)` for `r > 1` and `0` otherwise, so it never exceeds the
  mean — the guard makes the monotone-decreasing assumption true rather than assumed), or when
  `NB_MAX_TAIL_TERMS` (`100_000`) is reached. Clamp the result into `[0, 1]`.

  `probAtMost(m, t)` for a discrete family is defined as `1 − probAtLeast(m, Math.floor(t) + 1)` — there
  is exactly ONE summation routine, so the two functions are consistent by construction rather than by
  two implementations that happen to agree.
