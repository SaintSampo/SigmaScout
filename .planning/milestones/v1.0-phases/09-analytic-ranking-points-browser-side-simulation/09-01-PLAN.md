<objective>
Build the phase's measurement spine and prove it end-to-end, before any RP math changes.

This is the TRACER plan. Task 1 wires ONE season and ONE algorithm the whole way - published
scorer to optional artifact key to a rendered Compare-page section - and only then do the
expansion tasks widen it and freeze the two "before" instruments every later plan is scored
against.

Purpose: F1 is the audit's headline - ranking points are published with no accuracy measurement
anywhere on the site, and the numbers being published without a scorecard are the ones measured
2.06x miscalibrated. The ROADMAP's hard sequencing constraint is that the scorecard ships BEFORE
any accuracy change, or deliverables 3 and 4 are unmeasurable. D-11 requires both arms of every
later comparison to run through the same published scorer, because a scorer mismatch has already
manufactured a ~0.003 phantom regression in this project. D-09 needs a per-bonus left-hand side to
compare against. D-12 needs a frozen level-1 digest taken before the phase touches anything.

Output: an artifact-emitting mode on the existing scorer, an `.optional()` RP calibration key on
the live Compare artifact schema, an `RpCalibrationSection` on `/methodology/compare`, and two
committed, dated baseline files that plans 09-04, 09-06 and 09-10 consume by name.
</objective>
