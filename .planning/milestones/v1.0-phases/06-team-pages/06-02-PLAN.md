<assumption_delta_decision>
**Signal:** `optional` family — D-09 turns the required `actualWinner`, `actualRedScore` and
`actualBlueScore` fields optional on `TeamSeasonMatchSchema`. (The detector's `pluralization`
signals on the ROADMAP words "alongside" and "second" are false positives: both describe layout,
not an identity model.)

**Primary noun:** *a match this team is scheduled for or has played* — the row itself, not "a played
match". The generalized identity is the schedule entry; a *result* is a property some entries have.

**Decision: `promote`.** The general representation (a match row that may or may not carry a result)
becomes the primary, and the old specific one (a row that always carries a result) is demoted to a
variant distinguished by the presence of `actualWinner`. This is what D-09 chose explicitly: a
*single* match list per event, over mirroring the event artifact's separate `upcoming[]` array. The
`add-alongside` option was on the table and was rejected by name.

**Rationale:** TEAM-04 asks for a section per attended **or upcoming** event. Under the pre-D-09
shape an upcoming match is not merely unrepresented, it is *unrepresentable* — so an
`add-alongside` second array would have left the primary shape still asserting "every row has a
result", and the team page's own axis domain (D-06, computed across the whole team-season including
scheduled matches) would have had to read from two places that could drift.

**Invariant test (Task 2 implements it, Task 3 asserts it):** a row carrying any one of
`actualWinner`/`actualRedScore`/`actualBlueScore` must carry all three; a row carrying none of them
is valid. This is the validation rule CONTEXT.md mandates as the replacement for the lost type-level
guarantee — the protection moves from the type system to a test, rather than disappearing.
</assumption_delta_decision>
