<objective>
Build the per-event match display: predictions sitting beside actuals, on one shared scale, with the
overlap between two alliance bands drawn rather than asserted.

Purpose: this is the phase's centre of gravity and the reason the pipeline work exists. The user's
own framing drove it — a match prediction should show not just the total probability of a win, but
separately, each alliance's predicted score plus or minus one standard deviation, because an
alliance of three unpredictable robots genuinely has a wider band than three consistent ones.

It also carries the phase's highest-risk item. Mobile horizontal scroll here is not a repeat of the
Teams table's single page-wide scroller — it recurs once per event section, two to six or more times
per page, and it only has to fail once to make the whole page pan sideways. That exact bug already
shipped and was caught at a real-device sign-off.

Output: an event section list where each section owns its own scroll region, its own axis header,
and the team's metrics as captured when that event ended.
</objective>
