<objective>
Take the tracer's one-event replay to the whole corpus: five seasons, walk-forward, with matches from
concurrent events correctly interleaved, and produce the report a human actually looks at.

Purpose: This is where Phase 1's goal becomes observable. Success criterion 3 requires OPR's Brier score
and winner accuracy on any 2022–2026 season with every prediction produced strictly before that match's
result is folded in; criterion 5 requires calibration curves per algorithm per season and headline
accuracy from holdout seasons only. Everything needed for both exists after Plans 03 through 05 — this
plan connects them and runs it for real.

The interleaving matters more than it looks. Replaying one event to completion before starting the next
would mean a team's rating during a Week 3 regional was computed from a concurrent regional that had not
finished yet in real time — a subtle, silent form of the leakage this phase exists to eliminate. The
chronological stream has to be built across events, not per event.

Output: A real report over 2022–2026, and a human confirmation that it reads as intended.
</objective>
