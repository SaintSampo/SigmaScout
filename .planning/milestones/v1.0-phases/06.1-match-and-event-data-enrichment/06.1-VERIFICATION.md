## Ruling on the Blocking Question (SC-1 wording vs. shipped behavior)

**Ruling: Interpretation (1).** SC-1's wording was an imprecise expression of "a dot must not sit
in `unknown` when the fact IS derivable." The shipped behavior — playoff rows structurally forced
to `unknown` because bonus RP does not exist as a concept in playoff play — satisfies SC-1's actual
intent. This is a documentation-correction finding, not a scope reduction, for four independent
reasons found in the codebase and project history, not merely asserted by the plan's own audit:

1. **The domain fact is independently verified in the codebase, not just claimed.** `ELIMINATION_RP_TOTAL`'s doc comment in `packages/core/algorithms/sigma1/rp/constants.ts` records a full-population measurement (not a sample): `red_rp_earned`/`blue_rp_earned` are 0 for 100% of played elimination matches in every season — 2022 0/2613, 2023 0/2795, 2024 0/2867, 2025 0/3056, 2026 0/3212. A dot resolving solid/hollow on a playoff row would assert a specific bonus was earned or missed for a ranking-point mechanic that structurally cannot exist there. That is not "the fact is unknown," it is "there is no fact" — a different, stronger case than the one SC-1's `unknown`-avoidance language was written to cover.
2. **The user's own UAT instruction directly contradicts the literal reading.** 06.1-UAT.md test 26: the user reported "on the teams page, playoff matches do not have RP. All playoff matches should have RP greyed out" — i.e., the user explicitly asked for the `unknown` (greyed) state on playoff rows, the exact opposite of literal SC-1's "no dot remains unknown for a played match."
3. **The predicted side already implemented the qualification-scoped interpretation from plan 06.1-02 onward**, and the *initial* verification (2026-08-27T01:08:22Z, before UAT) marked SC-1 VERIFIED using exactly that scoped reading — its evidence text says "Every bonus-RP dot on a played **qualification** match row... resolves to solid/hollow." The gap UAT later found was that the *actual* (published-data) side had not yet caught up to the same scoping the predicted side and the initial verification's own truth-statement already assumed — not that the qualification-only reading itself was wrong.
4. **The fix that resolved the user's complaint is the qualification-scoped reading, not a rollback.** Plan 06.1-08 did not walk back qualification-only dot resolution; it *completed* it symmetrically across predicted, actual, and client-render paths. Rendering playoff dots solid/hollow was the defect the user reported, not the fix.

**Recommended corrected wording** (see frontmatter `recommended_roadmap_correction`; verifier does
not edit ROADMAP.md):

> Each bonus-RP dot on a played qualification match row renders solid when that specific bonus was
> earned (actual) or is predicted to be earned, and hollow when it was not — no dot remains in the
> `unknown` state for a played qualification match in a season with registered RP rules. Bonus-RP
> dots on playoff match rows (compLevel ef/qf/sf/f) are excluded from this criterion: FRC does not
> award bonus ranking points in playoff play (measured 0/N across the full played-elimination
> population, every season 2022-2026), so those dots correctly render `unknown` (greyed out) rather
> than resolving to solid/hollow — resolving them would assert a fact that does not exist.

This is surfaced as a human decision per the orchestrator's instructions; it does not block `passed`
status because the shipped behavior is independently confirmed to satisfy SC-1's demonstrated intent
(point 3 above — the initial verification, written before this question was even raised, already
read SC-1 as qualification-scoped).
