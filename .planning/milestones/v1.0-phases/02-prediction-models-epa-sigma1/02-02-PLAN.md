Give every season 2022-2026 a data-driven component map that reconciles against the alliance's own `totalPoints`, and give both algorithms a documented path for the 1,517 matches TBA shipped without a score breakdown.

Purpose: FRC changes its game and TBA changes its field names every year — 2026 alone renames the foul fields, nests most scoring inside a `hubScore` object, and renames `autoPoints`/`teleopPoints` to `totalAutoPoints`/`totalTeleopPoints`. A component map that works for 2024 silently produces nothing for 2026 unless each season is written and reconciled separately (D-02).
Output: Four per-season component maps, the season dispatch table completed, and D-05's fallback update.
</objective>
