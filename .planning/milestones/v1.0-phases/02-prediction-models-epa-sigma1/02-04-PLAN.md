<objective>
Build Sigma1: a Kalman-filter-family rating where every team metric carries a mean and a variance derived from that team's own match history, and every match prediction carries the full predictive variance behind its win probability.

Purpose: this is the algorithm the project exists to test. Statbotics computes a per-alliance standard deviation and then does not use it — their `pred_sd` line sits commented out above their win-probability calculation, and their `EPARating` docstring says outright that it "does not handle covariance between variables." Two wildcard rookie alliances and two metronomic veteran alliances with the same predicted margin therefore receive identical confidence from Statbotics. Sigma1 exists to exploit that gap, and D-12's nested link function is the instrument that measures whether exploiting it pays.
Output: The Kalman core, the covariance and consistency estimators, the three link modes, and the assembled `AlgorithmModule<Sigma1State>`.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| parsed components -> Kalman state | A single non-finite component value entering a belief propagates through every subsequent update for that team and, via the alliance sum, to its teammates. |
| numeric degeneracy -> published uncertainty | A divide-by-zero or a negative variance surfaces to a user as a plausible-looking `X ± Y`; it does not throw on its own. |
