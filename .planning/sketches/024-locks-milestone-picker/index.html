What round one taught
  • Most points in time are not interesting. The ones that matter are event milestones, so the picker offers nine per event instead of every match.
  • Picking an event first, then a milestone inside it (023 B), was the right model. B's per-event lanes cost too much height.
  • Live is its own first-class choice, one tap from anywhere.
  • The cutoff chart from 023 D stays: it shows where the line moved, and tapping it jumps to the nearest milestone.
