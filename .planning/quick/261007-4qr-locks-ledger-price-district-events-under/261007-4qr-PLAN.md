## The short-roster rule (Part 1 design, decided by the planner; flagged for confirmation)

The orchestrator's example rule was "the draft runs in order until the pool is empty, and the remaining picks are filler". The corpus says real events never do that. Every 2023-plus district event under 24 qualification teams seats WHOLE real alliances at the top seeds and puts WHOLE demo alliances at the bottom seeds. The demo alliances forfeit. This plan implements that convention. It meets every hard constraint: eight alliances kept, no fabricated rating, filler in no output, selection and playoff points flowing to real teams exactly as today, and fewer teams than alliances still refused.

Measured 2026-10-07, read-only, from `data/corpus.sqlite`:

| Event | Real teams on qual rows (N) | Real alliances | Demo alliances | floor((N-1)/3) | floor(N/3) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026txmca | 18 | 1-5 | 6, 7, 8 | 5 | 6 |
| 2026mefal | 20 | 1-6 | 7, 8 | 6 | 6 |
| 2023gaalb | 21 | 1-6 | 7, 8 | 6 | 7 |
| 2025ncash | 22 | 1-7 | 8 | 7 | 7 |
| 2024vapor | 23 | 1-7 | 8 | 7 | 7 |

- `floor((N-1)/3)` matches 5 of 5. `floor(N/3)` matches 3 of 5, and both of its misses are the N divisible by 3 cases.
- No 2023-plus district event (event_type 1) ever has a demo robot on an alliance beside a real robot: 0 partial demo alliances.
- Fully demo alliances played 19 playoff sets against real alliances and won 0, mostly scoring 0 points (forfeits). One set was demo against demo.
- At exactly 24 teams, 2026isde2 seated eight real alliances and 2026txfor seated seven plus one demo alliance. N of 24 or more is OUTSIDE this rule: today's eight-real-alliance draft is kept unchanged, so every event that simulates today simulates identically.

The rule, as implemented:
1. `draftedAllianceCount(teamCount, allianceCount)` (new, exported) returns `allianceCount` when `teamCount >= allianceCount * DRAFTED_ALLIANCE_SIZE`, and `floor((teamCount - 1) / DRAFTED_ALLIANCE_SIZE)` otherwise. For eight alliances: 24+ gives 8, 23 gives 7, 22 gives 7, 21 gives 6, 20 gives 6, 18 gives 5, 8 gives 2.
2. A SIMULATED draft runs the existing progressive captain rule and the serpentine order over alliances 1 to k only, where k is the drafted count. Round one runs 1 to k, round two runs k down to 1. Alliances k+1 to 8 are FILLER alliances with no members. Real teams left over earn 0 selection points and land in `notSelectedDraws`. That is what the 2 to 3 unpicked real teams at these events actually earned.
3. A SUPPLIED alliance pick that is not on the roster but satisfies `isDemoTeamKey` is FILLER. It is skipped as a member, and every real member keeps its TBA slot (slot 0 captain, 1 first, 2 second, 3 backup). Any other absent key still raises `InvalidAllianceSetError`. An empty pick list is still an error.
4. In the eight-alliance bracket an alliance whose pricing roster is empty is a filler alliance. Against a real alliance it loses with NO randomness consumed. Filler against filler resolves to the lower alliance number with NO randomness consumed. That choice cannot reach any real team's points, because a real alliance beats whichever filler alliance reaches it. A played match still returns its real winner first, as today. A mixed alliance (real plus filler, never seen at a district event) is priced from its real members only. No filler is ever given a rating.
5. Refusals: `teamCount < allianceCount` still raises `InsufficientRosterError` (the orchestrator's captain floor). For a NON-eight alliance count (the divisioned DCMP parents) the old `teamCount < allianceCount * 3` refusal stands, because the measured fallback has no short-roster convention and those events are never short.
6. `fieldSize`, `qualPointsByRank`, award draws and award-only teams are untouched. Filler never enters `baselines`.

If the orchestrator or Jacob prefers the orchestrator's example rule (eight real captains, trailing filler picks), only steps 1, 2 and 4 change. The supplied-alliance and refusal halves stay as written.

## The pending cell (Part 2 design)

`DistrictLedgerCell`'s unavailable variant gains an optional `pending?: true`. It deliberately does NOT get a new kind. A pending cell IS "no distribution in hand" at that instant, so every computation that keys on `kind === "unavailable"` (statuses, chance runs, `foldCells`, excluded teams, `grandTotalIsDistrictOnly`) keeps its meaning with zero edits. While the flag can be set, every such computation is already held at Pending through `perEventRunSignature === null`. Only the printed word and the `data-cell` attribute differ. The flag is set only when the new `distributionsPending` option is true and the event is not in `unavailableEvents`. Each tab computes `distributionsPending` with the exact expression that already nulls `perEventRunSignature`, so the cells and the Status column cannot disagree.
