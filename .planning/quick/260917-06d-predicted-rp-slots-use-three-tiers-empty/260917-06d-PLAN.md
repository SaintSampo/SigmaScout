# 260917-06d: predicted bonus RP dots become three tiers

## Objective

Replace the partial-fill predicted bonus dot with a three tier read: **empty** when the bonus is
unlikely, **faint** when it is a toss-up, **solid** when it is likely. The odds themselves stay
available in the tooltip and the accessible label; only the drawn mark becomes categorical.

This is sketch 012 variant **B** (`full colour above two thirds, tint between, hollow under one
third`), which Jacob first picked before switching to C. C shipped; the fill turned out to be hard
to read at 14px. B is now the decision. The old sketch README records C as selected — leave that
historical record alone, it is dated.

**Purpose:** a dot that is legible at a glance at its real size, instead of a continuous fill whose
40% and 60% states look alike.

**Output:** a tier function with named cutoffs, a single span dot carrying `data-tier`, three
CSS looks per alliance with recorded contrast ratios, and every stale comment gone.

## Locked decisions (Jacob — do not revisit)

- **D-01 Cutoffs.** Unlikely is `p < 1/3` (empty). Toss-up is `1/3 <= p <= 2/3` (faint). Likely is
  `p > 2/3` (solid). The cutoffs are named exported constants in `lib/bonusRp.ts`, and a pure
  `bonusDotTier(probability)` returns `"unlikely" | "tossup" | "likely" | undefined` —
  `undefined` for an absent or non finite probability, which still renders the dashed `unknown`
  dot.
- **D-02 The percentage stays in the tooltip and aria-label.** `bonusDotLabel` keeps emitting
  `predicted N% likely` unchanged.

## Design direction (refined; the executor implements this as written)

| Tier | Border | Interior | Letter |
|------|--------|----------|--------|
| `unlikely` | alliance colour, solid 1px | none (transparent) | alliance colour — the same look as an actual `missed` dot |
| `tossup` | alliance colour, solid 1px | new 25% alliance tint | `--alliance-{side}-dot-ink` |
| `likely` | alliance colour, solid 1px | existing 60% `--alliance-{side}-fill` | `--alliance-{side}-dot-ink` |

`likely` deliberately reads stronger than an actual `earned` dot (which stays on the 30%
`--alliance-*-soft` tint with an alliance-colour letter). Actual dots are NOT changed by this task.

The 25% toss-up tint sits close to `earned`'s 30% soft tint. That is accepted: the two live in
different columns and carry different letter inks and different labels. Do not "fix" it by moving
`--alliance-*-soft`.
