# 260917-06d: predicted bonus RP dots become three tiers

Replaced the continuous bottom-up fill on predicted bonus dots with a three tier read: empty for
unlikely, faint for toss-up, solid for likely. This is sketch 012 variant B, brought back at Jacob's
request. The exact percentage stays in the tooltip and aria-label; only the drawn mark changed.

## What changed

- `bonusDotTier(probability)` in `lib/bonusRp.ts` replaces `bonusDotFillPx` and `BONUS_DOT_INNER_PX`.
  It returns `"unlikely" | "tossup" | "likely" | undefined`, with exported cutoffs
  `BONUS_DOT_TOSSUP_MIN` (1/3) and `BONUS_DOT_TOSSUP_MAX` (2/3). The middle band is inclusive at both
  ends. An absent or non-finite probability returns `undefined` and still draws the dashed `unknown` dot.
- `BonusRpDots.tsx` is back to a single span per dot, carrying `data-tier` and a
  `bonus-dot--tier-{tier}` class. The nested fill and letter spans and `data-fill-px` are gone.
- `theme.css` gained `--alliance-{red,blue}-fill-faint` (25% tint) and the tier rules. Measured figures
  recorded in the token comment: letter ink on the faint tint 10.84:1 red, 10.27:1 blue; empty vs
  toss-up ground 1.49:1 red, 1.43:1 blue; toss-up vs likely ground 1.83:1 red, 1.78:1 blue. The
  measuring script first reproduced the two figures already on record for the 60% fill.
- A new test asserts `theme.css` holds a selector for every tier and side the component can emit,
  because jsdom applies no CSS and a renamed class would otherwise ship an unstyled dot.
- `MatchTable.test.tsx` and `EventMatchTable.test.tsx` assert `data-tier` in place of `data-fill-px`.
- `scripts/measureRpCalibration.ts` and `docs/models/rp-bonus-gap-attribution.md` prose describe the
  live three tier rule. The frozen attribution record's `dotThreshold: 0.5` literal is untouched.
- Orchestrator follow-up: stripped the plan-local decision ids (`D-01`, `D-02`) the executor left in
  three source comments, since they point at nothing a later reader can find.
