# Champ cutoff: simulate the DCMP award slots — plan

The Champ Locks tab predicts the Championship points line about 20 points low. On
2026 FNC it showed about 170 all season against a real line of 231. The points
projection was right: the 15th best final total was 172. The whole miss is award
slots. The DCMP winning alliance and its Impact, Engineering Inspiration and
Rookie All Star winners take Championship slots, and about 3 of those go to
teams outside the points top. This plan makes the predicted cutoff a SIMULATED
line that takes those slots out run by run. It fixes the two rewind defects the
new line needs, measures the result walk-forward, and ships only if the
measurement clears a bar set in advance.

**Locked decisions (Jacob), cited by id below:**
- **L1**: in the champ advancement run, PER RUN, decide the DCMP winning alliance
  and draw the DCMP Impact, Engineering Inspiration and Rookie All Star winners.
  Remove those teams and their slots from the pool, then read the line as the
  `pointsSlots`th highest drawn total of the remaining pool. Awards and winners
  already posted at the position are facts and stay deterministic.
- **L2**: until the DCMP awards post, the predicted cutoff is the simulated line
  (the median of `cutoffByRun`) with its 10 to 90 likely range. In range and Out
  of range cut at the same place, so the between property holds, and ONE
  derivation feeds both the chip and the line.
- **L3**: the locks.ts guarantee statuses (`locked`, `lockedAward`, `eliminated`)
  never use predicted awards. Only the cutoff, the likely range and the chance
  may.
- **L4**: fold in finding 3 (rewinds read "district only" and the champ run is
  suppressed) and finding 4 (rewound headers print today's earned total).
- **L5**: backtest walk-forward before shipping, with predict-before-update. If
  the backtest does not beat the naive line, STOP and report.

**Untouched, by instruction:** `packages/core/districts/locks.ts`,
`reservedSlots.ts`, `pooledLockInputs.ts`, `qualification.ts`, `pointModel.ts`,
`measureLedgerTenets.ts`, and every Locked / Locked out verdict on both tabs.

## The model, stated once

**A. The DCMP estimate (`packages/core/districts/hypotheticalDcmp.ts`).**
- Observations come from every past DCMP on disk. Each attendee (a team with at
  least one dcmp tier `eventPoints` entry) gives a normalized field rank `q`, its
  DCMP total (the sum of its dcmp tier `eventPoints` totals), and whether it won
  (an award of type 1 at a dcmp tier key). `q` is taken on its district total,
  which is `pointTotal` minus the DCMP totals.
- `normalizedFieldRanks` is ONE function, used for training (attendees at field
  chance 1) and at application. For team i:
  `q = (sum of fieldChance of other teams with a strictly higher district
  projection, plus half the fieldChance of other teams tied with it, plus 0.5)`
  divided by `max(sum of every team's fieldChance, 1)`, clamped into (0, 1).
- There are `HYPOTHETICAL_DCMP_BUCKETS = 10` equal `q` buckets. For a target
  season S, `hypotheticalDcmpTable(S)` accumulates ONLY seasons strictly before
  S. Each bucket has a DCMP total pmf (index is the point value, denominator the
  count) and `winChance = wins / observations`. The table is `undefined` when no
  prior season exists or when any bucket has fewer than
  `HYPOTHETICAL_DCMP_MIN_BUCKET_OBSERVATIONS = 30` observations. That covers 2016
  only, which keeps the "district only" fallback.
- `dcmpAwardCounts(S, districtKey, cmpSlots)` returns the per type ANCHOR
  counts. The district code is `districtKey` without its 4 digit year. The
  source is the most recent season before S with that district code. Otherwise,
  over every district entry from seasons before S, take the 5 nearest by
  `|cmpSlots - entry.cmpSlots|` (ties to the more recent season) and use each
  type's median, rounded half up. `source` names which one applied.
- `dcmpAwardCountDistribution(S, districtKey, cmpSlots, countMode)` returns, per
  type, a weights array indexed by count.
  - `fixed` is a point mass at the anchor.
  - `drawn` is the anchor plus a change drawn from the empirical distribution of
    that type's season over season count changes. The changes come from every
    pair of consecutive available seasons of one district code, where BOTH
    seasons are before S. The result is clamped at 0, and the mass there is
    folded together.
  - `drawn` with no pair before S falls back to `fixed`.
- `hypotheticalDcmpPart(table, q, spreadScale = 1)` widens the bucket's
  observations about the bucket median before building the pmf. Each value `v`
  becomes `round(median + spreadScale x (v - median))`, clamped to
  `[0, the season's dcmp total ceiling]`. `winChance` is unchanged, and the
  coupling reads the widened pmf. `spreadScale` 1 is the unwidened table.

**B. Where the estimate prices the DCMP row (`champLedgerRows.ts`
`buildDcmpRow`).** In priority order:
1. membership `out`: `notInField`, unchanged.
2. The field is a fact for this team (`dcmpStarted`, or `atLivePosition` with
   membership `in`) and the event priced row exists: the event row, unchanged.
   Its `winChance` is the mass of its Playoffs cell at
   `maxEventPoints(season, "dcmp").elim`, over the denominator. It is 0 when that
   cell is final, because a posted winner is already a fact in the statuses.
3. Otherwise, when an estimate is supplied for the team: an estimated row. The
   four category cells are `notYetPriced`. The Subtotal is an open cell over the
   estimate pmf. The row gets `estimated: true`, and `sources` stays the dcmp
   pass's own sources, so `champLedgerStatus.ts` floors and ceilings are
   unchanged.
4. Otherwise `notYetPriced`, unchanged: the district only fallback.

On a rewound position before the DCMP starts, case 2 cannot fire, because
`atLivePosition` is false and `dcmpStarted` is false. So every team is estimated,
attendees included, and the real roster never prices anything. That is the no
leak guarantee for finding 3. `hasOpenCategory` must also count an OPEN DCMP
SUBTOTAL, or a chance 1 team with every district category final would fold as
final.

**C. The per run draw (`advancementChance.ts`, champ mode).**
- **Legacy mode:** no team carries a `dcmp` part and `awardDraws` is absent or
  empty. The existing code path runs byte for byte. The District Locks tab lives
  here.
- **Champ mode** adds three things. `AdvancementChanceTeam.dcmp?: { counts;
  denominator; fieldChance; winChance }`. When it is present, `counts` is the
  team's DISTRICT part: the district subtotal convolved with its rookie bonus and
  adjustments. `AdvancementChanceInputs.awardDraws?: readonly { awardType;
  countWeights: readonly number[]; candidates: { teamKey; weight }[];
  pendingEvents: { eventKey; entrants: { teamKey; weight }[] }[] }[]` holds the
  draw specs. `countWeights` is indexed by count; a fixed count is one hot. The result gains two
  optional diagnostics, `awardSlotsByRun` and `outsideAwardSlotsByRun`, as
  Int32Array of length `draws`.
- Per run, in this fixed RNG order:
  - For each static pool team in input order: one uniform for its district
    value. If it has a dcmp part, always two more: membership `m = u1 <
    fieldChance`, then `u2` for the DCMP value `F^-1(u2)`. It won
    `m && winChance > 0 && u2 >= 1 - winChance`. Its total is district plus DCMP
    (DCMP is 0 when not a member).
  - Then for each award draw in order: one uniform draws this run's count `k`
    from `countWeights`. It is always consumed, even for a point mass. Then one
    uniform per pending event, which picks one entrant by weight (none when the
    entrants' total weight is 0) and adds that entrant to this run's candidates.
    Candidates dedupe, keeping the larger weight. Then exactly
    `countWeights.length - 1` uniforms. The first `k` of them pick up to `k`
    distinct candidates without replacement, proportional to weight, and the
    rest are consumed unused. Zero weight never picks.
- `W` is the drawn winners plus the drawn award winners, as a set. Then
  `pointsSlots_r = max(slots - |ranked teams in (posted awardQualified union
  W)|, 0)`: exactly `pointsRaceSlots` arithmetic, where "ranked" means present
  in `inputs.teams`. `lockSlots_r = max(pointsSlots_r - reservedSlots, 0)`.
  `pool_r` is the static pool minus `W`. The line is the `pointsSlots_r`th
  highest total in `pool_r`, taken in the existing top down histogram walk.
- **Chance:** team i is inside in run r when i is in `W`, or when i is in
  `pool_r` and its at or above count is at most `lockSlots_r`. Ties at the last
  slot are out, as today.
- If ANY run has no line (`pointsSlots_r` is 0, or `pool_r` is smaller than it),
  `cutoffByRun` is omitted, and the tab falls back to the midpoint rule.
- `outsideAwardSlotsByRun[r]` counts members of `W` in the static pool whose
  drawn total ranks below the static `pointsSlots` inside the WHOLE static pool
  before removal. This is the measured "outside" quantity (3.0 per district).
- Candidates, entrants and `countWeights` are validated: finite, non negative
  weights; `countWeights` non empty with a positive sum; `fieldChance` and
  `winChance` in [0, 1]. Anything else throws `AdvancementChanceInputError`.

**D. Judged award draw specs (`champLedgerChances.ts`
`buildChampAwardDraws`).**
- None at all once the DCMP awards stage is final at the position. Posted awards
  are facts already in `statuses.awardQualified`.
- Otherwise, for Impact (0), EI (9) and RAS (10):
  - `countWeights` come from `dcmpAwardCountDistribution`, in the count mode of
    `champCutoffTuning(season)`.
  - `candidates` are teams holding a `qualifyingAwards` entry of that type at a
    DISTRICT tier event whose award stage is final at the position.
  - `pendingEvents` are the district tier events whose award stage is not final,
    each with entrants = the teams listing it in `eventPoints` or
    `remainingEvents`. District event rosters are public before the event, so
    this is not a leak.
- Weights follow the season's tuned weighting (`champCutoffTuning(season).weighting`,
  either `"uniform"` or `"decoration"`):
  - Eligibility first: RAS is rookies only, EI is veterans only, Impact is
    anyone. A team with no `awardProfile` is ineligible for RAS and weight 1
    otherwise.
  - `"decoration"` weights by the base rate cell the award cell already prices
    from: `awardBaseRate(season, bucket, rookieState)`, read at
    `awardPointsBucketIndex(10)` for Impact and `awardPointsBucketIndex(8)` for
    EI and RAS. Use the mapping `districtLedgerRows.ts` already has
    (`awardProfileFor`, export it); do not add a second lookup. A season with no
    table falls back to weight 1.
- A dcmp tier award is NEVER a candidate. A unit test pins that.

**E. One derivation for the line and the chips (L2).**
- `simulatedChampLine(cutoffByRun, draws)` lives in `predictedCutoff.ts`. It
  runs the run cutoffs through the shipped `pointPercentiles`, the same
  histogram convention `simulatedCutoffRange` uses, and returns
  `{ points: Math.round(p50), likely: { p10, p90 } }` or `undefined`.
- **ONE state, `champRangeState(...)`** (a pure function in
  `champLedgerChances.ts`), is read by BOTH the chips and the cutoff view. It has
  exactly four arms:
  - `settled`: the DCMP awards stage is final at the position, or `cmpSlots` is
    null. Nothing is drawn any more, so the shipped midpoint rule and rank chips
    (or `capacityUnknown`) stand unchanged, with `source: "boundary"`. They are
    deterministic here and cannot flip later.
  - `pending`: not settled, and something upstream of the line is still
    computing. That covers the per event run (`runSignature` null), the district
    chance run (running, or expected and not yet complete), estimates awaiting
    field chances (`hypotheticalDcmpEstimates` reports `awaitingFieldChances`),
    and the champ run itself (running, or idle while a run was built).
  - `simulated`: the champ run is complete, excluded no team, and returned
    `cutoffByRun`. It carries `simulatedChampLine`'s `points` and `likely`.
  - `noCall`: not settled, and the pipeline reached a TERMINAL refusal. The
    reason is carried and named: no history table for the season (2016), an
    `in` team whose DCMP could not be priced, a refusal by the champ run
    builder, a champ run that excluded any team, `cutoffByRun` absent, or a
    Worker error in the district or champ run.
- **Chips** (`applyChampRangeState(statuses, rows.teams, state)` in
  `champLedgerStatus.ts`). It touches ONLY teams whose verdict is `contending`
  (today's `inRange`/`outOfRange`). Locked, Locked · award, Locked · winner,
  Locked out and prequalified come straight from the verdicts and render
  immediately in every arm.
  - `simulated`: In range iff `projection >= points`, else Out of range.
  - `pending`: every contending team gets `rangeCall: "pending"`. It renders one
    neutral placeholder chip, with the same size and no colour of either chip,
    so nothing moves when it settles. Copy is per the sketch skill; it must not
    say In range or Out of range.
  - `noCall`: every contending team gets `rangeCall: "noCall"`. It renders a
    neutral "No call" chip, and the chip's accessible description and the stat
    line's small text name the reason.
  - `settled`: the shipped rank rule.
  - In `pending` and `noCall`, the In range and Out of range filter chips print
    an em dash for their count. A `pending` or `noCall` team stays visible under
    every filter, as `capacityUnknown` does today, and no chance line prints
    under it. The rank rule ordering is NEVER exposed while the state is
    `pending` or `noCall`.
  - It leaves verdicts, `verdictCensus`, `floorCutLine`, `awardQualified`,
    `prequalified`, `reservedSlots` and `pointsSlots` untouched.
- **Cutoff view.**
  - `simulated`: prints the SAME `points` as a `predicted` arm with
    `source: "simulated"`. Its boundary is the lowest In range median and the
    highest Out of range median, with the line itself standing in for an empty
    side. The likely range comes from the same call. So the between property
    holds by construction.
  - `pending`: a new `pending` arm. No figure (a neutral placeholder), no likely
    range, no dashed rule.
  - `noCall`: a new `unavailable` arm carrying the reason. "not available", no
    dashed rule.
  - `settled`: the shipped arms with `source: "boundary"`.
  - The "(district only)" label survives only on the grand total cells. The stat
    line never prints a district only number in a non settled state, because
    that would be the rank rule fallback Jacob ruled out.
- **Why `noCall` rather than a rank rule fallback:** a fallback that later gives
  way to the simulated line is the flip Jacob ruled out. A terminal refusal is
