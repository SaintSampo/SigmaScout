Note on tune.ts's acceptance rule, because it explains several lines below that
otherwise look like regressions: better() has an ACC_NOISE deadband of 0.05pp.

accept a move that lowers accuracy by up to 0.05pp if log loss improves. Every
"-> acc=" line below that appears to go down is such a move.
