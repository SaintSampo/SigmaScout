  L0 -> L1   +0.215pp

THE FOUR COMPONENTS DO NOT SUM TO THEIR OWN BUNDLE:

  foul       +0.073pp
  elim       +0.084pp
  defensive  +0.155pp
  Huber      +0.037pp
  ------------------
  sum        +0.349pp   of individually-measured, individually-re-tuned credits
  bundle     +0.205pp   measured directly as L1 -> L3
  ------------------
  INTERACTION  -0.144pp

  The interaction is NEGATIVE and it is large - 41% of the summed credits
  disappear when the four components are combined. Stated plainly rather than
  smoothed: THE FOUR COMPONENTS ARE PARTIAL SUBSTITUTES FOR ONE ANOTHER, NOT
  COMPLEMENTS. Each one, given the 13 shared knobs free to re-fit around it,
  captures some of the same residual structure the others capture. Adding all
  four buys materially less than adding any one of them would suggest.

  This is the honest opposite of the convenient result. A tidy table would have
  had the four credits summing to the bundle and each component owning a clean
  slice of the residual. They do not, and the arithmetic is reported as it fell.

WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE INDIVIDUAL FAILURES:

  Each of the four components fails promotion on its own (C2.txt). But the
  BUNDLE passes promotion against L1:

    L3 vs L1: dAcc +0.205pp, 95% [0.059, 0.353] excludes zero;
              dBrier -0.00067, 95% [-0.00093, -0.00039] excludes zero;
              +0.205pp >= the 0.169pp bar.
    ALL THREE PROMOTION CONDITIONS PASS.

  So the residual is real and it is measurable, but it is NOT ATTRIBUTABLE to
  any single one of these four components. That is precisely the "unidentifiable
  model" shape this project's failure log already records once, and it is what
  F-23's upper-bound caveat was protecting against in the other direction.

  The four components jointly hold ~0.205pp that none of them individually can
  claim. A ladder built one component at a time cannot recover it, because the
  effect only exists in combination.
