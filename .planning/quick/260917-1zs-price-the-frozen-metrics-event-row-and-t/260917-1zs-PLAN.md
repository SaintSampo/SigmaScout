<objective>
Price, offline and against the real corpus, the shape that would let the live Worker stop writing
team-season artifacts: an event file that carries every match (played and upcoming) with the frozen
prediction AND the frozen six-team metrics as of that match, plus a small per-robot index.

Purpose: the CPU work (`rp-fold-exceeds-worker-cpu-budget.md`, "RE-MEASURED AFTER F2") established
that the twelve team-season artifacts are 7.6 ms of a 17.5 ms tick, and that removing them takes the
tick to 9.9 ms. Option (a) — a published-shape change — is the only remaining structural lever. This
task decides whether that work starts and in what shape. It publishes nothing, uploads nothing, and
changes no shipped behavior.

Output: a committed pre-registered bar and field audit, an offline pricing instrument, and a findings
section carrying per-event byte tables, a storage extrapolation, an end-to-end robot-page price, an
honest list of what gets worse, and a go/no-go verdict.

Tracer note: the pre-registration gate (Task 1) deliberately precedes the instrument, because a bar
committed after its own numbers is not a bar. Task 2 is the vertical slice — corpus to publisher to
sink to proposed encoder to brotli to report — and its own first runnable milestone is ONE event end
to end before it widens to whole seasons.
</objective>
