<objective>
Separate the published Match Band from the internal win-odds variance, correct the SPR band for the even-split shrinkage, remove Swing Score from the website entirely, and rewrite the Sigma methodology page copy and figures so they describe the corrected model with measured numbers.

This implements exactly Jacob's four requests: (1) apply the correction, (2) separate the display band from the win odds, (3) fix the page copy, (4) remove Swing Score from the website, with no band or Swing Score for OPR and EPA, because Sigma Score is what matters now and it is for SPR only. Locked decisions D1 through D5 in the planning context govern every task below.

Purpose: SPR's published band today covers 47.1% of alliance results against a 68.3% target, because a robot's Sigma is the 1 standard deviation of its even-split SHARE of the miss, and summing three shares' variances yields Var(alliance) divided by roster size. Multiplying by roster size restores about 71.7% coverage. The same variance also feeds the rank simulation's win/tie spread, where widening it measurably worsens Brier (0.1559 to 0.1631), so the two quantities must split: win odds keep today's number, display gets the corrected one.

Output: one helper in sigmaScore.ts, the layer and Worker producing a Sigma-only display band under renamed wire keys, no swingFactor anywhere on the wire, the web reading only the new keys with no Swing text, a committed coverage measurement script, and corrected Sigma page copy and figures.
