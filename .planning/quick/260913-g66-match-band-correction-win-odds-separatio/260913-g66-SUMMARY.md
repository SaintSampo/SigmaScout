# Quick task 260913-g66 — Match Band correction, win-odds separation, page copy, Swing Score removal

Jacob asked for four things: (1) apply the correction, (2) separate the display band from the win
odds, (3) fix the page copy, (4) remove Swing Score from the website — no band and no Swing Score for
OPR and EPA; Sigma Score is SPR only.

**Nothing was published, deployed or pushed.** A republish, a Worker deploy and a web ship are owed
(see "Deployment owed" below). No D1 reseed is needed: the state shape is unchanged.

## Why

Measured 2026-09-12 (scratch replay) and re-measured by the committed script below: SPR's Match Band
held **47.1%** of actual alliance scores against a 68.3% one-standard-deviation target. Sigma Score
is the 1σ of a robot's EVEN-SPLIT SHARE of its alliance's miss, so quadrature-summing three shares
gives Var(alliance)/rosterSize — the band was sqrt(rosterSize) too narrow. It is the same shrinkage
`empiricalMoments.ts` already undoes for RP variables. OPR and EPA's Swing-derived bands meanwhile
held 82.8% / 76.0%, so one bar meant three different things.

The band variance also fed the rank simulation's win/tie/loss spread (`matchOutcomeDistribution`).
Widening it there worsened SPR's implied win-probability Brier 0.1559 -> 0.1631 (red and blue misses
correlate, +0.21), hence the separation.
