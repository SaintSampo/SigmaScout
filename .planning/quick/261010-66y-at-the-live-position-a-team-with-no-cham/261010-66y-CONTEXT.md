### The gap (confirmed in code, 2026-10-10)

- `champFieldMembership` (`apps/web/src/components/districts/champLedgerRows.ts`): once the championship has started, a team with a dcmp tier row is `in` and a team WITHOUT one is `out`. `champLedgerStatus.ts` (~540 and ~583) gives an `out` team NO championship ceiling at all.
- "Started" for a team with no row is `dcmpStartedForTeam`: every FIELD FIXING dcmp key ON THE ARTIFACT has started. Started is the state block's own word (`deriveStageFromState(...).started`), which moves with the first match.
- The artifact learns a dcmp key ONLY from team rows (`dcmpEventKeysFor`: `eventPoints` and `remainingEvents`). There is no event list on the artifact. `remainingEvents` is written only by the offline publisher; the Worker's live pass adds `eventPoints` rows as TBA posts them and never adds a `remainingEvents` row. Championship registrations open after the last district event, so unless a district publish runs in that gap the live artifact holds NO dcmp row when a championship starts.
- So at a live championship played in divisions (FIM, NE, ONT, TX), when TBA posts the first division's rows before another division's, the artifact knows one division key, it has started, and EVERY team of the other divisions reads `out`: their whole championship ceiling (249 points in 2026) disappears, a top team of the posted division can read Locked, and the lock is taken back when the other divisions' rows land. The same happens at any championship where the artifact knows only PART of the registration (a publish that ran mid registration): the registered rows start, and the attending teams not yet listed read `out` for the minutes until TBA posts their points.
- Today a lone division key also classifies as a SINGLE championship (`championshipShape`, `packages/core/districts/finalsBracket.ts`: one stem, one member), so the single event joint proof could run on one division as if it were the whole championship.
- Two California style championships have the same exposure: one posted, the other not yet seen.

History views are not affected: a rewound position reads the timeline with the season's final rows. This is a LIVE gap only, and no finished season shows it at Now.

Scope: browser (`apps/web/src/components/districts`), `packages/core/districts`, and the shared verdict pass (`packages/harness/districtRankingsMerge.ts`) only where D5 finds the same gap. No artifact schema change, no algorithm version change, no D1 migration. If the verdict pass changes, the Worker bundle changes and the orchestrator deploys it with the pending deploy.

## Implementation Decisions

### D1. At the LIVE position, a team with no dcmp row reads `out` only when the field is PROVEN complete
All three must hold, read off the artifact at Now:
- **(a) started:** every field fixing dcmp key has started (today's rule, unchanged).
- **(b) posted:** every field fixing key carries a POSTED row, meaning some team has an `eventPoints` row at it. A `remainingEvents` row is a registration, not a posting.
- **(c) complete**, any one of:
  - (c1) `dcmpSlots` is not null and the number of teams with a posted row at a field fixing key is at least `dcmpSlots` minus half (rounded down) of the smallest posted field fixing key's team count. A missing division or a missing second championship hides a whole event's worth of teams, far more than the tolerance; an under filled field (2022 NE 78 of 80, 2026 ONT 99 of 100, 2026 ISR 38 of 42, 2022 ONT 67 of 80) still passes.
  - (c2) a divisioned championship's finals (parent) key carries a posted row (the finals are played only after every division is done).
  - (c3) every field fixing key reads Awards final under the live reading of quick task 261009-vp9.
- When the field is NOT proven, a team with no row reads exactly as it does before the championship starts (`open`: its bubble chance and one hypothetical championship ceiling). It is never `out`.
- A team WITH its own row is unchanged.
- A REWOUND position is unchanged (the timeline rule with final rows).
- Everything that follows membership follows this: the lock math, the joint proof's reading of a team with no row (trace every reader: `dcmpStartedForTeam`, `fieldFixingDcmpKeys`, the proof inputs in `champLedgerStatus.ts`, the reservation readers, `champTeamHiddenAtDcmp`, the status counts, the field chance). A team is not hidden from the table as "not in the field" while the field is not proven.
- Stated assumption (goes in the module header, no todo): TBA posts one event's points rows for all of its ranked teams together.

### D2. A lone division key is not a single championship
- `championshipShape`: a stem whose only member is a digit suffixed key (a division without its siblings or its parent) is `unsupported`, so the single event proof never runs on one division. No published district has such a key set (checked 2026-10-10 over the 109 local district artifacts: every digit suffixed dcmp key sits beside its parent), so no sweep line may move.

### D3. The divisioned joint proof runs before the finals key is on the artifact (follow up 10, reading R13 of 261009-kt3)
- Today `divisions without their finals event` is `unsupported`, and TBA writes the first finals row only when the finals are played or its awards are given. At a LIVE divisioned championship the joint proof therefore cannot run during the division playoffs at all.
- New: when the division keys are 2 or 4, the parent key is absent, and the field is proven complete by D1 (c1), the shape is divisioned with the finals read as not started and wholly open (the finals key is the stem; no row, no facts, every finals category open).
- **Equivalence gate (binding):** for every divisioned championship of the sweep (16 championships, 2023 to 2026) and every stop BEFORE the finals have a played row, the bound, the locked set and the reasons with the parent key's rows REMOVED from the artifact equal those with them present. If the equivalence does not hold everywhere, or the wiring is not small and clean, DROP D3, keep the refusal, and record it as a stated limit in the SUMMARY. Never fit.

### D4. A backup robot seen on the field joins its alliance (follow up 8)
- `playedBracketMatchesFor` tolerates a team on a played playoff side that is in no pick list. The joint proof reads a backup's playoff points only through a LISTED pick, and gives seats only on ALIVE alliances, so a backup that has already played for an alliance now decided, and that TBA has not (yet) listed, is read as an unpicked rival with no playoff points from that alliance. The corpus shows every backup listed after the fact (35 of 35), which says nothing about when TBA lists one live.
- New: in the dcmp bracket facts handed to the joint proof (`dcmpBracketFactsFor` / `dcmpBracketFactsAtPosition` and the facts' consumers), a team that appears on a side of a played playoff row at or before the position and is in no pick list is treated as a listed pick of that side's alliance. Two such teams on one alliance, or an alliance that would exceed `MAX_WINNING_ALLIANCE_SIZE`, refuses the facts (the proof does not run; the shipped test stands).
- Only rows at or before the position are read, so a rewound stop learns nothing from the future. The guards of 261009-kt3 and 261009-tx9 for a listed, unconfirmed fourth apply to it unchanged.
- Gate: the four sweeps do not move (every backup in the corpus is already listed). A unit test strips a real listed backup from the picks (2026pncmp frc7034) and shows the facts with the played rows equal the listed facts at every stop after its first played row.
- The District Locks tab needs no change: an unpicked team's Playoffs stay wholly open there until the playoffs are final. Confirm that in the SUMMARY with the line of code.
