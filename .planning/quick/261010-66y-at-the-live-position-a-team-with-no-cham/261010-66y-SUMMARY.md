## The rule, in six lines

1. The field fixing keys are every dcmp key except a divisioned championship's finals key.
2. The field is proven when every field fixing key has started, every one carries a posted `eventPoints` row, and the field is complete.
3. Complete means, in order: by capacity (`dcmpSlots` published and the teams with a posted row at a field fixing key number at least `dcmpSlots` minus half, rounded down, of the LARGEST posted key), by a posted row at the finals key, or, only in a season that is over, by Awards final at every field fixing key.
4. At a rewound position nothing changes. At the live position the one flag is false exactly while some field fixing key has started and the field is not proven.
5. While it is false a team with no row reads `open` with one whole hypothetical championship and a finals on top, the joint proof refuses `fieldNotProven`, more whole championships are held back, a division team's finals Playoffs ceiling is the whole dcmp Playoffs ceiling, the District Locks overlay does not apply, and the championship's awards do not read final.
6. A team with its own row is unchanged.
