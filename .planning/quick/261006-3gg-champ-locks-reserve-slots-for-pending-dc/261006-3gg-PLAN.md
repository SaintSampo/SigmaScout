# Quick task 261006-3gg: Champ Locks reserve slots for pending DCMP consuming awards

## Why

Jacob, 2026-10-06: "it is critical that at any point a team is only locked if it is
impossible for them to not qualify." The champ tier passed `reservedSlots = 0`
(`champLedgerStatus.ts` decision 2, `districtRankingsMerge.ts` champ pass), so a
pre-awards `Locked` at a DCMP was one slot per pending consuming award too generous.
FNC 2026 at the "playoffs done, awards open" stop: frc7890 `Locked` with threatCount
10 against 11 slots; four of the five judged consuming awards (2 EI, 2 RAS) then went
to teams whose ceiling sat below 7890's floor, cutting slots to 7 without removing a
threat. 7890 survived only by winning a judged award itself.

A second leapfrog the investigation surfaced: while DCMP playoffs are open, the
winning alliance (up to 4 teams) consumes slots regardless of points. A 4th pick with
a ceiling below a `Locked` team's floor can still win and take a slot.

## Rule

`reservedChampSlots` (new, `packages/core/districts/champReservedSlots.ts`):

    winnerSlots = elimFinal || awardFinal ? 0 : MAX_WINNING_ALLIANCE_SIZE (4)
    awardSlots  = awardFinal ? 0 : impactCeiling + eiCeiling + rasCeiling
    reserved    = neverHappening ? 0 : winnerSlots + awardSlots

Award count ceilings per type = max(the district's own earlier seasons, the five
nearest districts by cmpSlots over all earlier seasons) — `dcmpAwardCountCeilings` in
`hypotheticalDcmp.ts`, beside the anchor the cutoff model uses. A ceiling, never the
anchor: the anchor is a prediction and this is a guarantee.

`awardFinal` implies the event is over, so it also zeroes the winner reservation (2020
DCMPs posted Chairman's with no matches). `neverHappening` = every district-tier event
finished at now AND no DCMP row carries state or a schedule AND the artifact's year is
before the calendar year the caller passes (closes the gap between the last district
event and the DCMP registration list, which the district rule's own carve out leaves
open).

The reservation feeds `lockSlots` alone, exactly as the district tier: `"eliminated"`,
the published cut line, the In range rank rule and the chance run's slot count stay on
the unreserved count. The chance run draws the consuming awards itself per run, so a
worst-case reservation there would double count; it passes 0 with that reason.
