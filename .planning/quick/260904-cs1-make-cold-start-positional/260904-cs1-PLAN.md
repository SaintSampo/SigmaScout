<objective>
Make the cold start positional: `index === 0` of the replay range is the cold start, by
construction, instead of a season that must equal a module constant (D-1).

Purpose: `COLD_START_SEASON = 2022` went stale silently the moment `extend-corpus-2019-2020`
moved the corpus start to 2019, and the consequence is measured — a `[2019, 2020, 2022]` tuning
replay marks **2022** cold, discarding the state built from two prior seasons. The number being
wrong is the symptom; a constant that must agree with whatever range a caller happens to pass,
with nothing enforcing the agreement, is the defect. Correcting 2022 to 2019 would leave the
identical trap for the next corpus change.

Output: a positional `seasonBoundaryFor`, a test that PINS D-2's equivalence, the deletion of the
stale constant and its last reader, and a recorded note that the ten re-tune verdicts survive as
internally consistent but become non-comparable.

**Explicitly out of scope, and not to be run by any task here:** a tuning search, a promote, a
publish, a harness run, or any other re-measurement. A harness run and a republish are in flight
under the orchestrator's control. This task changes code, tests and notes only.

**Also out of scope:** `apps/web/src/routes/__fixtures__/` — do not touch anything under it.

## Why this is safe to land BEFORE the re-tune (D-2, and the generalized form of it)

CONTEXT.md verified four ranges by direct evaluation. The reason the two that matter are
unchanged generalizes to every caller in the repo, and the generalization is what makes this
low-risk:

`isColdStart` only *matters* when there is state to carry, and **at index 0 there never is.**
Under the current constant, a range whose first season is not 2022 takes the `else` branch at
index 0, iterates an empty `liveStates` map, and produces an **empty** carried map. `replay.ts:143`
is `initialStates?.get(algorithm.id) ?? algorithm.initState([...teams])` — an empty map and
`undefined` resolve identically. So positional and constant differ at index 0 only in a
`console.log` line; predictions are byte-identical. (`carryStatus` at `cli.ts:582` /
`publish.ts:1672` is `initialStates?.has(id)`, false either way.)

That is why the callers audited below all pass unchanged — but it is an argument, and D-2 says the
equivalence must be **asserted**, not assumed. Task 1 asserts it.
