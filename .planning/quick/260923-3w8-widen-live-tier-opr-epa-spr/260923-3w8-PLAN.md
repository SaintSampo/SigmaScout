# Widen the live folding tier to opr, epa, spr

Jacob's decision, 2026-09-23, on `260923-1tu-FINDINGS.md` item C6 and section 5 step 3. The tier was
held at `spr` first by the free plan's 50-subrequest cap (deleted by 260923-3w4) and then by its 10 ms
CPU cap; the account has been on Workers Paid since 2026-09-22 (30 s CPU, 10,000 subrequests per
invocation) and neither binds. What remains is the published-numbers consequence, which the two
major version bumps pay for.

## Verified, not changed (reported in the summary)

`packages/harness/publish.ts` emits `seed-{id}.sql` per resolved published algorithm at the module's
own version and passes exactly that id set to `emitCursorSeedSql`'s baseline markers;
`scripts/rebaseline.ts` prints every `id@version` from the live manifest at verify and records

orphans the moment the manifest names the new versions; `scripts/publishProbeStubs.ts` already loops
every manifest algorithm.
