### The pay rule (D1)

1. **TBA pays by wins.** Read from TBA's public source (`district_helper.py`: `_calc_elim_match_points_2023` and `_calc_elim_match_points`; `district_point_values.py`) and then fitted against every paid value. It is TBA's rule, not this code's.
   - Only a decisive semifinal or Finals row counts. A tie, a loss and an unplayed row count for nobody.
   - A decisive row is credited to the alliance that won it and to the three robots on the field for the winner.
   - Winning a semifinal set adds table points to the alliance. Eight alliance bracket: 13 for sf7 and sf8, 7 for sf9, sf10, sf11 and sf13, 6 for sf12, 0 for sf1 to sf6. Four alliance finals of a divisioned championship: 10 for sf3 and for sf5, 0 for the others.
   - SINCE 2024: a robot is paid its alliance's table points times its own semifinal wins over its alliance's semifinal wins, rounded UP, plus 5 for each Finals win it was on the field for where its alliance won the Finals. The sum is then multiplied by the tier weight (3 at a District Championship, a division and a finals event). The rounding comes BEFORE the weight.
   - IN 2023: the title added 10 to the alliance's points, and a robot was paid the whole of that times ALL its wins (Finals wins included) over all its alliance's wins, rounded up, then times the weight.

### The secured minimum (D2)

7. **Definition.** For a robot with a win at a position: the least the pay rule can end at over every way the rest of the bracket can go, the robot on the field for no further win, its alliance walked down every path still open from the set it plays next (each semifinal set lost or won; where the path reaches the final, the series lost with 0 or 1 wins, or won). An alliance with no match left has one future and the minimum is then exactly what the rule pays. Nothing is capped by hand: only wins pay, a semifinal set is one decisive row however many times a tie is replayed, and a Finals series ends at the second win however many matches it takes.

## Readings

- **R1, the rule is declared per season.** `PLAYOFF_PAY_RULE_BY_SEASON` names 2023 as `allWins` and 2024 to 2026 as `semifinalWins`. A season absent from it is credited nothing, and a unit test holds the declared seasons equal to `BRACKET_REGISTERED_SEASONS`: registering 2027's bracket without deciding its pay rule fails a test instead of crediting on a rule nobody measured. The table's doc comment and the test's failure message tell the person registering a season to read that season's district playoff point rule in the game manual and TBA's `district_helper.py` first, and not to copy last season's line on trust.
- **R2, where a credit exists.** Eight published alliances of at least three picks, no team on two lists, every decisive row at or before the position mapped to two different alliances, the season declared. A team gets one only if a list names it and its minimum is above 0. Anything else credits nobody at that event (the whole event, never a guess for part of it).
- **R3, the bounds rules.** OPEN: `openPlayoffBounds(row, wholeCeiling)` is the only place an open Playoffs category becomes a floor and a ceiling. An exact settled value is TBA's own number: the floor is the larger of it and the secured minimum, ceiling 0. Otherwise the most is the settled placement's ceiling or the whole ceiling, the secured minimum (clamped to 0 and to the most) is the floor, and the rest of the most is the ceiling. Floor plus ceiling is always the most of before this task. FINAL: TBA's number is already in the floor, and `securedBeyondPosted(row)` adds what the secured minimum holds beyond it, which is 0 wherever the number is the real final number. A team's ceiling is its floor plus its open ceiling, so it rises with the floor and never sits below it.
- **R4, the joint proof.** The proof models an unsettled open Playoffs category whole through its alliance values, so a credit in a floor leaves that rival's `extra` by the same amount and the rival's reach is unchanged. What changes is the team's own floor, which rivals must now pass. `extra` can read below 0 for a credited team with nothing else open: the sum floor plus extra is what the proof reads, and it is the sum of before. The proof reads a rival's floor apart from that sum in one place only, the reading of a listed only pick (`withoutSettled` in `champJointLock.ts`), and that is exactly the pick whose credit is withheld (premise P3): its credit goes back from `floor` to `extra` in `poolRivals`. So every bound of after this task is the proof of before it asked at a higher own floor, which the property test of quick task 261010-l0s holds can only lower a bound.
- **R5, the header.** FINAL at the position: the figure holds TBA's number, and `securedBeyondPosted` is added. OPEN and rewound: the category's own number is not in the figure, so the floor of `openPlayoffBounds` is added whole. OPEN at Now: the figure is the published total, which already holds any number TBA has posted for the category, so only what the floor counts beyond that number is added. The same points are never counted twice and the header never falls when a run is pending.
