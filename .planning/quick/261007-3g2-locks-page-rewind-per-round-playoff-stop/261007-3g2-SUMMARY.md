# Quick 261007-3g2: Locks page rewind, per-round playoff stops Summary

The Locks picker now has thirteen stops per event. FIRST's Round 1 to Round 5 replace the single Playoffs stop, and the old stop is renamed Finals. A rewound round stop rebuilds the bracket: sets played at or before the stop's as-of cut keep their real results, and the rest is simulated. No new state block field, no Worker deploy, no republish.

Jacob's reasoning (2026-10-07): the matches that move a locked playoff range are sf5 to sf13 and the finals clincher; round boundaries are the clean cut because sets inside a round never feed each other. Round 1 is included so the list has no gap. Per round: Round 2 locks two alliances at 0 and lifts two floors to 13; Round 3 locks two more at 0 and lifts two floors to 7; Round 4 locks fourth at 7 with floors 20 and 13; Round 5 locks third at 13 with the finalist floor at 20; Finals locks 30 and 20.
