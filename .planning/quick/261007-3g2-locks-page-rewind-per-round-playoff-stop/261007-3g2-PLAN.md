<objective>
Replace the Locks milestone picker's single Playoffs stop with six playoff stops named with FIRST's own
round names: Round 1, Round 2, Round 3, Round 4, Round 5 and Finals. Thirteen stops per event. A rewound
view at a round stop prices the bracket from the sets already played at that stop (real results for
played sets, simulated for the rest) and shows the ledger's locked ranges as they stood then.

Purpose: a reader can watch each alliance's playoff points lock in through the double elimination
bracket instead of jumping from "Alliances done" straight to "Playoffs done".

Output: round steps on the district timeline, round milestones in the picker, played playoff rows at or
before the cut fed into the rewound simulation, a thirteen column picker whose geometry derives from the
stop count, updated copy, methodology sentence, tests and the live only e2e count. Everything comes from
the event artifact and the as-of INDEX that already ship: NO new state block field, NO Worker deploy,
NO republish.

Requirement ids (from the locked CONTEXT, which numbers no D-NN):
- R1 six playoff stops, thirteen per event, FIRST's names
- R2 round to TBA set mapping is BRACKET_SETS' own sf numbering (Round 1 = sets 1 to 4, Round 2 = 5 to 8, Round 3 = 9 and 10, Round 4 = 11 and 12, Round 5 = 13; Finals = f)
- R3 Finals is the existing Playoffs stop renamed (playoffsDone, <eventKey>:playoffs)
- R4 a round stop conditions the bracket on the sets played at or before its cut
- R5 round happened = playoffsDone OR every set of the round played in the loaded artifact; no state block field
- R6 at id <eventKey>:round:<n>; unknown ids resolve to now
- R7 picker geometry derived from the stop count; 375px checked by screenshot
- R8 headers, copy, methodology, tests and the e2e count updated
</objective>
