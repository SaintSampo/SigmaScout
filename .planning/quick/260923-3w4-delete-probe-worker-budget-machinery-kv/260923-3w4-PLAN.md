# Delete the probe Worker, the subrequest budget machinery, and KV

The Cloudflare account moved to Workers Paid on 2026-09-22 (30 s CPU per cron tick, 10,000
subrequests per invocation, 1M KV writes/month). Jacob decided on 2026-09-23, on the audit in
`.planning/quick/260923-1tu-workers-paid-rearchitecture-audit-find-c/260923-1tu-FINDINGS.md`
(section 2 items C2, C3, C4 and section 3), to delete everything that exists only to fit the
retired free-plan limits.

## Tasks

1. **Delete the state-probe Worker.** `apps/worker/src/stateProbe.ts`,
   `apps/worker/test/stateProbe.test.ts`, `apps/worker/wrangler.probe.toml`, the `deploy:probe`
   package script, and every live reference to them (code comments, `docs/worker-operations.md`'s
   "Pre-event probe" section and every probe-only paragraph). Keep `mergeTeamSeasonArtifact` and
   `artifactMerge.ts` — the next quick task reinstates per-team writes and needs them; update the
   comments that say they survive only for the probe.

2. **Delete the subrequest deferral and budget machinery.** Keep `rotate`, `sortEventKeys` and
   their tests (no-starvation is a property of the rotation) and keep the `subrequestsUsed` tick
   log field as telemetry. Delete `SUBREQUEST_RESERVE`, `usableCap`, the `consume`/`tryConsume`
   split, `estimateEventSubrequestCost`, `TICK_FIXED_SUBREQUEST_COST`,
   `EVENT_PREFLIGHT_SUBREQUEST_COST`, `PROBE_SUBREQUEST_COST`, `EventOutcome`'s
   `status: "deferred"` branch, `eventsDeferred`, `RunTickDeps.subrequestCap`/`subrequestReserve`,
   the three opportunistic `budget.remaining > ...` guards, `WriteArtifactResult.deferred` and
   `ArtifactReadBudgetExhaustedError`. Delete or rewrite the tests that pinned deferral, and the
   `[vars]`-style comment blocks that argue from "~41 usable subrequests".

3. **Delete `MAX_PROBES_PER_TICK` and `PROBE_ROTATION_PERIOD_MS`.** Probe every open probe window
   every tick. Keep `runProbes` running BEFORE the algorithms-manifest read and module build (that
   ordering is outage-derived).

4. **Delete `GLOBAL_REBUILD_INTERVAL_MS`.** Rebuild `teams/{year}` on every tick that touched a
   team, keeping the event-completion trigger. Remove `lastGlobalRebuildAtMs` from the tick meta.
   Close `.planning/todos/pending/teams-list-live-delta-overlay.md`.

5. **Delete KV.** `[[kv_namespaces]]` from `apps/worker/wrangler.toml`, `MANIFEST` from
   `apps/worker/src/env.ts`, and the KV branch of `readManifestText` in
   `apps/worker/src/liveWindows.ts`. Fix every test env factory that builds a fake `MANIFEST`, and
   every live doc reference.

6. **Fix the stale `selectChangedRows` comment** in `apps/worker/src/stateStore.ts` that justifies
   it by D1's retired 100,000-rows-written-per-day free allowance. The function stays; its real
   reason is idempotency.
