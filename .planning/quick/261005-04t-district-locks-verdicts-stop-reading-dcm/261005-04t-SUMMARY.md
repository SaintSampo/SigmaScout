# Quick 261005-04t: District Locks verdicts stop reading DCMP points; Declined; rewind note

## Why

`DistrictTeam.pointTotal` on the district artifact is TBA's all tier total. The District tab's lock
floor started from it and subtracted only reopened district tier categories, so once a district
championship was played every rewound position credited teams with championship points they had
not yet earned. Live 2026fim at week one: team 2337 read Locked with 0 earned (192 of its 342
points were championship points). Offline, shipped vs district tier only at end of week 4:
107 / 184 Locked / Locked out vs 48 / 153. Record: memory `district-lock-floor-dcmp-leak`.

## What changed (branch locks-fixes, worktree SigmaScout-locks-fixes)

- 82d9760f `districtLockBounds`: the floor drops every non district tier event total first.
- 5024390e `districtFieldOverlay.ts`: `applyChampionshipFieldOverlay(statuses, artifact, { atLive })`.
  Active only at Live with published capacity and a started championship. Playing reads Locked,
  earned a place and not playing reads Declined, everyone else Locked out. Raw `verdict` and
  `lockedBy` stay the guarantee.
- 45eeb5c4 tenets sweep scores against the district tier final standing (109 seasons, 921,658
  team positions, zero violations on both tenets; the publisher's yardstick gives 571 / 477).
- d043fccc an all zero championship row whose qualification is not finished is "points not yet
  reported" (provisionally playing), so a live championship cannot flash Declined before points land.
- 1ead5f4c Declined chip (Out of range look), rendered only when someone is Declined; field
  definition set while the overlay is active; rewind note on both tabs below the picker's arrows
  when the selection is not Live; three Methodology paragraphs; live spec guards.
