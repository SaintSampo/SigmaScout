<objective>
Three changes, all decided by Jacob on 2026-10-05.

1. The District Locks tab's Locked and Locked out verdicts stop reading District Championship points (D-01, D-02).
2. Once the championship has started, the Live view shows who is actually playing it, with a sixth status, Declined (D-06).
3. Rewound views on both Locks tabs carry one plain note that their predictions use later knowledge, and Methodology says the same (D-03, D-04).

The cause of 1 is proven and is NOT to be re-investigated. `DistrictTeam.pointTotal` is TBA's all tier total. `computeDistrictLedgerStatuses` starts its floor from it and subtracts only reopened DISTRICT tier categories, so once a championship has been played every team's championship points sit in the District tab's lock floor at every position.

Why 2 exists: with the floor fixed, 91 teams across the 14 live 2026 districts read differently at now, among them 29 that played their championship and would read Locked out. Jacob chose to show the field on the Live view instead.

THE RULE JACOB APPROVED.
- Any rewound moment, and any time before the District Championship starts: district event points only. This is the raw verdict, Task 1.
- Once the championship has started, on the Live view only, who is playing it decides what the tab SHOWS: playing reads Locked, earned a place but not playing reads Declined, everyone else reads Locked out.

THE OVERLAY, exactly (D-06).
1. The raw model is `computeDistrictLedgerStatuses` as Task 1 leaves it. The overlay is a separate pure function over that model. It changes only the displayed `status` and `byAward`; `verdict` and `lockedBy` stay the raw guarantee on every result. Every consumer that needs the guarantee (the chance run, the cutoff, the champ tab, the scripts) keeps reading the raw model and never calls the overlay.
2. It applies only when all three hold: the caller passes atLive true (absent means no overlay); `artifact.dcmpSlots` is not null; and the championship has started, meaning some dcmp tier entry of some team, in eventPoints or remainingEvents, has `deriveStageFromState(state).started` true.
3. A team is PLAYING when it has a dcmp tier eventPoints entry with qual plus alliance plus elim above zero. A team with no dcmp tier eventPoints entry at all (points not yet reported for it) is also playing when it has a dcmp tier remainingEvents entry, unless its raw verdict is neither locked nor lockedAward AND it holds a qualifying award with awardOnly true from one of its district tier events (an award only invitee). A dcmp tier eventPoints entry carrying award points only is NOT playing.
4. Shown status: playing gives locked, with byAward true exactly when the raw verdict is lockedAward. Not playing with a raw verdict of locked or lockedAward gives declined, byAward false. Everyone else gives lockedOut, byAward false; that includes teams tied on the line that are not playing, and award only attendees. A prequalified result passes through. So under the overlay no team reads In range or Out of range.

Purpose: a rewound District Locks view must not call a team Locked at week one because of points it earned at a championship two months later, and the Live view after the championship must not call a team that played it Locked out.
