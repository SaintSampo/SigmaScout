THE MODEL SHAPE (pre registered here; the executor implements it as written and does not change it after seeing 2023 to 2026 scores):
Per DCMP tier event the run carries a SLATE: an expected count of each award given AT THIS EVENT and each team's eligibility chance. Roles: `single` (consuming awards and judged), `division` (judged only), `finals` (consuming only, judged count from its own measured anchor, expected 0), `wholeChampionship` (the generated simulated DCMP bake that stands for a whole championship: consuming awards at full district share plus the judged anchor times the number of the district's single or division keys, at least 1).
- Consuming counts: the mean of `dcmpAwardCountDistribution(season, districtKey, cmpSlots ?? 0, setting.countMode)` per type (the champ draws' own count model; setting = `champCutoffTuning(season).setting` unless passed), times the event's share: 1 divided by the number of the district's championships (keys of role single or finals) for single and finals, 0 for division, 1 for wholeChampionship.
- Judged count: a measured walk forward anchor per role (the mean number of judged award winning teams per event of that role in the most recent prior season holding that role; fallback rung the pooled single plus division mean of that season; a season with neither is not registered).
- Who: within the award field, team j's Impact chance is a water fill of total K = expected Impact over weights a_j = q_j times liftImpact(decorationBucket(priorImpactWins_j)) with caps q_j (so P(win) never exceeds P(eligible)); EI the same over veterans with liftEI(decorationBucket(priorJudgedAwards_j)); RAS the same over rookies with lift 1 (rookies carry no prior awards to sort on). Judged: c_j = 1 minus (1 minus pImpact_j)(1 minus p8_j); water fill total J over weights liftJudged(cell_j) times (1 minus c_j) with caps (1 minus c_j), then pJudged_j = filled_j / (1 minus c_j) (0 where 1 minus c_j is 0). So expected judged winners equal J and judged awards go to teams that did not win a consuming award.
- Lifts are measured walk forward: for each eligible (or non consuming) team event, e = the event's actual count over its pool size, y = won; lift(class) = sum y over sum e. Classes below 100 observations fall to the pooled rung (lift 1), named. Judged cells fall cell, then bucket pooled, then season pooled (lift 1); these rung names are `AwardBaseRateSource`'s own three so `awardSources` keeps its type.
- Draw: Impact Bernoulli (only if its chance is above 0), then the 8 point Bernoulli (EI for a veteran, RAS for a rookie, only if above 0), then judged Bernoulli (only if above 0); the highest single award is kept (10, else 8, else 5, else 0) times the tier weight. Never a stack.
- Exact odds: P10 = pI; P8 = (1 minus pI) p8; P5 = (1 minus pI)(1 minus p8) pJ; P0 the rest.
- Every profile in the award field must carry both prior counts; if any lacks one, every team's lift falls to the season pooled rung (lift 1) for that event and `awardSources` says so. No team is ever priced from a fabricated class.

Pending eligibility (a team entered at a district event whose awards are still open at the position), stated: P(eligible) = 1 minus the product over its open district events E of (1 minus w_T,E / W_E), where w is the champ draws' own entrant weight (`dcmpEntrantWeight` under `setting.weighting`) and W_E the sum over E's entrants. This is exactly the chance the champ draws make T its event's entrant in one run, so the two cannot disagree. A held team is 1. Everyone else is 0.
