Planner choices (state these in the SUMMARY):
1. shiftedByFinals and the Champ Locks District points row: a best guess prints ONLY where the cell has pricing, its season has a registered bracket, it is not flagged shiftedByFinals, and the five named placements account for all of its mass. Every other Playoffs cell (the District points row, which has no pricing because it sums several events; a DCMP division cell moved up by finals points; a pre 2023 season; any off table mass) prints the MEDIAN FORM, `~N` over `likely a to b`. Reason: such a cell is a sum of more than one event, no single finish describes it, its drawer already draws a histogram with median and likely tiles, and the median form is how this site prints every other sum (Subtotal, event total, grand total). This also retires the old top four wording completely. The flag is checked explicitly because a finals shift can land exactly on named values (a DCMP 21 plus a finals 39 is 60).
2. playoffMilestone SURVIVES, trimmed to its kind. The lock math never reads it (it reads `playoffMilestoneByTeam`), but the drawer's ruled out filter (`districtPlayoffOutcomes`) and therefore the best guess need the kind. Its `chance` and `conditionalMedian` have no reader left and are removed, along with the threshold summary that computed them.
3. The drawer's HEADLINE and most likely TILE for a Playoffs cell follow the cell's best guess. Without this, the fixture already used across the tests (third 20, fourth 16, none 34) would read `best guess 3rd or 4th` on the cell and `Out before the top four in 34 of 100 runs.` in the drawer. The outcome list rows (third and fourth stay separate), the chance of points tile and the ruled out filtering stay exactly as shipped.
