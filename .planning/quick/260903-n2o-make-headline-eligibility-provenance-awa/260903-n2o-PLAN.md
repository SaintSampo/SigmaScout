<objective>
Restore the structural guarantee that quick task `260903-krp` deleted: a season the
optimizer was fitted on can no longer be marked headline-eligible. And, first, delete the
user-facing sentence that asserts leak-free hyperparameter selection for 2022, 2023 and
2024 — a claim the shipped provenance contradicts and which reaches production on the next
push of `main` with no republish required.

Purpose: `isHeadlineEligible(season, corpusSeasons)` encodes a premise about the OPTIMIZER
while reading only a list of years. The premise ("this season's hyperparameters were
selected using only seasons before it") becomes true when the rolling-origin re-tune
promotes origin-selected parameters. It has not. Until then the rule must check the fact
rather than assume it.

Output: a provenance-aware eligibility rule with a required, non-defaulting per-algorithm
selected-on input; a corpus-sourced (not CLI-range-sourced) season set; real coverage on
`publish.ts`'s call site; a corrected report caption; and a Compare page that says less
rather than saying something false.
</objective>
