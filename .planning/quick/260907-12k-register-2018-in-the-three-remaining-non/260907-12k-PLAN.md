# Quick task 260907-12k: Register 2018 in the three remaining non-user-visible registries

## Motivation

2018 is ingested (2026-09-06) and registered in both algorithm dispatch tables — score components
and RP rules — as of quick tasks 260906-813 and 260907-057. Rows 1-3 of the nine-registry table in
`.planning/todos/pending/extend-corpus-2018-2017-2016.md` are closed.

This task closes rows 4, 5 and 8: the district point ceilings, the district display-name map, and
the Statbotics reference constants. All three are **inert** until either a 2018 publish or a
harness run reaches 2018, which is precisely why they are safe to land ahead of the publish.

## THE HARD CONSTRAINT — `apps/web/src/lib/seasons.ts` is not touched

`FIRST_SEASON` stays `2019`. Flipping it to 2018 puts 2018 in the live year dropdown immediately,
and **no 2018 artifacts exist in R2**, so every 2018 page would 404. This project already hit
exactly that during the 2019/2020 rollout, when a concurrent push deployed a year dropdown ahead
of its artifacts. The correct ordering is **publish, then reveal**, and the publish is neither done
nor in scope here.

Row 7 (`seasons.ts`) therefore stays OPEN in the todo table, and each of the three files touched
here gets one sentence recording that its 2018 entry is deliberately inert until the publish lands.
That sentence is the mechanism that stops a future reader "completing" the set by flipping
`FIRST_SEASON` without checking R2.

## What was measured, and by whom

Every value below was measured by the **orchestrator** on 2026-09-07 and independently
spot-confirmed by the **planner** against `data/corpus.sqlite` the same day. Transcribe; the

### R-1 — district point ceilings: 2018 is identical to every other registered season

Parsed from `district_rankings.event_points_raw` over all 2018 rows (3,724 `event_points` entries
across 1,543 team rows), split by tier:

| tier | qual | alliance | elim | award |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| district | **22** | **16** | **30** | **15** |
| dcmp (observed) | 66 | 48 | 90 | 30 |

Rookie-bonus maximum observed in 2018: **10**. DCMP weight is **3**, confirmed directly — every
dcmp maximum is exactly 3x its district counterpart (66/22, 48/16, 90/30).

**The one nuance that must be written down rather than smoothed over.** 2018's *observed* dcmp
award maximum is 30, where 2019's is 45 (= 3 x 15, hitting the declared ceiling exactly). 2018
simply never had a team collect the full award stack at a District Championship. `award: 15` is
still correct as a **ceiling**: the module's own header already establishes that a ceiling set too
LOW is the only direction this model must never err in (it understates a rival's reachable total
and can declare a false "locked" guarantee), while a ceiling set too high "only delays a verdict".
**Do not lower 2018's award ceiling to 10.** State the reason in the header.

### R-2 — district display names: two spellings 2018 introduces

`SELECT DISTINCT district_key FROM events WHERE year = 2018` on 2026-09-07 and got exactly this

`tx` did not exist in 2018 and needs nothing. This is the identical re-keying class the file's own

### R-3 — Statbotics reference: 2018 is available and it corroborates the additivity story

The endpoint is `/v3/year/{season}`. An earlier orchestrator probe of `/v3/season/2018` returned
404 — that is the **wrong path** and must not be recorded anywhere as evidence 2018 is
unavailable. Live values fetched 2026-09-07:

| season | acc (`metrics.win_prob.season.acc`) | mse | count |
|---|---|---|---|
| **2018** | **0.7435** | **0.1747** | 16,930 |
| 2019 | 0.7322 | 0.1763 | 18,022 |
| 2022 | 0.7815 | 0.1502 | 14,645 |

Statbotics' own 2018 accuracy (0.7435) sits well below its 2022 (0.7815). That is independent,
third-party corroboration that 2018 is a genuinely harder season to predict — consistent with this
project's own measurement that 2018 is the only corpus season whose alliance scores are
*anti*-correlated (corr(red, blue) totalPoints = -0.4567 against +0.24..+0.52 everywhere else). It
is a useful anchor for any future 2018 headline claim and deserves a sentence in the file.

## Concurrency — staged-path assertion is mandatory

Other sessions commit to this checkout. A plain `git add <paths>` is **not** sufficient: it does
not prove that only those paths are staged. This bit this project for real on quick task
260906-813, where a concurrent session's edits were absorbed into an unrelated commit.

Every task stages by explicit path, then asserts the staged set **equals** the intended set, then
commits. The assertion command is written out in each task's Verify block. If it reports a
mismatch, **HALT** — do not `git reset` blindly and do not commit; report what was found.
