# Plan: adversarial statistical review of BPR

Answer four questions about the *shipped* BPR (`packages/core/algorithms/bpr.ts`,
`packages/bpr/frozen-params.json`), as a statistician rather than as a re-measurer:

1. What is the estimator, stripped to its load-bearing skeleton?
2. Which parts carry the model and which are decoration?
3. Where is it misspecified, and how would we know?
4. What can be simplified or fixed without spending the sealed holdout?
