# Quick Task 260910-x09: EPA Deviation Ablation

The first measurement of what SigmaScout's EPA divergences from Statbotics actually
cost. Built as stage 1 of 4; stages 2-4 (land the carryover fix, audit the
component-map claim, ship the checkbox UI) consume this task's output.

## What was built

`scripts/measureEpaDeviations.ts` (+ tests, + `pnpm measure:epa-deviations`, +
`reports/epa-deviation-ablation.json` for stage 4). Four arms driven over one
shared chronological stream per season, `carrySeason`-threaded across boundaries,
scored with `aggregateScores` — **the published scorer, which counts ties** — so
these numbers are directly comparable to published ones rather than to a scratch
convention.

**Arms are wrappers, not edits.** Each is `{ ...epa, id, predict, update, carrySeason }`
built inside `scripts/`. `git diff -- packages/` is empty across all three commits.
Nothing shipped changed; the carryover fix exists only as an arm.

**Run:** 9 seasons (2016-2019, 2022-2026), 4 arms, 147,221 scored matches, 1,653 event blocks.

## THE HEADLINE — the carryover fix works, and works exactly where it should

Pooled, paired, event-blocked:

| metric | delta | 95% CI | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| winner accuracy | **+0.01059** | [+0.00846, +0.01285] | BETTER |
| Brier | **-0.00521** | [-0.00638, -0.00412] | BETTER |

Both improve, so **Rule A is satisfied** (ship when accuracy AND Brier both improve).

### Where the gain lives — the pre-registered concentration test

The mechanism predicts the effect concentrates at boundaries where the point scale
MOVES, and is near-nil where it does not. Pre-registration was explicit that a
uniform effect would REFUTE the mechanism even if the pooled number looked good.

| season | scale ratio | epa ACC | fix ACC | Δ ACC | verdict (paired) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2016 | cold start | 0.7195 | 0.7195 | 0 | IDENTICAL |
| 2017 | 2.76× grow | 0.6680 | 0.6720 | +0.0040 | better (marginal) |
| 2018 | 1.24× grow | 0.7333 | 0.7315 | −0.0018 | indistinguishable |
| **2019** | **0.19× collapse** | **0.6441** | **0.7236** | **+0.0794** | **BETTER** |
| 2022 | 1.06× flat | 0.7697 | 0.7749 | +0.0051 | better (marginal) |
| 2023 | 1.68× grow | 0.7602 | 0.7593 | −0.0009 | indistinguishable |
| 2024 | 0.61× shrink | 0.7513 | 0.7548 | +0.0036 | indistinguishable |
| 2025 | 1.91× grow | 0.7773 | 0.7780 | +0.0007 | indistinguishable |
| 2026 | 1.89× grow | 0.7943 | 0.7931 | −0.0012 | indistinguishable |

**2019 is the whole result.** `winnerAccuracy +0.07943, 95% [+0.06775, +0.09115]`,
`brier -0.04234, 95% [-0.04946, -0.03545]`, 194 event blocks. The single boundary
with a 5× scale collapse is the single season that moves, and it moves by 7.9
percentage points. Every other season sits inside ±0.5pp.

That is the concentration test passing. The pooled +1.06pp is not a broad
improvement — it is 2019 averaged across nine seasons, and it should be described
that way rather than as a general accuracy gain.

## RECOMMENDATION FOR STAGE 2: ADOPT

Three independent reasons, in order of weight:

1. **It is a correctness fix, not a tuning win.** The project's own verbatim reading
   of Statbotics' `init.py` (`02-CONTEXT.md` D-16) specifies the carry is "converted
   into the NEW season's point units." `carryover.ts` converts into the OUTGOING
   season's. This is a defect against our own recorded reference and would be worth
   fixing even at neutral accuracy.
2. **It is decisively better on the season it affects** and Rule A holds pooled.
3. **It is harmless on the live season.** 2026: accuracy indistinguishable (−0.0012,
   CI spans zero), Brier BETTER (−0.00027, CI excludes zero).

### Where it is worse — state these plainly when shipping

- **2018 Brier: +0.00052, 95% [+0.00030, +0.00075] — WORSE, interval excludes zero.**
  The only definitive regression anywhere in the run. Tiny, but real.
- **2019 ONSET (first 500 matches) Brier: +0.00833, [+0.00127, +0.01741] — WORSE**,
  on the very boundary where the season-long gain is largest. This is the fix's
  honest weak spot: the live per-season scale estimate is noisy before it has
  observed much, so the lazy conversion trades a confidently-wrong scale for a
  correct-but-uncertain one. It pays for itself many times over across the season
  (+7.9pp) but it is not free at the instant of the boundary.
  **Candidate refinement for stage 2:** defer conversion until a minimum observation
  count, mirroring `EPA_SCORE_SD_SEED_COUNT`'s existing fading-seed idea. Worth
  measuring before adopting; not worth blocking adoption on.
- No season shows a definitive accuracy regression.

## The win-probability arms — the invariant held

Pre-registered: a scale on the logistic cannot change `sign(margin)`, so winner
accuracy MUST be identical to baseline in every season; the run throws naming the
season if not. **It did not throw.** Every paired winner-accuracy difference is
exactly `0.00000`, in all nine seasons, for both win-prob arms — so exactly, that the
bootstrap declined to resample (there is no uncertainty to resample).

Brier moves, which is the entire point:

| arm | Brier delta | 95% CI | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `epa-winprob-season-sd` (leaky reference bound) | +0.00546 | [+0.00518, +0.00573] | WORSE |
| `epa-winprob-fixed-sd` | +0.00478 | [+0.00390, +0.00564] | WORSE |

Both are worse than what ships, which **vindicates the current expanding-window
choice** rather than merely excusing it. Note `epa-winprob-season-sd` uses the
season-final SD and is therefore outcome leakage — it is an unshippable reference
bound, and it still loses. This pair is the single most useful thing stage 4's UI can
show a reader: a deviation that provably cannot touch winner accuracy while clearly
moving calibration teaches why this site reports both.

## What could NOT be measured, and why (three different reasons)

Named rather than silently omitted:

- **`component-map` — `unmeasurable-in-this-harness`.** `epa.ts`'s `update()` and
  `carrySeason()` call `componentMapForSeason(season)` DIRECTLY; there is no injection
  point, and a `scripts/` wrapper sees only the state before and after, never the map
  the update used. The missing seam is an optional component-map parameter threaded
  through `update()`/`carrySeason()` — a `packages/` change, therefore out of scope for
  a measurement-only task. **This is stage-3 input:** the audit needs that seam first.
- **`per-year-postprocess-2018` — `unmeasurable-no-reference`.** The 2018 sigmoid's exact
  form was never transcribed into this repo. Implementing a guess would measure an
  invention. Recovering it means re-fetching Statbotics' source. See the correction
  filed in `epa-divergences.md` §3 below.
- **`fouls-cross-attribution`, `offseason-population` — `unmeasurable-in-this-harness`** (no seam).
- **`elim-weight`, `variance-free` — `closed`** (no longer divergences).
- **`published-total-excludes-fouls` — `display-only`.** Labelled, not measured: a
  display-only deviation cannot move a win probability, and measuring it would emit a
  suspicious `0.000` a reader would have to be told to ignore.

## Doc correction filed

`docs/models/epa-divergences.md` §3 justified skipping per-year post-processing partly
on this basis:

> "2022-2026 (this project's covered seasons) postdate 2018, so the switch/scale
> sigmoid would not fire for any season this project scores regardless"

That went stale when the corpus was backfilled to 2016. **2018 is scored today** —
16,889 scorable matches — so the sigmoid fires on a covered season and this is a live
unmeasured deviation, not a moot one. The correction withdraws only the "never fires"
convenience argument; the standing-policy half of the reasoning is untouched and still
stands on its own. The `unmeasurable-no-reference` status and what closing it requires
are recorded alongside it.

## For stage 4 (the checkbox UI) — CANCELLED 2026-09-11, DO NOT BUILD

**Stage 4 was cancelled by the developer after stage 3, and the section below is retained
only as the historical design note. Do not read it as an open TODO.**

Why it was cancelled: by the end of stage 3 the register held exactly ONE deviation with
`status: "measured"` — `winprob-scale`. `carryover-scale-anchor` was fixed and closed in
`epa@8.0.0+baseline` (quick task 260911-3kc); `elim-weight` and `variance-free` were already
closed; `component-map`, `per-year-postprocess-2018`, `fouls-cross-attribution` and
`offseason-population` are all unmeasurable; `published-total-excludes-fouls` is display-only.

So k = 1, which kills the feature twice over. There is no combinatorial problem to solve
(2^1 = 2), and the single toggleable deviation provably CANNOT move winner accuracy — a scale
on the logistic cannot change `sign(margin)`, verified as exactly `0.00000` in all nine
seasons. A "tick a box and watch winner accuracy move" surface would have shipped one checkbox
that cannot move the number it points at, beside eight rows that cannot be ticked at all.

The content survived; only the interaction died. It lives in
`reports/epa-deviation-ablation.json`, `docs/models/epa-divergences.md`, and
`docs/models/statbotics-breakdown-reference.md`. If a future reader wants a page, the honest
shape is a STATIC table of the register — status and measured effect per deviation, with the
"unmeasurable, and here is exactly why" rows kept, since those are the most credible cells.

*Historical design note, from before the cancellation:*

`reports/epa-deviation-ablation.json` carries one row per (deviation, season, arm) with
stable keys. Two things the UI must respect:

- `notes.additive: false` — individual effects were measured, combinations were NOT.
  2^k arms is 16 at k=4. The UI must not sum deviation effects to synthesize a combined
  figure; `arm.deviations` is an array so real combination arms can be added later
  without a schema change.
- Unmeasurable deviations carry their reason string. Render the reason, not a blank or
  a zero — "we could not measure this and here is why" is the honest cell, and for the
  2018 sigmoid it is also the most interesting one on the page.
