# Quick Task 260909-25z: Re-bootstrap the full 23-knob vs parsimonious BPR gap

## The decision, first

**F-20 INVERTS.** The number it now rests on is **+0.421pp, 95% [0.270, 0.569]**
(FULL minus PARSIMONIOUS, paired event-clustered, 2000 resamples, post-P3 design
era). F-20 had concluded KILLED on a 0.238pp gap against a 0.31pp bar, "more
comfortably" so under 0.45pp. Nothing about that survives.

**The pre-registered rule FIRES** - all three conditions pass on both symmetric
arms. **My recommendation is still DO NOT FIRE THE 2023 TEST YET**, and the
reason is not that the gap is doubtful. It is that **39% of the gap is one
knob**, so the 23-knob variant is not what the scarce shot should be spent on.

`TEST-2023.md` is written, committed and **NOT executed**.

## The three arms

Orientation throughout: **delta = FULL minus PARSIMONIOUS**. Positive accuracy
and negative Brier both favour the full variant.

| arm | dAcc (pp) | 95% interval | excl 0 | dBrier | 95% interval | excl 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A as-recorded | **+0.379** | [0.221, 0.538] | YES | -0.00081 | [-0.00110, -0.00052] | YES |
| B re-tuned (governing) | **+0.421** | [0.270, 0.569] | YES | -0.00089 | [-0.00114, -0.00063] | YES |
| C mixed (diagnostic) | +0.421 | [0.270, 0.569] | YES | -0.00089 | [-0.00114, -0.00063] | YES |

n = 82,735 over 911 event blocks in every arm. Levels: parsimonious 72.871% /
0.17559 Brier; full re-tuned 73.291% / 0.17470.

**Rule A passes on accuracy AND Brier**, which was the condition written to catch
a candidate that wins one and loses the other. It does not happen here.

## Rule application

| condition | Arm A | Arm B | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rule A - dAcc > 0 AND dBrier < 0 | +0.379, -0.00081 | +0.421, -0.00089 | PASS |
| Signal - accuracy interval excludes zero | [0.221, 0.538] | [0.270, 0.569] | PASS |
| Consistency - both arms agree | same sign, both exclude | same sign, both exclude | PASS |

**Verdict of the rule: FIRE** (4.8 sigma on Arm A, 5.5 sigma on Arm B).

One wobble recorded rather than smoothed over: `RULE.md` did not name a slice for
the Signal condition. On the **champs** slice Arm A's interval includes zero
while Arm B's excludes it, so a champs-slice Consistency condition would have
FAILED. The contrast is the combined pairing, but the ambiguity was real and it
favoured the outcome.

## Why the forking-paths objection does not bite here

The obvious attack on this result is that a bar was re-derived after the fact in
the direction that flips the answer. That objection is correct in general and is
stated in `VERDICT.md` without hedging.

**It does not apply, and the reason is checkable.** Both of F-20's numbers moved:

| | F-20 | measured here |
|---|---|---|
| the gap | 0.238pp | **0.421pp** |
| the bar | 0.31pp ("really ~0.45pp") | 0.169pp paired (0.152pp = 2x this contrast's own SE) |

The measured gap **exceeds F-20's original, uncorrected 0.31pp constant on its
own**. `DECISION.md` rule 5 fires under **both** readings - the literal constant
and its own stated "two standard errors" definition. **No threshold had to move
for this verdict to hold**, so there is no fork to have taken. `VERDICT.md`
recommends treating the literal constant as operative precisely because it
requires nothing to be re-derived.

## What actually drives the gap - the finding that changes the recommendation

The parsimonious model emits **268 exactly-0.5 predictions** across the design
era, **every one in 2016**. The full model emits one. Under D-Q3 an abstention on
a decided match is a **miss**.

- Per-season paired delta in 2016: **+1.098pp** (267 no-calls). Every other
  season: +0.03 to +0.45pp.
- Drop no-call units: gap falls **0.421 to 0.277pp**. Drop 2016: **0.291pp**.
- **`biasLr = 0.001` alone**, at its pre-P3 value with **no re-tune**, removes
  **269 of 270** no-calls and lifts accuracy **72.871% to 73.035%** - **0.164pp,
  39% of the whole gap**. Brier improves too.

At cold start every team sits at an identical prior, the alliances tie
numerically, and the model returns exactly 0.5. The side bias breaks the tie.
That is **tie-breaking, not predictive skill** - though D-Q3 is right to charge
for it. It also explains why `DECISION.md`'s ablation table scored the side bias
at a mildly *harmful* +0.03pp: the pre-P3 convention did not penalise an
abstention, so **the old convention hid the defect D-Q3 charges for**.

## The F-23 asymmetry did not arise

The three-arm design existed to prevent re-tuning only the full side. It turned
out to be unnecessary: **the post-P3 re-tune of the parsimonious side returned
its seed unchanged** - zero differing keys, converged after sweep 1 with no
accepted move. `frozen-params.json` was already at a post-P3 coordinate-descent
optimum. So Arm C is bit-identical to Arm B, there is no un-re-tuned side to
inflate against, and **Arm B carries no F-23 discount**. The widening from
+0.379 to +0.421 is entirely the full side improving.

## Recommendation, with the tensions

**DO NOT FIRE YET.** Argued in `VERDICT.md` section 5:

- **(a)** A **14-knob** parsimonious-plus-side-bias variant has never been
  measured and captures 0.164pp of the 0.421pp for one parameter. Firing on the
  23-knob variant leaves the decisive question unanswered and the holdout a shot
  poorer. The alternative is **free** - design-era only.
- **(b)** Parameter bloat is a standing rule. Five of the extra components
  measured under 0.1pp individually, and `elimWeight` has an independent negative
  result in this repo (2026-09-05, 6/6 keep-incumbent) - on VPR, so suggestive
  rather than dispositive.
- **(c)** F-23 still applies to the **attribution**: after `biasLr`, **0.257pp
  remains unattributed** across four components, or an interaction none of them
  owns. Adopting ten knobs for an effect whose source is unidentified is this
  project's own recorded failure mode.
- **(d)** Firewall erosion: rule 5 is itself pre-registered, so honouring it is
  not inventing a rule. The real risk is **re-sealing twice** - once now, once
  next week on a better variant. Re-seal once.
- **(e)** **2023 is not virgin data.** It was read once inside `260908-b4t`'s
  single sealed 2023-2026 run. A candidate-vs-incumbent run is a legitimate new
  test, but a clean out-of-sample season is not on offer.

Next: measure the 14-knob variant, decompose the residual with each component
**re-tuned**, then pre-register once and fire once.
