## Accept / reject rule — the same Rule-A shape as RULE.md

Orientation is `holdout.ts`'s own: **delta = CANDIDATE minus INCUMBENT = FULL
minus PARSIMONIOUS**. Positive accuracy delta and negative Brier delta both
favour the full variant.

**ACCEPT the full 23-knob variant only if ALL of:**

1. **Rule A** — accuracy delta **above zero** AND Brier delta **below zero**, on
   the **combined** pairing. Accuracy alone is not a pass. Winning accuracy while
   losing Brier is a **decisive reject**.
2. **Signal** — the accuracy delta's 95% percentile interval **excludes zero** on
   the **combined** pairing.

The slice named is **combined**, deliberately and in advance — `VERDICT.md`
section 2 records that `RULE.md`'s silence on this point was a real ambiguity
that happened to favour the outcome, and it is not repeated here. Champs and
non-champs are reported and read, but they do not gate.

**Otherwise: REJECT.** The parsimonious model stays frozen and the design-era
gap is recorded as not having generalised.
