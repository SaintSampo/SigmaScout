Why BOTH sides. Re-tuning only the full variant and comparing it against the
pre-P3-tuned frozen-params.json is exactly the F-23 asymmetry - one side fitted
to the new scoring path, the other not - and it inflates the full side. Symmetry
is the requirement, not a nicety. Each side seeds from its OWN recorded pre-P3
optimum, which mirrors what DECISION.md rule 4 did within a single objective.
