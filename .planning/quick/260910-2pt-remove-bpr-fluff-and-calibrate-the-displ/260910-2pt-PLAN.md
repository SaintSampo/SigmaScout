# Plan: remove BPR fluff, calibrate the displayed variance

Acts on recommendations A, B and C of `260910-25c-REVIEW.md`.

## Scope

**Remove fluff**
- `seasonShrink`, frozen at its identity value 1.0, whose expression
  `p.seasonShrink * muL + (1 - p.seasonShrink) * 1.0` reduces to `muL` while
  reading as though a yearly shrink toward league average happened.
- The write-only `this.year` field in `packages/bpr/model.ts`.
- The two docstrings quick task 260910-25c proved false: "anti-additivity"
  (the code is super-additive for every alliance) and the slow-talent/fast-form
  framing (92.5% of each update lands in the component zeroed each season).

**Calibrate the displayed variance**
- Fit `sd(z) = a + b*(mu - 3)` on design-era innovations.
- Apply `c(mu)^2` to `variance`, `redScoreVarianceOwn`, `blueScoreVarianceOwn`
  in `predict()` — and to nothing else.

## Constraints (pre-committed)

- **Design era only.** The calibration is fitted on 2016-2022. No holdout figure
  is read or produced.
- **No re-tune.** No value in `BPR_PARAMS` or `frozen-params.json` changes.
  Standing decision: BPR tuning is closed.
- **Prediction neutrality is the load-bearing invariant.** `pRedWin` and
  `winner` must be BIT-IDENTICAL, because that is the only thing that keeps the
  sealed 78.05% a true statement about this module. Proven by fingerprint over
  the design era, not asserted.
- **`spread` is out of scope.** `TeamMetrics.spread` is the algorithm's own
  confidence and never reaches the screen (2026-09-09 decision), so it is not a
  displayed interval and is left alone.

## Tasks

1. Fit the calibration on design-era innovations; record the fit and its residuals.
2. Remove `seasonShrink`, the dead `this.year`, and correct the two docstrings.
3. Apply the calibration to the three emitted variance fields only.
4. Bump `BPR_VERSION` 1.0.0 -> 2.0.0 (D-13: `predict()`'s observable output changed).
5. Prove prediction neutrality by replaying the shipped port over the design era
   before and after, comparing a sha256 over every `pRedWin`.
6. Pin the invariant in `bpr.test.ts`; typecheck; run the full suite and
   separate pre-existing red from new red.
