# Quick task 260906-6zc: Carry-mean shrinkage sweep (`carryMeanReversion`)

## Motivation

`reports/autopsy-260905/FINDINGS.md` named the mechanism behind EPA's accuracy lead: it is an
EARLY-INFORMATION edge. EPA wins the early-season, early-event, green-team and blowout slices
and loses (or draws) the late-season, late-event, well-observed and elimination slices. Its
stated implication was "stronger cross-season priors / cold-start initialization in VPR".

Every experiment since read "stronger" as MORE CONFIDENT and moved the carry VARIANCE:

| task | knob | verdict |
|---|---|---|
| 260905-jyf (Stage 1) | `carryVarianceFactor` screen | inconclusive/negative |
| 260905-kjb (Stage 2) | `carryVarianceFactor` tune | 2025 +0.41pt, missed bar by 0.005pt |
| 260905-o48 (Stage 3) | `carryEvidenceRate` | ten keep-incumbent verdicts |
| 260904-v9n | `elimObservationNoiseMultiplier` | closed NEGATIVE, 6/6 keep-incumbent |
| 260905-tpx | RP own-scale process noise | NO-WIN |
| 260905-wwt | online `carryTrust` EWMA | DOES-NOT-VALIDATE |

All six failed the same way: they help 2025 and are vetoed by 2024, which has gone 0-for-16
accuracy-positive across every carry-confidence formulation tried.

**Nobody has moved the carried MEAN.** `sigma1CarryNormalizedRating`
(`packages/core/algorithms/sigma1/carryover.ts:128`) shrinks a carried rating toward
`EPA_ROOKIE_BASELINE` by `params.carryMeanReversion`. EPA's equivalent is FROZEN at Statbotics'
`EPA_MEAN_REVERSION = 0.4` (D-04). VPR's promoted values are **0.0693** (2023, 2024) and
**0.0880** (2025, 2026) — VPR keeps ~91-93% of last season's rating spread where EPA keeps 60%.

VPR simultaneously RESETS belief variance to the full cold-start prior
(`carryVarianceFactor: 1` for every season but 2025). The combination is incoherent: maximally
dispersed carried means that the filter then refuses to trust.

This predicts the two autopsy signatures the variance work never explained:

- VPR is systematically MORE confident on disagreements (mean `|p-0.5|` 0.065 vs EPA's 0.039)
  while LOSING them.
- VPR loses the blowout slice (margin >20) 56.1%/43.9%, the largest single disagreement slice
  (n=2,705 of 5,429).

Both are the fingerprint of OVER-DISPERSED ratings, not of a weak prior.

**Why this is not more of the same.** Raising `carryMeanReversion` makes carried ratings LESS
extreme. Every failed experiment pushed the opposite sign. 2024's veto reason — "priors mislead
when a season breaks pattern" — is an argument FOR this direction, not against it.

## Interface facts (verified, this session)

**Cold start is positional.** `seasonBoundaryFor` sets `isColdStart: index === 0`
(`packages/harness/seasonBoundary.ts:63`), and `carrySeason` returns before any carry work when
it is true (`sigma1/index.ts:1777`). On a `--seasons 2022-2026` replay **2022 carries nothing**,
so `carryMeanReversion` CANNOT move 2022. This is the same free control 260905-wwt used, and it
is the sha256 check in C-3 below.

**Gap is 1 at every carry-active boundary in range.** `reversionOverGap(r, gap)` returns `r`
unchanged when `gap === 1` (`carryover.ts:92-95`), so effective shrinkage equals the raw
parameter for 2023, 2024, 2025 and 2026. (2022's promoted 0.5261 would compound to 0.775 over
its two-year gap from 2020, but 2022 is cold in this range and never reaches that code.)

**One edit site.** `params.carryMeanReversion` is read in exactly one place that can move a
prediction: `sigma1CarryNormalizedRating` (`carryover.ts:128`). EPA's own carry
(`carryover.ts`'s frozen `epaCarryover` / `carryNormalizedRating`) is a SEPARATE function that
never reads Sigma1 params (D-04) — patching the Sigma1 path structurally cannot move the EPA
baseline.

**Per-season promoted values, and provenance.** Only 2025 and 2026 are
`sourceKind: "search-winner"`. 2019/2020/2022/2023/2024 are `carried-version` from
`reports/tune-joint-off.json`, whose own committed `note` says every parameter but
`linkC`/`covShrinkage` "was tuned against the RETIRED residual-history R estimator and is
stale."

## Planner decisions

**P-1 — The patch REPLACES the per-season promoted value uniformly for every season.** The
promoted values differ per season, and a uniform override is the only way to read the sweep as
ONE axis; a multiplicative scaling would confound the axis with the existing per-season spread.
2022 is unaffected regardless (cold start), so the override is effectively "one reversion for
2023-2026".

**P-2 — Three arms, not one: 0.25 / 0.40 / 0.60.** A single point cannot distinguish "monotone
better" from "interior optimum", and would force a second round. 0.40 is EPA parity — the
single most interpretable value on the axis. 0.25 and 0.60 bracket it so the sweep reports a
shape, not a point.

**P-3 — `carryPriorYearShare` is NOT touched.** It is the other carry-mean knob and it differs
per season (0.3 vs 0.7996). Moving both at once would make an arm's result unattributable. If
this sweep validates, `carryPriorYearShare` is the natural follow-up, not a co-mover.

**P-4 — Measurement only. Nothing ships from this task.** No `Sigma1Params` field is added, no
`SIGMA1_CODE_VERSION` bump, no promotion, no tuner run. The parameter already exists and is
already searchable; if an arm wins, the OUTPUT of this task is a recommendation to re-tune that
axis, recorded with its numbers. Working-tree-only, reverted after each arm, per the standing
pattern in 260905-jyf/wwt.

**P-5 — Criteria are pre-committed below, before any arm runs.**

## Pre-committed reading criteria

- **C-1 (hypothesis, falsifiable).** On the three carry-active seasons VPR loses (2023, 2025,
  2026), at least one arm must improve winner accuracy vs the promoted baseline. If all three
  arms are WORSE on all three seasons, the hypothesis is FALSIFIED and the task closes negative.
- **C-2 (2024 veto).** Report the best arm's 2024 delta explicitly. 2024 is VPR's only winning
  season; a change that surrenders it is reported as such, not averaged away.
- **C-3 (control).** Every arm's 2022 predictions stream must be sha256-IDENTICAL to the
  baseline's. A difference means the patch leaked outside the carry path and the arm is void.
- **C-4 (the actual goal).** Pooled VPR winner accuracy vs pooled EPA winner accuracy, per arm.
  This is the number the task exists to move.
- **C-5 (Rule A ship-relevance).** Vs the promoted baseline: pooled accuracy up AND pooled Brier
  not worse. Reported for each arm even though nothing ships from this task.
