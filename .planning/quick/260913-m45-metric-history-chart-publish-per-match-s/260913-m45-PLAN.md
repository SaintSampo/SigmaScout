## Design choices (planner, recorded so the executor does not re-decide them)

1. Capture offline in the fold loop, right after `foldPlayed`, through a new read-only
   `SigmaScoutLayer.sigmaFor`. That reading is "after this match's fold and its talent", the same
   "after this match" meaning every other history-row metric has.
2. Merge offline only at the team-season build, AFTER `withHistoryPercentiles`, through an exported
   pure helper `withHistorySigma`. The sigma entry is value only: no percentile (a per-match pool has
   no meaning, and the chart needs no tier) and no spread.
3. Worker: read Sigma at END OF TICK for `realTouchedTeams`, the same instant `touchedMetrics` is read,
   so Total and Sigma on a row always pair from one instant. Carry it separately from
   `touchedMetrics` into `mergeTeamSeasonArtifact`, where only the new history rows use it. The Worker
   fold loop already computes SPR per-match metrics for the talent prior, so a per-match live Total
   and Sigma would be cheap. It would also change live Total semantics for SPR only, so it stays a
   SUMMARY follow-up.
4. The band fill is `var(--color-text-muted)` at a named low opacity, never the accent. The legend is
   a small HTML row under the plot: "Total" and "± Sigma", with swatches driven by the SAME constants
   as the Line and the Area. Its height is one exported constant, also reserved by the Tab skeleton
   through the same predicate (chart-craft.md, "derive coupled geometry").
