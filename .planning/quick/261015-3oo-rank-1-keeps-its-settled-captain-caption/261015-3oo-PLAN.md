## The facts (proof)
With a fixed ranking and k drafted alliances, alliance n's captain is the highest ranked team not yet allied; before its turn at most n minus 1 captains and n minus 1 first picks are allied, so its captain ranks at most 2n minus 1. Hence:
1. Rank 1 always captains alliance 1.
2. A team ranked 2k or lower can never captain (16 or lower at eight alliances).
Declines do not break either: a decline allies nobody. Observed zero captain draws is NOT used as evidence (ranks 9 to 15 can captain rarely).
