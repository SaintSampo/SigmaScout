# 260917-0p3: measure a split-tick chunk before building it — Summary

This summary was written a day late, during the 2026-09-18 stock-take. **No chunk number exists.** The
instrument was built and the bar was set, and the run was never taken. Do not read anything in this
directory as evidence about what a split tick costs.

## What was built

- `71b1b198` What the platform allows for tick splitting, researched against the official docs and
  recorded with citations in `.planning/todos/pending/rp-fold-exceeds-worker-cpu-budget.md`.
- `08376e9b` A `chunk=teams` probe arm in `apps/worker/src/stateProbe.ts` that emulates the consumer
  half of a split tick as its own invocation. It touches D1 zero times, asserted behaviourally by
  equality on the fake database's recorded SQL list. `chunk=event` is recognized and inert. Both are
  off by default, so every earlier arm keeps its meaning.
- `fb143c51` A five-arm rig under `measure/`, the pinned roster and `2026alhu` carried byte-for-byte
  from 260915-t7o, the bar held as data in the rig and evaluated mechanically, and the runbook rows.

The arm also established, by construction and with no run needed, that a real consumer needs no D1
read: three fields the merge reads cannot come from the published event row, and none of the three is
in D1 either.

## Why it was never run, and why it should not be run now

The question was what it would cost to move the twelve per-tick team-artifact writes into a second
invocation. Two things overtook it within a day.

1. **The writes are gone.** 260917-jr4 took the team artifacts off the live tick and 260918-16t
   shipped that in Worker `c9b4642e`. A teams chunk would have nothing to write.
2. **The bar is not measurable.** It is stated in absolute `cpuTime` (p50 at or under 8 ms and mean at
   or under 9 ms). 260918-16t then found that this instrument cannot reproduce an absolute `cpuTime`
   to better than about 5 ms between runs on unchanged code, and set the rule that bars here are
   pre-registered as within-run differences between arms.

## What is left in the tree

The `chunk=teams` arm and its Group 11 tests are still in `stateProbe.ts`, off by default and green.
They go when the probe goes: `mergeTeamSeasonArtifact` survives only as the probe's `allPhaseB`
baseline arm, and neither can be deleted first. The platform research in `71b1b198` stays useful if
splitting Phase A is ever priced, and any such bar must be a within-run difference.
