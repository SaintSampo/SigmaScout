# Quick task 261007-3ik: the Locks menu follows Season start, and the drawers drop their implicit rows

<objective>
Three things on the Locks page (District Locks and Champ Locks tabs), all in apps/web, no Worker,
pipeline or artifact change.

R1, THE MENU DOES NOT FOLLOW SEASON START (reproduced by the orchestrator against live 2026pnw on
both tabs). The picker keeps a local record of which event the menu shows, and by the sketch 024
design Season start and Live never touch it. On a finished district the menu sits on the LAST event
at Live (Auburn on District Locks, DCMP on Champ Locks); pressing Season start presses the pill,
hollows every dot and the Next text reads "Next: Oregon State Fair · Schedule released", while the
menu still says Auburn or DCMP. Jacob reads that as "the event does not update". Fix: one pure rule,
`milestoneFocusKey(model, selection)`, computed on every render, and no local state in the picker at
all.

R2, THE TWO IMPLICIT ROWS GO (Jacob, 2026-10-07: "when you click a team's playoff blue box or awards
blue box on the locks page, in the info that drops down, remove two rows: 'Out before the top four'
and 'No award'. These are implicit."). The headline and both tiles keep reading the hidden row; only
the rendered list loses it. The Alliance selection list's Not selected row is out of scope and stays.

R3, THE SWEEP. Jacob called the picker sloppy and asked for other trivial bugs to be fixed. Scope:
the files this plan touches. Fix anything trivially wrong found while editing them and record what
was looked at and found, "nothing further" included, in the SUMMARY text. Do not widen to other
pages. One finding is already made at planning time and is Task 3: an alliance placed fifth to
eighth while the bracket is still running leaves its Playoffs cell open with exactly one outcome
row, the implicit one, so R2 alone would hand the drawer an empty labelled list.

Tracer-first is waived for this plan: these are two independent defects in shipped, proven code,
and each task is already its own vertical slice (pure rule, the component that renders it, and the
