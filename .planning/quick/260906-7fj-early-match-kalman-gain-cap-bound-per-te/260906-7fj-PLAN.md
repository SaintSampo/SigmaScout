# Quick task 260906-7fj: Early-match Kalman gain cap

## Motivation

Successor to 260906-6zc, which closed the last open half of the season-boundary carry axis.
With Stage 1-3 and 260905-wwt having closed the carry-VARIANCE half, and 6zc the carry-MEAN half,
nothing about the boundary is left to try — yet VPR still trails EPA by 0.20pt pooled winner
accuracy (0.7648 vs 0.7669 on the 83,655-match intersection population).

6zc also relocated the problem. Its decisive column was `early_acc`, which degraded FASTEST as
the carried mean was shrunk — so VPR's carried mean is informative and UNDER-used, not
over-dispersed. The over-confidence signature the autopsy measured (VPR more confident on
disagreements, mean `|p-0.5|` 0.065 vs EPA's 0.039, while LOSING them; VPR losing the blowout
slice 56.1/43.9) therefore does not originate at the boundary.

`reports/autopsy-260905/FINDINGS.md`'s green-team slices say where it does originate:

| slice | n | EPA share of disagreements won |
|---|---|---|
| Someone's very 1st match | 247 | **48.6% (VPR wins)** |
| Greenest team on field: 1-5 prior matches | 1,850 | **53.6% (EPA wins)** |
| Greenest 6-15 | 2,086 | 52.1% |
| Greenest 16+ | 1,246 | 50.6% (edge gone) |

**VPR wins a team's very first match and then loses matches 1-5.** The cold-start SEED is fine;
the first few UPDATES are where the rating goes wrong. That is a within-season gain problem.

## The mechanism

`updateAllianceSum` (`packages/core/algorithms/sigma1/kalman.ts`) applies
`K_j = P_j / (Sum P_i + R)`, unbounded in [0, 1]. `mean_j += K_j * innovation`.

A team is seeded at the full cold-start belief variance, and `coldStartConsistencyVarianceRel`
is 0.0163 (2023/2024) to 0.0321 (2025/2026) against a `minConsistencyVarianceRel` floor of
0.00097 — roughly 17-33x the floor. So for a green team among two well-observed teammates,
`P_green` dominates the pooled variance and `K_green` approaches 1: that team absorbs nearly the
ENTIRE alliance innovation, and one match very nearly overwrites its rating.

EPA structurally cannot do this. Its `percent_func` learning rate is bounded and floors at 0.2,
so no single match can dominate a team's estimate.

Over-dispersed ratings in the 1-5-match window is exactly what produces confident-but-wrong
predictions and lost blowout disagreements.

**Why the alliance-sum form makes this unusually well-targeted.** `K_j` is a SHARE of the pooled
variance, so a cap binds only when one team's `P_j` dominates its teammates' combined `P` plus
`R` — i.e. exactly one green robot on an otherwise-known alliance. Three green teams together
each get `K ~ 0.33` and are untouched by a cap of 0.5; three veterans each get a small `K` and
are untouched too. The intervention is self-limiting to the slice the autopsy named, with no
window parameter and no match counter.

## Interface facts (verified, this session)

**Two gain sites, and they must move together.** `updateAllianceSum` (`kalman.ts:117`) computes
the gain for the BELIEF update. `componentGains` (`index.ts:490`) recomputes the identical
formula to attribute a per-team residual (`residualsByTeam`, consumed by `covariance.ts` and the
consistency estimator). Capping one and not the other would make the attributed residual
disagree with the update the filter actually applied.

**`updateAllianceSum` is shared with the RP path.** `rp/state.ts:337` calls it for the RP
threshold variables. The cap must NOT reach there: D-01's objective is Brier over the predicted
WIN PROBABILITY, which is structurally blind to the RP pmf, so capping RP could not move the
measured numbers but WOULD make an observed effect unattributable to the score side. Handled by
giving both functions an OPTIONAL `maxGain` that only the score-side call sites pass.

**Inert at default, provably.** `K_j = P_j / (Sum P_i + R) <= 1` whenever every `P >= 0` and
`R >= 0`, so `Math.min(K_j, 1)` returns `K_j` bitwise unchanged in IEEE-754. `maxGain = 1` is a
true no-op, not an approximate one. The RP call site, passing nothing, defaults to 1.

**2022 is still the free control.** `isColdStart: index === 0` means 2022 carries nothing, but
UNLIKE 6zc the gain cap is a within-season mechanism and DOES act in 2022. So 2022 is NOT a
byte-identical control here — it is a fourth live season. This is a deliberate difference from
6zc's C-3 and is why C-3 is restated below rather than reused.

**Baselines reused, never re-replayed:** `reports/rpnoise-baseline-260905` (vpr, promoted pin)
and `reports/autopsy-260905` (epa rows only). Anchors: 83,655 scored, per-season epa/vpr gaps
-0.33 / -0.59 / +1.25 / -1.06 / -0.27 pt.

## Planner decisions

**P-1 — One knob, no match counter.** The cap is applied unconditionally rather than gated on a
team's match count. Per the mechanism above it is already self-limiting to high-variance teams,
and a window parameter would add a second axis that makes any result unattributable to the cap
itself.

**P-2 — Arms 0.60 / 0.45 / 0.30.** 0.60 binds only for a strongly dominant green team; 0.45 for
a moderately dominant one; 0.30 binds even in the equal-variance case (three equal teammates
each sit at ~0.33), making it a deliberately BROAD cap rather than a green-team-only one. The
three points therefore report not just a magnitude but whether the effect is specific to the
green-team case or general.

**P-3 — The cap applies to the score components only.** RP threshold variables keep the
uncapped gain. See interface facts.

**P-4 — Measurement only. Nothing ships.** No `Sigma1Params` field, no `SIGMA1_CODE_VERSION`
bump, no promotion, no tuner run. If an arm wins, the output is a recommendation to add
`maxTeamKalmanGain` as an inert-at-default parameter and tune it — not a shipped change.

**P-5 — Criteria pre-committed below, before any arm runs.**

## Pre-committed reading criteria

- **C-1 (hypothesis, falsifiable).** At least one arm must improve pooled winner accuracy vs the
  promoted baseline. If all three arms are worse pooled, the hypothesis is FALSIFIED and the task
  closes negative — the same standard 6zc was held to.
- **C-2 (the mechanism must show where it was predicted).** The `early_acc` slice must improve at
  least as much as full-set accuracy for any arm that wins overall. An arm that improves pooled
  accuracy WITHOUT improving the early slice did not work for the stated reason, and is reported
  as unexplained rather than as a confirmation.
- **C-3 (no season is sacrificed).** Report every season's delta. 2024 is live here (not a
  control) — a change that surrenders VPR's only winning season is reported as such.
- **C-4 (the actual goal).** Pooled VPR winner accuracy vs pooled EPA winner accuracy, per arm.
- **C-5 (Rule A ship-relevance).** Vs the promoted baseline: pooled accuracy up AND pooled Brier
  not worse.
