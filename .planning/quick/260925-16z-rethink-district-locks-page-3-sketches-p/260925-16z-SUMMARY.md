# Quick task 260925-16z: rethink the district locks page

**Three sketches published, a histogram is feasible for all four categories, and the work belongs
in the browser plus a small pipeline lookup, with nothing new in the Cloudflare Worker.**

## What was built

`.planning/sketches/021-district-points-ledger/` on real `v1/district/2026pnw.json`, all eight
district events played, 126 teams:

- **A: Ledger table.** Two rows per team, four category cells, event total, grand total, status
  chip. Blue cells open a drawer under the team with the cell's histogram beside the grand total.
- **B: Team cards.** The eight cells as chips, each event a bar on one 0 to 83 scale (grey earned,
  blue 10th to 90th band, median tick), grand total bar with today's line. Phone friendly.
- **C: Histograms in the cell.** A with every open cell drawing its histogram inline on the
  column's fixed scale.

Shared: a "Rewind to" slider (week by stage in the sketch, by match on the real page), five status
chips as filters with live counts, the locks rule recomputed at every position.

## Feasibility (full text in the README)

- **Qualification:** the rank histogram the site already simulates, pushed through the fixed
  rank to points formula. Nothing new.
- **Alliance selection and playoffs:** simulated inside the same Monte Carlo run as ranks (top
  eight are captains, greedy pick by SPR to be measured against `event_alliances`, a 13-match
  double elimination priced by SPR alliance means and variances). One joint draw per run, so the
  three on-field categories stay coupled; independent pmfs convolved would overstate the spread.
- **Awards:** publish calibration tables for the sort the methodology page found, not a model:
  P(Impact | position in the most-decorated ordering), P(Rookie All Star | position in the
  rookie ordering), base rates by decoration bucket for Engineering Inspiration and the 5-point
  judged awards. Calibrated by construction, never names a winner, never feeds `locks.ts`.
- **Totals:** event total is the per-run sum; grand total is the exact convolution of the two
  events plus rookie bonus.

## Placement

- **Pipeline:** award tables per season, selection model parameters, baked per-team marginals for
  unstarted events on the presim sidecar (about 60 numbers per team per event).
- **Browser Web Worker:** every event in progress and every slider move, from the event artifact
  plus the embedded SPR state block, exactly as the Simulation tab already does.
- **Cloudflare Worker:** nothing new. The tick folds and republishes; it does not simulate.

## Verification

Headless Chromium (`apps/web`'s Playwright) rendered all three variants with zero page errors.
At slider position "Now" the sketch's recomputed statuses match the live artifact exactly:
50 locked, 76 out of range. Screenshots were reviewed once per variant and four fixes applied
(cell label wrap, chip truncation, a dropped "at or above the line" percentage, the award slot
rule).
