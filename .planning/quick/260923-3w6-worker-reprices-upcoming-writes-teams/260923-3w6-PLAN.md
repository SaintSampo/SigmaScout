# The Worker prices upcoming matches and writes team artifacts again

Jacob decided on 2026-09-23, on `260923-1tu-FINDINGS.md` items C1 and C5, to reverse the three
CPU-budget workarounds of 260915-isq (browser pricing from a `state` block), 260917-jr4 (no team
artifact writes) and 260918-16t (an ephemeral `live` block plus browser overlays). The account is on
Workers Paid since 2026-09-22: 30 s CPU per cron tick instead of 10 ms.

This task is the **Worker and publisher half**. The web half — deleting `eventPricing.ts`,
`eventPricing.lazy.ts`, the team-season overlay, the derived standings and the live-roster
discovery — is the next quick task, **260923-3w7**. So every schema the web imports stays in place,
marked deprecated; this task only stops PRODUCING the two blocks.

**Published numbers must not change.** Upcoming rows the Worker writes must be byte-identical to
what `priceUpcomingFromState` and the offline publisher produce from the same state. The mechanism
is a single extracted pure pricer that all three paths call.

## Task list

1. **`refactor`: extract the pure upcoming pricer.** New browser-safe
   `packages/harness/upcomingPricing.ts` holding `ScheduledMatchInput`, `UpcomingPricingModel`,
   `PriceUpcomingResult` and `priceUpcomingRows` — the body of `priceUpcomingFromState`'s loop,
   parameterized by an in-memory model (algorithm module, state, Sigma Scores, RP accumulator, mean
   shift, rule module) instead of an `EventStateBlock`. `eventStatePricing.ts`'s
   `priceUpcomingFromState` becomes the block-deserializing wrapper and re-exports the moved types,
   so the web compiles unchanged. Pure refactor: `eventStatePricing.parity.test.ts` is the proof.

2. **`feat`: the Worker prices upcoming matches in Phase B.**
   - Phase A's per-algorithm read widens from the touched teams to the touched teams PLUS every team
     on a still-upcoming match (`readScopedStateChunked` in `stateStore.ts`, chunked at
     `MAX_SCOPE_KEYS_PER_READ` for D1's bound-parameter limit; the old "chunked reads deliberately
     not implemented" comment was a subrequest argument and goes).
   - Phase A hands Phase B an `UpcomingPricingModel` built from its own post-fold accumulators.
   - Phase B prices every upcoming match through `priceUpcomingRows` and writes the publisher's
     priced rows into the event artifact's `upcoming`, replacing `buildEventScheduledRow`.
   - `apps/worker/test/scheduled.rp.test.ts`'s state-block harness is repurposed as the equivalence
     proof: the Worker's OWN upcoming rows must `toEqual` `sbOfflineAt(k).upcoming` after each tick.

3. **`refactor`: stop emitting the `state` block.** `maintainedStateBlock`, the Phase B completion
   read, `spliceEventStateBlock`, `completeEventStateBlock`, `missingStateBlockKeys` and
   `buildEventStateBlock`'s call in `publish.ts` (plus the `state blocks:` log line) all go.
   `buildEventStateBlock`, `EventStateBlockError`, `priceUpcomingFromState` and
   `stateBlockScopeKeys` stay for the web and for the parity tests, marked for 260923-3w7.

4. **`feat`: reinstate per-team artifact writes.** `readExistingTeam` returns;
   `mergeTeamSeasonArtifact` gains the team's priced upcoming rows and drops `matchIndexByKey`
   (a metric-history row's `matchIndex` becomes its own array position, which is the only thing any
   consumer reads). Team writes stay unconditional; only the `teams/{year}` feed stays gated on
   `isOfficialEventType`.

5. **`refactor`: stop emitting the `live` block and the live roster.** `buildTickLiveRows`,
   `maintainedLiveBlock`, `mergeEventLiveBlock`'s Worker call and `writeLiveRosterObject`'s Worker
   call go. `packages/harness/liveEventRows.ts` and `liveRoster.ts` stay — the web still imports
   both until 260923-3w7.

6. **`refactor`: delete the dead browser-pricing and browser-relay scripts.**
   `localPricingFixture`, `measureReplayParity`, `replayParityMirror`, `browserFoldEntry`,
   `measureEngineDeterminism`, `measureBoundedDrift` and their tests.

7. **`docs`: `docs/worker-operations.md` and `docs/publish-budget.md`.** The "Live events need no
   operator" table, the 260915-isq section, the `event-state-block-*` warnings, and the per-team R2
   write volume.
