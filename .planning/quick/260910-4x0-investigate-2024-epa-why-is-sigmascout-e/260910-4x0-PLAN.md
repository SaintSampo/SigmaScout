# Quick Task 260910-4x0 — Why is SigmaScout's 2024 EPA so much worse than Statbotics' 2024 EPA?

## Question

The published 2024 row shows SigmaScout EPA at **0.7348 winner accuracy / 0.2179 Brier**
against Statbotics' **0.7627 / 0.1620**. The Brier gap is the eye-catching one: our EPA's
2024 Brier is worse than our own OPR baseline's (0.2014), despite EPA being ~8 accuracy
points better than OPR. Find the cause.

## Method

Read-only investigation. No production code changed. Scratch scripts live in
`experiments/260910-4x0/` (untracked).

1. Establish ground truth from the live published artifacts (`v1/compare/{year}.json`,
   `v1/methodology/epa-vs-statbotics.json`) and `data/statbotics-cache.json`.
2. Separate the two failures — winner accuracy depends only on `sign(margin)`, Brier
   depends on the probability scale. They cannot share a cause.
3. Test each candidate by measurement, not correlation.
