# 260910-4x0 — Why 2024 EPA trails Statbotics: two independent defects

## Answer

Two unrelated defects, and **2024 is the worst-hit season for both**.

| | Ours (published) | Statbotics | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Winner accuracy | 0.7348 | 0.7627 | **-2.79 pp** |
| Brier | 0.2179 | 0.1620 | **+0.0559** |

1. **Brier** — the win-probability denominator pools every season since 2016, flattening
   2024's logistic by ~3.9x. Fixing it recovers **75%** of the Brier gap.
2. **Accuracy** — 2024's component map is the most granular of any season (13 components).
   Collapsing it to Statbotics' granularity recovers **62%** of the accuracy gap.

They are orthogonal: the probability scale can never change `sign(margin)`, so it cannot
move accuracy; component granularity moved accuracy by 1.72 pp and Brier by 0.0001.

## Defect 1 — `allianceScoreStats` is pooled across seasons, not seeded

`packages/core/algorithms/epa.ts:473` divides the predicted margin by an expanding-window
SD of alliance scores, then `carrySeason` (`epa.ts:899`) passes it across the season
boundary as `allianceScoreStats: state.allianceScoreStats`.

That line's doc comment calls this "seeding ... from the prior season's final value".
**It is not a seed.** The whole Welford accumulator carries forward — including its
observation count — so the season boundary is a no-op and the window never resets. A seed
decays as new data arrives; this one cannot, because the count carries too.

Measured on the exact arm `publish:seasons` uses (offseason-inclusive):

| Season | own obs | pooled obs | own SD | pooled SD | ratio | own share of pool |
|--------|---------|-----------|--------|-----------|-------|-------------------|
| 2016 | 31,144 | 31,144 | 34.97 | 34.97 | 1.00 | 100.0% |
| 2017 | 36,662 | 67,806 | 84.52 | 99.46 | 1.18 | 54.1% |
| 2018 | 41,048 | 108,854 | 107.54 | 118.95 | 1.11 | 37.7% |
| 2019 | 43,254 | 152,108 | 19.67 | 124.11 | **6.31** | 28.4% |
| 2020 | 9,474 | 161,582 | 44.67 | 122.23 | 2.74 | 5.9% |
| 2022 | 36,024 | 197,606 | 31.17 | 118.66 | 3.81 | 18.2% |
| 2023 | 40,388 | 237,994 | 38.57 | 110.90 | 2.88 | 17.0% |
| **2024** | 44,198 | 282,192 | **27.20** | **106.43** | **3.91** | **15.7%** |
| 2025 | 47,618 | 329,810 | 53.09 | 100.66 | 1.90 | 14.4% |
| 2026 | 40,816 | 370,626 | 144.59 | 109.03 | 0.75 | 11.0% |

By 2024 the season's own data is 15.7% of the pool, so it cannot move it. 2024 is hit hard
because it has the **second-lowest score SD of any season** while the pool is dominated by
2017/2018's very large scales. 2026 escapes almost entirely (ratio 0.75) — its own scale
happens to sit near the pooled one, which is why 2026 looks healthy and 2024 does not.

**This is the dominant term, and it is measurable across the whole corpus.** Pearson
correlation between the absolute log SD distortion and each season's Brier excess over
Statbotics is **0.930** across all 10 seasons.

**88.7% of all 2024 predictions fall in [0.4, 0.6]**, and one single prediction in the whole

produces.

### Corroboration already sitting in the published artifacts

The methodology page and the Compare page score **the same model on the same 2024 matches**
and disagree on Brier — because `scripts/epaVsStatbotics.ts` starts its replay at 2022 while
`publish:seasons` starts at 2016, so the two carry different pool sizes:

| | 2024 accuracy | 2024 Brier |
|---|---|---|
| `v1/methodology/epa-vs-statbotics.json` (2022-start) | 0.7328 | **0.1862** |
| `v1/compare/2024.json` (2016-start) | 0.7348 | **0.2179** |

Accuracy is materially identical; Brier differs by 0.032 on start year alone. That is the
defect isolated by an accident of the existing tooling.

## Defect 2 — 2024's component map is the most granular of any season

| Season | components |
|--------|-----------|
| 2019, 2022 | 6 |
| 2020, 2025 | 7 |
| 2018, 2023 | 9 |
| 2017 | 10 |
| 2016, 2026 | 11 |
| **2024** | **13** |

2024 keeps `autoAmpNote`, `autoSpeakerNote`, `teleopAmpNote`, `teleopSpeakerNote`,
`teleopSpeakerNoteAmplified`, `endGameOnStage`, `endGamePark`, `endGameHarmony`,
`endGameNoteInTrap` and `endGameSpotLightBonus` as separate per-team EWMAs. Each is
estimated from the same ~12 quals per team, and 13 noisy estimates are summed into every
alliance total — on the season with the second-lowest score variance in the corpus.

This is `docs/models/epa-divergences.md` §6, accepted at the time on the explicit grounds
that "the harness's own accuracy measurement ... is what would surface a granularity choice
that costs accuracy". **It has now surfaced.**

## Experiments

One 2016→2024 replay per arm, offseason-inclusive, scored on 2024 official non-surrogate
decided matches. Baseline reproduces the published slice exactly (n = 16,958 published −
194 ties = 16,764; accuracy 0.7348 to four places), so the variants are trustworthy.

| Arm | accuracy | Brier |
|-----|----------|-------|
| A baseline (as published) | 0.7348 | 0.2204 |
| B per-season score SD | 0.7348 | **0.1764** |
| C fouls out of the margin | 0.7367 | 0.2204 |
| D per-season SD + fouls out | 0.7367 | 0.1760 |
| E collapsed to 5 components | **0.7520** | 0.2205 |
| — Statbotics 2024 | 0.7627 | 0.1620 |

- **B closes 75% of the Brier gap** (0.0440 of 0.0584) and moves accuracy by exactly 0.
- **E closes 62% of the accuracy gap** (+1.72 pp of 2.79) and moves Brier by 0.0001.
- B and E are independent, so both together should land near 0.752 / ~0.176 — and slightly
  better than that, since E's cleaner margins feed B's logistic.

## Ruled out by measurement, not by argument

| Candidate | Test | Result |
|-----------|------|--------|
| Component extraction drops/double-counts points | Parsed components vs actual score, all 10 seasons, 279k alliance-sides | mean error **0.0000**, 0 bad parses |
| `adjustPoints` distortion | Magnitude per season | 2024: nonzero on 0.03% of sides, **0.035%** of scored points |
| Population mismatch vs Statbotics | Scored counts vs Statbotics' documented counts | same population (all official quals + elims) |
| D-04 foul cross-attribution noise | Arm C | **+0.19 pp only** — despite 2024 having the highest foul share of any full modern season (7.55%) |

## Scope beyond 2024 and beyond EPA

- **Defect 1 affects every season except the cold-start one**, worst in 2019 (ratio 6.31,
  which is also the worst accuracy gap at -8.8 pp and the worst Brier at 0.2310). 2019
  deserves its own look — its accuracy deficit is far larger than the scale defect alone can
  explain, since scale cannot move accuracy at all.
- **BPR is structurally immune to defect 1.** Its `predict` derives the scale from per-team
  posterior variances and a learned `logTau` (`packages/core/algorithms/bpr.ts:520-525`),
  never from a pooled score SD. That is why BPR's 2024 Brier is 0.1572 while EPA's is 0.2179.
- **Fixing defect 1 is not a one-liner reset.** `EPA_FALLBACK_SCORE_SD = 25` is close to
  2024's true SD (27.2) but nowhere near 2026's (144.6), so resetting to an empty accumulator
  would damage every season's opening matches. The correct shape is a seed that *decays* —
  prior-season SD at a small effective observation count — which is a model change needing
  its own acceptance run, not a quick fix.

## Not done here

No production code was changed. Both fixes are model changes that move published numbers and
must go through the normal acceptance path. Scratch scripts are in `experiments/260910-4x0/`
(gitignored): `recon.ts`, `fouls.ts`, `comps.ts`, `variants.ts`, `collapse.ts`.
