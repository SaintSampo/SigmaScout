<objective>
Make TBA's own rankings and alliances the published standings and alliance selection of a live
event, fetched by the tick as the event moves through its phases, and demote the counted standings
to a correct gap filler for the minutes before TBA's first rankings response.

Purpose: CONTEXT.md "Official TBA data first" and "Phase awareness". At 2026vari TBA held 21 ranked
teams and 8 alliances all event; the Worker never asked for either, so the page showed a fallback
order, a banner Jacob never wants to see on a real event, and an empty Alliances tab.

Output: two new polls, one pure merge module, the pass built in plan 01 grown to do the work, a
corrected counting rule, and three notice states on the Insights tab.
</objective>

  directly as RP earned. Correct.
