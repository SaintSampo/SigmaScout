<rp_carry_finding>
CONTEXT.md asks the planner to verify, from the code, whether the RP belief carry rule discards
what offseason matches teach before the next season. ANSWER: YES. Nothing about ranking points
crosses a season boundary at all, which is a stronger rule than the one ratings follow. The plan
therefore FOLDS offseason matches into RP state, mirroring ratings, as CONTEXT.md directs.

Evidence:
- `packages/harness/sigmaScoutLayer.ts` lines 63 to 86: `SigmaScoutLayerOptions` has two members,
  `sigmaCarry` and `rpColdPrior`. There is no RP carry option. Lines 125 to 134: the constructor
  builds `new RpMomentsAccumulator(ruleModule, ...)` and `new RpMeanShiftAccumulator(ruleModule)`
  from nothing, every time.
- `packages/harness/publish.ts` lines 2143 to 2153: `publishSeasons` builds one new layer per
  algorithm per season, passing only those two options. Lines 2713 to 2725: the only things handed
  to the next season are `records.carryStates` (level 1) and `layer.sigmaCarryOut()`.
- `packages/core/rankingPoints/meanShift.ts` lines 157 to 160: `fromState` returns a fresh
  accumulator for another season's state. `packages/core/rankingPoints/empiricalMoments.ts` lines
  355 to 359: `#restorePopulation` drops another season's summary, and lines 341 to 349:
  `fromBeliefs` keeps only variable names the season's own rule module declares. So the Worker's
  resume path cannot carry one season's RP state into the next either, and every rebaseline
  replaces the D1 rows.
- `packages/core/algorithms/epa.ts` lines 1307 to 1312: `carrySeason` resets `rpSlotOffsets` and
  `rpLeague`, and line 1398 declares `carryFrom: "last-official-match"`, so EPA's bonus slots are
  both rewound to the last official match and then emptied. `packages/core/algorithms/spr.ts` line
  1048 declares the same `carryFrom`.

ONE CAVEAT the executor must not paper over, and the orchestrator must tell Jacob. The premise in
`eventTypes.ts` and in CONTEXT.md that offseason play follows the official season is not always
true inside a season. The corpus holds `2026isrtp` ("Israel Team Practice", type 99, 2026-04-03,
19 played matches), and 77 official 2026 events play 7,089 matches (5,963 qualification) after its
first match. Ratings already fold it (project memory: "type 99 inside the official season still
folds"). Folding its RP observations too moves the published RP odds of those 77 events by a
little, and the published RP calibration for 2026 with them. That is why SPR is bumped and the
calibration is re-issued in Task 3. In 2017, 22 offseason events precede `2017nhfoc`, which has no
qualification matches, so nothing published moves there. Every other season's first offseason
match is after its last official one.
</rp_carry_finding>
