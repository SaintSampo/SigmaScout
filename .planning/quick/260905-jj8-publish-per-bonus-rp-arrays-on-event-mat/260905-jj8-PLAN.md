   `actualRedBonusRp`/`actualBlueBonusRp` (`z.array(z.boolean()).nullable().optional()`)

   `eventMatchAxis.ts`'s `toRow`; `EventMatchTable.tsx`'s score lines map them through
   `bonusStatesFromProbabilities`/`bonusStatesFromFlags` exactly as
   `team/MatchTable.tsx` does, keeping the `applicable` playoff gate. Stale artifacts
   without the keys keep rendering `unknown` dots — the designed degradation.
