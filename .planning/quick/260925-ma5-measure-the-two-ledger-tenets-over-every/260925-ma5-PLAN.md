# Quick task 260925-ma5: measure the two ledger tenets

Jacob, 2026-09-25:

> "check to make sure a few tenets are always true: No team that ever is displayed as
> 'locked' should ever fail to qualify on points; no team that is ever displayed as 'locked
> out' should ever end up qualifying on points."

The Road to District Champs tab lets a reader rewind the season to any stage and re-reads
every team's status there. A status word is a promise: `Locked` says the slot is already
guaranteed and `Locked out` says it is already gone. Both promises are made from a floor and
a ceiling recomputed at the rewound position, against a `dcmpSlots` capacity and an
award-consumed slot count that are themselves rewound. Neither promise has ever been checked
against what actually happened.

## What gets measured

For every district artifact in `data/local-publish/districts/` (109 published district
seasons, 2016 through 2026, written by 10-06's dry run), at every stage position the tab's
rewind rail can land on, take the status the tab WOULD display for every team and compare it
with that season's own final verdict.

- **Tenet A** — a team displayed `Locked` on points (not the `Locked · award` variant) must
  be inside the final points-qualified set.
- **Tenet B** — a team displayed `Locked out` must be outside it.

**"Qualified on points" is the artifact's own `districtLock.status` at `now`**, and nothing
else: `locked` is qualified on points, `lockedAward` is qualified by an award rather than by
points, `eliminated` is not qualified, and `contending` is a tie at the cut line that
`locks.ts` deliberately refuses to break. Only `locked` enters the set. That definition is
the artifact's own published answer, so the measurement cannot disagree with the site by
having recomputed the finish line differently.

## The wiring is the component's, not a re-derivation

The script calls the SAME modules `DistrictLedger.tsx` calls, in the same order, and defines
no status rule of its own:

1. `districtTierEvents` over `artifact.teams` for the district's own event list, and
   `deriveStageFromState(entry.state).final` for the `now` stage per event.
2. `buildDistrictTimeline({ events, eventArtifacts: new Map() })`. An EMPTY artifact map
   yields exactly the stage positions — season start, then each event's `qualsDone` /
   `alliance` / `playoffs` / `awards`, then now — which is the right sweep set, because a
   within-quals position only loosens both the floor and the ceiling relative to the
   `qualsDone` step that follows it.
3. `districtStageAtPosition(timeline, i, nowStageByEvent)` for the stage at the position.
4. `buildDistrictLedgerRows({ artifact, distributions: empty, stageByEvent: atNow ? undefined
   : stageByEvent })` — the `atNow ? undefined` fallback is the component's own.
5. `computeDistrictLedgerStatuses({ artifact, teams: rows.teams })`.

Those three modules are React free. `districtLedgerStatus.ts` imports only
`packages/core/districts/*`; `districtLedgerRows.ts` and `districtTimeline.ts` reach
`apps/web/src/lib/{liveEvent,simulationInputs,teamKey}.ts`, and none of that chain imports
React or a `.tsx`.

## Tasks

1. `scripts/measureLedgerTenets.ts`, exposed as `pnpm measure:ledger-tenets` (appended at the
   end of the scripts block, no other `package.json` change, no lockfile change). Credential
   free, no network, read-only. Prints a per-season and a total census: positions swept,
   team-positions evaluated, `Locked` shown on points, `Locked · award` shown, `Locked out`
   shown, and the two violation counts. Every violation prints its district, position id,
   team, floor, ceiling, final total and final status. Exit 1 when any violation exists.
2. `scripts/measureLedgerTenets.test.ts`. A pure describe on a tiny hand-built artifact where
   both tenets hold, plus one whose final verdict is deliberately corrupted so the checker is
   proven able to fail; then a corpus-guarded describe (`existsSync` on
   `data/local-publish/districts` plus an explicit `it.skip`) running the real sweep and
   asserting zero violations, with the swept counts pinned as floors.
3. Run it for real; record the full census in the SUMMARY.

If a violation appears, `locks.ts` and the status rule are NOT to be touched: report the rows
and stop.
