<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### What "knocked out" means
- A team whose alliance's bracket placement is DECIDED at the position (`allianceBracketMilestones` returns `{ kind: "decided", placement }` for its alliance, `packages/core/districts/bracket.ts`). Its playoff points are `playoffPoints(season, tier, placement)`.
- A team NOT PICKED once alliance selection is final at the position (the event's Alliances category final, the team on no alliance): its playoff points are 0 and settled.
- Finalists, alliances still alive, and every team before alliance selection closes: unchanged, the Playoffs cell stays open.

### Rendering (both Locks tabs)
- The settled Playoffs cell renders as the existing `final` kind (the grey earned look), following the judging-only precedent in `districtLedgerRows.ts` (~line 1230, `awardOnly`): a per-team settled category while the event's own stage stays open. `row.stage` stays the event's.
- The grey number is the artifact's own `eventPoints.elim` where TBA has published the row with the playoffs final; otherwise the value derived from the decided placement via `playoffPoints`. Document why this is the one place a derived value is allowed to print (a decided placement is a bracket fact, unlike a derived qualification rank; the existing comment at ~1238 explains why qual must never be derived, keep that rule).
- Applies at the district tier (`DistrictLedger` rows) and the DCMP row of the Champ tab alike, at Now and at rewound stops. At a rewound stop the bracket facts come from the as-of played playoff matches already conditioned by `asOfPlayedPlayoffMatchKeys`.

### Lock math (both status modules)
- `districtLedgerStatus.ts` and `champLedgerStatus.ts` must read the per-team settled playoffs: for such a team the floor INCLUDES its settled playoff points and NO elim ceiling is added. The floor is still derived by subtraction from `pointTotal`; be explicit about the live case where `pointTotal` may not yet carry TBA's elim points (then floor = pointTotal - earned.elim + settled value) versus the rewound case where it does.
- The awards ceiling stays open for every team until awards post. The winning-alliance and judged-award reservations (`champReservedSlots.ts`) are unchanged.
- `floorCutLine`, `eliminated`, the In range rank rule, the chance runs and the simulated cutoff line are not redefined; they simply see the tightened inputs.

### Claude's Discretion
- Where the per-team settled map is produced (the simulation result already carries the bracket milestones that feed the Playoffs cell's small line; prefer deriving the settled set in the row builders once and passing it to both status modules over recomputing the bracket in three places).
- Whether the open distribution for a decided team is already a point mass in the bake (it should be, since the simulation conditions on played sets); the cell kind still changes to `final` so the grey look and the lock math follow.
- Test placement: pure tests beside `districtLedgerRows.test.ts`, `districtLedgerStatus.test.ts`, `champLedgerStatus.test.ts`; keep the FNC 2026 case in mind for a fixture (`data/fixtures/phase10/` has 2026pnw; add a minimal synthetic bracket fixture rather than a new large artifact).
- Methodology copy: `districtLedgerContent.ts` says Locked tests the points still available to rivals; add one flat sentence that a team knocked out of the playoffs has its playoff points settled at once. Voice rules apply (third person, no dashes).
