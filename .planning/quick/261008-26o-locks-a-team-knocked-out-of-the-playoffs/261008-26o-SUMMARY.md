# Quick 261008-26o: Settled playoff points for knocked out teams Summary

When an alliance's bracket placement is decided, its teams' Playoffs cells now turn grey on both Locks tabs, at Now and at rewound stops. That placement value replaces the whole 3x Playoffs ceiling in the lock math. TBA's own final number goes into the floor. A placement-table value goes into the ceiling only, because TBA prorates teams that sat out part of the playoffs. On the PNW fixture's FNC-like stop, rewound over the finished DCMP, frc5468 now reads Locked where the blunt rule locked nobody.

## What was built

- **`settledPlayoffPoints`** (districtLedgerRows.ts) runs once per (team, event) in `buildTeam`. It settles a team ONLY when its alliance's milestone is `decided` and the position's Playoffs stage is open. It returns `{ points, exact }`. `exact` is true when the value is the artifact's own `elim` and the event's playoffs are final at Now (every rewound stop over a finished event). Otherwise the value is `playoffPoints(season, tier, placement)` with `exact: false`.
- **The cell:** a `final` grey Playoffs cell at `points`, placed right after the awardOnly branch. `row.stage` stays the event's, and `hasOpenCategory` and the event and grand total logic are untouched. `row.settledElim` is spread into the row only when defined. A sentence beside the existing "qual must never be derived" comment explains why a decided placement is the one exception.
- **`settledElimBounds`** is the one rule all three floor loops apply in place of the Playoffs ceiling: `exact` gives `{ floor: points, ceiling: 0 }`, and not exact gives `{ floor: 0, ceiling: points }`. The earned `elim` still leaves the floor first, as for any open category. All three loops use it: `districtLockBounds`, plus the District points loop and the DCMP loop in champLedgerStatus.ts.
- **Champ rows:** `ChampLedgerSource.settledElim` is copied from the pass rows by a new `sourceOf` helper (in `foldDistrictRow` and `buildDcmpRow`), and spread only when defined. Case 2 re-IDs the dcmp pass cells verbatim, so the grey cell reaches the DCMP row with no rendering change.
- **Methodology:** one new paragraph directly after the pooled Locked paragraph. The 261005-04t pin now reads its two paragraphs at anchor+2, and the new paragraph is pinned at anchor+1.
- **measureChampTenets.ts:** only the header clause changed. The sweep passes no distributions, so it measures the blunt Playoffs ceiling, which is the more conservative side.
- **Todo:** `.planning/todos/pending/locks-settled-playoffs-follow-ups.md`, with five items.

**2. A derived placement value is therefore an upper bound, not a guarantee, and putting it in a team's own floor could show Locked for a team that sits out. The fix is commit 93cabd4d.
