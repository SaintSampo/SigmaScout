# 260915-4p9: Browser pricer for upcoming SPR matches (step 1 of 4)

## Outcome

A browser-safe module, `packages/harness/eventStatePricing.ts`, prices upcoming SPR matches from an
event `state` block. It reproduces the offline publisher's upcoming **event** rows and **team-season**
rows exactly, including the offline unseen-team rule. The block it prices has been round-tripped
through JSON and zod first. Publisher, Worker and web output are unchanged. This is step 1 of the
browser-pricing direction in `todos/pending/rp-fold-exceeds-worker-cpu-budget.md`.

## What was built

- **`EventStateBlockSchema` (in `pageArtifacts.ts`, not yet referenced by `EventArtifactSchema`).**
  - Shape: `{ algorithmId, algorithmVersion, snapshotShapeVersion, rows }`.
  - Rows are verbatim `StateRow`s with `stateJson` as a string, league row first, then team rows by key.
  - Strings let the Worker splice in the D1 rows it just wrote without parsing. A parsed-object
    variant saves only 1.8% gzipped: 8,034 vs 7,892 B on a 49-team fixture event.
- **`buildEventStateBlock` / `priceUpcomingFromState`.** They enforce the block's invariants and throw
  typed errors. The pricer reuses `deserializeState` and the four passenger readers, plus `spr.predict`,
  the Sigma band functions, the RP accumulators with the mean shift, `analyticRpPmf` and `rounding.ts`.
- **`publishedRows.ts`.** The publisher's upcoming event row and team row builders, moved there
  verbatim. The publisher and the pricer now share them.
- **Mirrored in the pricer:** the `enrichUpcoming` band read and the `#rpFieldsFor` logic. They were
  mirrored, not extracted, because `sigmaScoutLayer.bandGuard.test.ts` pins that source text. Exact
  parity plus mutations guard the mirror.
- **`rulesLoader.ts`.** `loadRpRuleModule(season)` loads one season through a dynamic import. It is
  pinned to the same object as `RP_RULE_MODULES[season]`.
- **`seedSql.ts`.** The Node-only seed-SQL emitter, moved out of `stateSnapshot.ts`, which no longer
  imports Node.
- **Import-graph guard.** `eventStatePricing.browserSafe.test.ts` checks the pricer's import graph:
  no `node:` modules, no better-sqlite3, no all-seasons rules map, no `publish.ts`. It has positive
  controls, and it was observed red before the split and green after.

## Parity coverage

The fixture is `packages/harness/fixtures/digest-slice.json`: 2022, 3 regionals, 265 matches. Its last
event's final 12 qualification matches and all its playoffs are held back as upcoming. Cases:

- 12 warm qm matches.
- The full list: 29 event rows and 29 team rows, with and without `sortTime`.
- An unseen team: its own alliance loses the band, the other alliance keeps its band, and the match
  loses RP.
- An RP-ineligible event type.
- Playoffs, including an empty-roster semifinal.
- The mean shift past warmup (non-vacuity proven).
- An independent rounding check.
- Error contracts.
- A demo-team probe.

## Known gaps, carried to later steps

- **Demo-team beliefs (pinned KNOWN GAP).**
  - Sigma and RP beliefs are keyed by the raw demo key, but SPR's team row is the pseudo-team key, so
    those beliefs never reach D1 or the block.
  - The pricer omits the band and RP where offline shows them. This affects offseason schedules only,
    which default publishes exclude.
  - A fix would change D1 seed output.
- **The band rule is per alliance.** A fully seen alliance keeps its band while the match loses RP.
  This mirrors offline exactly; the step 3 UI should expect one-sided bands.
- **The event artifact has no `eventType`.** Step 2 must publish it, in the block or on the artifact,
  or step 3 must read it from the events list.
- **Stale comments.** `apps/worker/wrangler.toml:18-21`, `wrangler.probe.toml:32` and
  `migrations/0001_algorithm_state.sql:9` still say `stateSnapshot.ts` imports Node. Step 2 follow-up.
