# Quick Task 260907-1w7: Unwire the Video column

The Video column is gone from both match tables. Nothing was torn out.

## What changed

`EventMatchTable.tsx` and `team/MatchTable.tsx` each lost the `MatchVideoCell` import, the
`<td data-testid={video-*}>` cell, and the `<th>Video</th>` header. `EVENT_MATCH_TABLE_HEADERS`
dropped `"Video"` and `EVENT_MATCH_TABLE_COLUMN_COUNT` went 7 → 6, which keeps the header row and
the loading skeleton agreeing about the column count. The two
`describe("Video column (quick task 260906-7eu)")` blocks were removed with them (35 lines each).

## What was deliberately kept

- **All of `packages/`** — TBA `videos` at the Zod boundary, `extractVideoKey`, the corpus
  `video_key` column and its additive migration, `selectMatchVideoKeys` and the conditionally
  spread `video` field on the published schemas. Untouched.
- **`MatchVideoCell.tsx`** and its test — still green, simply never mounted.
- **`lib/matchVideo.ts`** and its test — the 11-char YouTube-id allowlist behind a 512-char bound
  that guards the iframe `src`. This is the security-sensitive piece and the one most worth having
  back verbatim.
- **`eventMatchAxis.ts`'s `video` row field** — the artifact-to-view-model carry.
- **`.match-video-*` in `theme.css`.**

Re-enabling is an import, a `<th>`, and a `<td>` in each of the two tables, plus taking the column
count back to 7.

## Deviation

The removal-depth option the user selected listed `.match-video-*` CSS under REMOVED in its
preview. It was kept instead, and this was flagged in the same turn rather than after the fact.
The rules cannot render while nothing mounts `MatchVideoCell`, so deleting them changes nothing
visible, and it would have converted "restore six lines" into "restore six lines plus forty of
CSS" — contradicting the reason the unwire-only option was chosen.
