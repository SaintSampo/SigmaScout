<objective>
Add ONE searchable Sigma1 hyperparameter, `carryEvidenceRate` — EVIDENCE-WEIGHTED season-boundary
variance retention. A returning team that played `n` matches last season is seeded at
`coldStartVariance * exp(-rate * n)` (composed with Stage 2's uniform `carryVarianceFactor` and
floored at `minConsistencyVariance`). Default `0` is exactly today's behaviour. This is Stage 3 of
the carry-variance work; Stage 2 registered the uniform form, and this is the sharper formulation
Stage 2's own recorded disposition asks for.

Purpose: the Stage 2 tune (2026-09-05, recorded in
`.planning/todos/completed/retune-sigma1-rolling-origin.md`) ran `carryVarianceFactor` over five
origins x two arms against the live `vpr@8.0.0+rolling-2026-09b` incumbent and returned TEN
keep-incumbent verdicts. The result is not a flat negative — it has a shape, and that shape is what
this task is built on:

- **2025/on was the strongest challenger ever posted against rolling-2026-09b under the
  accuracy-primary objective**: +0.004070 out-of-sample accuracy against an N=62 bar of 0.004124 —
  missed by 0.005 percentage points — WITH better Brier (-0.002078), at winner factor 0.845. In
  exactly the season the disagreement autopsy predicted (2025 = EPA's largest early-information
  edge). A miss is a miss and nothing shipped, but that is signal, not scatter.
- **2026/on converged on factor exactly 1.0 unaided** — the knob buys nothing there.
- **2022-2024 lost out-of-sample at moderate factors** (winners 0.452-0.520), 2024 worst — the
  prior-trust-hostile season that also burns EPA — while NINE of ten searches independently chose
  sub-1 factors IN-SAMPLE.
- Screen note: `carryVarianceFactor` survived with the second-largest Brier range of all 16 knobs
  (1.038e-2), its one-at-a-time optimum sitting at the default 1.
- DISPOSITION: the knob STAYS SEARCHABLE (unlike elim-R, excluded for scattering directionless),
  but the Stage 2 QUESTION is CLOSED AS MEASURED: a UNIFORM boundary variance factor does not clear
  the D-T7 bar on any origin, and "if the early-season gap is reattacked, the next formulation
  should be sharper than uniform (e.g. evidence-weighted per team), not a re-run of this one."

That last line is this task's entire mandate. And the diagnosis it implies is concrete rather than
vague: a UNIFORM factor hands a 5-match rookie the same trust discount as a 60-match veteran. Nine
of ten searches wanted sub-1 in-sample and most of them then lost out-of-sample, which is the
signature of a knob whose in-sample gain (veterans, correctly trusted more) is being paid for
out-of-sample by its collateral damage (low-evidence teams, wrongly trusted more). An
evidence-weighted rate makes the discount proportional to the evidence that actually justifies it,
and it can express the 2025/on near-accept region for a veteran while leaving a rookie essentially
untouched — a shape the uniform form has no way to write down.

Output: one new schema-defaulted, provably-inert `Sigma1Params` field wired end to end through
`carrySeason`, composing multiplicatively with `carryVarianceFactor`, registered searchable, fully
tested, with the `SIGMA1_CODE_VERSION` non-bump recorded. Implementation and tests only — the
sensitivity screen, joint tune, and any promotion run afterward from the main context and are
explicitly out of scope.
