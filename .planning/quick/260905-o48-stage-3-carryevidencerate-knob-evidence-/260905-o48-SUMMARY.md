# Quick Task 260905-o48: Stage 3 carryEvidenceRate knob (evidence-weighted carry) Summary

One-liner: registered `carryEvidenceRate`, an evidence-weighted per-team season-boundary variance-retention rate (`exp(-rate * outgoingMatchCount)`) that composes multiplicatively with Stage 2's uniform `carryVarianceFactor` in `carrySeason`'s belief-variance seed — schema-defaulted inert at 0, fully wired, fully tested, and registered searchable at `[0, 0.03]`. No tune, screen, or promotion run; implementation and tests only.

## What shipped

**Task 1 — `packages/core/algorithms/sigma1/params.ts`, `index.ts`, `params.test.ts`:**

- Added `readonly carryEvidenceRate: number` to `Sigma1Params`, immediately beside `carryVarianceFactor`, with a doc comment covering what it is, its inertness at 0, its composition with `carryVarianceFactor`, its dimensionless-per-match units (passes through `resolveSigma1Params` unchanged, like `carryVarianceFactor`), its motivation (the Stage 2 tune's numbers), and its evidence input (`matchCount`, never a per-component quantity).
- `DEFAULT_SIGMA1_PARAMS.carryEvidenceRate: 0` — a deliberate inert literal, not derived from an imported constant (mirrors `carryVarianceFactor: 1` directly above it).
- Schema: `carryEvidenceRate: z.number().finite().min(0).max(0.03).default(0)`. The domain is CLOSED at 0 (unlike `carryVarianceFactor`'s open `.positive()`) because 0 is the inert default here; a negative rate would inflate a returning team's seed above the cold-start prior, the exact direction `carryVarianceFactor`'s own `.max(1)` already refuses. No new object-level `.check(...)` — the field has no cross-parameter relationship.
- `index.ts`'s `carrySeason`: computes a per-team evidence factor ONCE, hoisted out of the per-component loop, immediately after the `oldTeamState` read. Sanitises the carried `matchCount` (non-finite -> 0, negative -> 0) before it reaches `Math.exp` — a REAL guard, not ceremony, because unlike `carryVarianceFactor`'s purely multiplicative seed, an exponential can manufacture `NaN` from a finite-looking pipeline and a negative exponent would silently invert the mechanism into inflation. At rate exactly 0 the factor is 1 via an explicit `=== 0` branch. The Stage 2 seed guard is EXTENDED (not rewritten): the untouched-today path now requires `carryVarianceFactor === 1 && carryEvidenceRate === 0`, and the active branch takes the maximum of the floor and the product of BOTH factors in one expression with one clamp.
- `params.test.ts`: new schema round-trip tests, a new state-level probe fixture (returning teams at 5/50/150 outgoing matches plus one carry-worthy-but-non-returning team) covering evidence differentiation, exact value, composition with `carryVarianceFactor`, the no-carried-state branch, the floor, and the `adjust` pin — plus stream-level inertness/wiring/boundary-gate tests that REUSE Stage 2's `carryVarianceObservables`/`swingingObservables` helpers unchanged.

## What was CONFIRMED about `matchCount` (not assumed)

`Sigma1TeamState.matchCount` is declared on the interface (`index.ts` ~line 208), initialised to `0` in `coldStartTeamState`, incremented as `working.matchCount + 1` exactly once per team per `update()` call, and reset to `0` by `carrySeason` itself when it builds `nextTeams`. Those four sites are the complete lifecycle — no fifth writer exists. `oldTeamState.matchCount` read inside `carrySeason` IS the outgoing season's own per-team processed-match count.

Separately, `stateSnapshot.ts`'s `deserializeSigma1State` reads a team row as `JSON.parse(row.stateJson) as SerializedSigma1TeamRow` — a raw TypeScript cast, NEVER run through a Zod schema. `matchCount` is therefore NOT validated on the way back in, only cast. The sanitisation guard added in `carrySeason` is consequently the ONLY thing standing between a corrupted or hand-edited snapshot row and a poisoned season — not defence in depth for a check that happens elsewhere.

## The prior evidence and what would count as success

The Stage 2 uniform tune went ten-for-ten keep-incumbent, but 2025/on came within 0.005 percentage points of the D-T7 bar at factor 0.845 WITH better Brier, and the pattern across origins (nine of ten searches choosing sub-1 in-sample, then 2022-2024 losing out-of-sample) is the signature this evidence-weighted form is aimed at: a uniform factor hands a 5-match rookie the same trust discount as a 60-match veteran, so its in-sample gain on veterans is paid for out-of-sample by collateral damage on low-evidence teams. A keep-incumbent verdict for `carryEvidenceRate` remains a fully plausible outcome and would NOT be a defect in this implementation. The thing to watch in the tune output is whether 2025/on clears the D-T7 bar and whether 2022-2024's out-of-sample losses shrink — that pair, not the winner's rate alone, is the answer to Stage 3's question.
