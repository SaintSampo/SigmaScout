## Planner readings (each the more covering side; they go in the module header's frames section where it says so and in the SUMMARY)

- **P1. Seat groups (D2, Discretion).** The per division unpicked sets reach the proof as `JointLockInput.seatGroups`, one `{ alliances, eligible }` per division. Absent (single, each California championship) the bound behaves as ONE group holding every alliance and every rival on no alliance, which is the shipped loop.
- **P2. Eligibility is read off the status rows.** A team has a row at a division when it has a dcmp source at that division key at the position (the keys of `dcmpSettledByEvent` for that key). It is unpicked there when no alliance of that division's facts holds it among its CONFIRMED picks (alliance selection points above 0 at that key). A team whose only championship row is the finals row, or with no row, is named by no group.
- **P3. A listed pick that is not confirmed is a hindsight fact, on both sides and for T itself (header and SUMMARY).** On an alliance that still lists it as a member (reading R8: an alliance its division has not placed; G2 keeps a placed alliance at its confirmed picks) such a team is paid its alliance's scenario value as a member AND is an eligible unpicked team of its division for a seat or a fill in, at floor plus extra with no alliance value. One rival may then be counted twice, which only raises the bound. And when T is such a team, a frame whose winner lists T is NOT skipped: T may never have been on that alliance, so its win does not qualify T. The proof recognises the case as a team that an alliance lists and a seat group names.
- **P4. A rival that no group names is eligible in EVERY group (plan checker W2; header and SUMMARY).** A pool or slot only rival on no alliance that no group names (no dcmp source at any division key) is offered every division's seats and every candidate winner's fill in, and still counts through a consuming award and through one judged award alone. The proof then never assumes a competing team's row is posted. It may be counted once per group, which only raises the bound. A team an alliance lists and no group names (a confirmed pick) is eligible nowhere. This is wider than CONTEXT D2's sentence and is kept whatever it costs; it cost nothing on the corpus.
- **P5. The winner's seats are fill ins only.** A backup on W is counted as taking a slot whatever its points. There is no seat on W at a points value.
- **P6. The fill in pool** is the eligible rivals of W's own group that are not members of W: pool rivals short of T at floor plus extra, and slot only rivals. For a single championship this equals the shipped unpicked count.
- **P7. Dead fields go.** `JointLockFrame.extraSeats` had one writer, reading R5; it is removed with `fillInsFromAnyRival`, `enumeratedSeatBonus` and `anyRivalSeats`. `DivisionedJointStructure.finalsSpareByAlliance` is renamed `spareByAlliance`: it is the alliance's one seat pool.
- **P8. The judged budget stays one pool shared by the groups** (the 261009-kt3 relaxation: a rival can win only its own division's). The per group covers are combined by the best split of the budget.
- **P9. Reading R11 after the change:** the reachable filter uses the largest seat value over the placement values and every usable frame's fixed values.
- **P10. M4** ("restrict fill ins to rivals already covered") is read as: the fill in pool counts only eligible rivals within reach of a seat or a judged award, the rivals the cover already counts. The literal reading (a fill in adds nothing) is M2's effect at another line and fails the same tests.
