## Readings of D2 and D3 this plan fixes (each is the more conservative one, per the orchestrator's rule)

The planner reproduced D2 on the local 2026fnc artifact before writing this plan (scratch script, not committed), and again after Jacob's 2026-10-09 revision of D2 (one point paying award per rival, placement maxima 90 / 75 / 39 / 21). Readings 1 to 8 and 10 are accepted and locked in CONTEXT D5. Reading 9 is amended for the maxima (its monotonicity argument stated in full), reading 11 is replaced by D2's one award rule, and reading 12 is new in the revision and revised again for CONTEXT D7 (the settled record carries its own ceiling). Every reading below is binding on the executor.

1. **Steps 3b to 6 are counted as the best allocation of their resources, not by D2's greedy order.** D2's literal order undercounts in three constructed cases, which would break D2's own soundness argument:
   - R1, consuming award against fill in: two uncovered rivals at the same need, u1 unpicked and n1 on a decided alliance, C=1, K=0, f=1. Largest need first may spend the consuming award on u1 and leave the fill in nothing; the true maximum covers both (consuming to n1, fill in u1).
   - R2, seat matching: m_T=200, seats worth 75 and 21, unpicked rivals at 180 and 125, C=K=f=0. D2's 3b gives 75 to 180 and 21 to 125 (146, not covered), count 1; 21 to 180 (201) and 75 to 125 (200) covers both.
   - R3, judged award against fill in (one award per rival): unpicked u at m_T minus 5, picked n at m_T minus 10, K=1, f=1, C=0. Ascending deficit spends the one judged award on u and leaves n uncovered (u is then no longer a fill in candidate), count 1; judged to n and the fill in to u covers both.
   The count is therefore: covered by steps 1 to 3, plus `min(|X|, SJ + C + min(f, |X_U|))`, where X is every rival still uncovered, X_U its unpicked part, and SJ the EXACT maximum number of uncovered points rivals the seats and the judged budget can lift to m_T together (a small dynamic program, Task 1). This is an upper bound on every allocation, so it is at least D2's greedy count in every case (tested).
2. **A seat that does not reach m_T still pays its points**, so the holder's judged need falls. The dynamic program covers this.
3. **Slot only rivals.** `pointsRaceSlots` counts a prequalified team's DCMP consuming award (and a winner award) as a consumed slot, so a prequalified team that is not yet award qualified can take a slot from T without passing it on points. Such teams count in step 1 (member of W), step 4 (consuming awards) and step 6 (fill ins), never on points.
4. **U (unpicked) is every pool or slot only rival that is not a member of any alliance**, not only teams in the DCMP field. Members are picks with DCMP alliance selection points above 0 (D2); a pick with no district row is not a member, which raises f and the spare seats.
5. **extra_X is the shipped open ceiling minus exactly the two DCMP pieces the proof models itself**: the DCMP Awards ceiling while Awards are open, and the DCMP Playoffs ceiling while Playoffs are open and the source carries no `settledElim`. Everything else stays in extra: open district tier ceilings, a settled non exact placement's ceiling (its placement MAXIMUM through `SettledPlayoffs.ceiling` and `settledElimBounds`, D7 and reading 12), a hypothetical DCMP.
6. **Alive for the proof** is an alliance the routing has not placed, OR any of whose members' DCMP source lacks `settledElim` while the position's Playoffs are open. No team's playoff points can then fall between its floor and the proof.
7. **Single championship** means exactly one dcmp tier key in the whole district (`dcmpEventKeysFor(artifact).length === 1`). 2026 California's two single event championships do not run the proof (two winning alliances and two award budgets), alongside the divisioned FIM, NE, ON and TX of D1.
8. **Playoffs final.** The "no alive alliance" case (W none, f 0) runs only when the DCMP winner award is already posted at the position, so its holders have left the pool and S'. Playoffs final without the posted award: if the routing decided the final, W is the routed winner (its members covered, f fill ins); otherwise the proof does not run.
9. **Placement assignments** (amended 2026-10-09): every ordered assignment of the top `min(3, |others|)` values of `maxPlayoffPointsByPlacement(year, "dcmp", 2..4)` (75, 39, 21 at 2026) to distinct alive alliances other than W. Assigning fewer or smaller placement values never covers more rivals: any cover the resources achieve at lower points or smaller seat values stays achievable at higher ones, so the maximum cover is monotone in every rival's points and every seat's value, and the bound for an enumerated assignment is at least its maximum cover. Decided placements are always the bottom of the table (fifth to eighth fall in Rounds 2 and 3, fourth in Round 4, third in Round 5, first and second in the final), so the alive alliances other than W share placements 2 and below, each real assignment is dominated by an enumerated one, and the reduced assignment set is sufficient. The Task 1 header states this argument and the exhaustive E1 and E2 tests guard it.
10. **K = 14, not 13.** D3's rule is "the maximum observed across ALL history entries plus a margin of 1". The history runs 2016 to 2026 and eight 2019 championships (chs, fma, fnc, in, isr, ne, pch, pnw) gave 13 fifteen point judged awards; 2023 to 2026 never exceeded 12. Planner measurement over `data/local-publish/districts`, every remainder a whole number.
11. **One point paying award per rival** (replaces the first plan's 3 judged awards per rival, Jacob 2026-10-09): `MAX_POINT_PAYING_AWARDS_PER_TEAM = 1` in `champJointLock.ts`, a module constant and not an input, so the bound's dynamic program reads it in one place. Under it a judged award lifts only a rival within 15 points of m_T (after its seat, if any), and a consuming award recipient takes its slot whatever its points. The count formula of reading 1 already counts each covered rival once, so the cap changes only which rivals the judged budget can reach.
12. **A decided placement whose settled value is not exact pays its MAXIMUM in the proof AND in both status modules' ceilings** (revised for CONTEXT D7, 2026-10-09). `settledPlayoffPoints` prints `playoffPoints`, 60 for second place, while the event's Playoffs are open at Now; a losing finalist that won one Finals match is paid 75. So `SettledPlayoffs` gains a `ceiling` field: for a non exact value it is `maxPlayoffPointsByPlacement(season, tier, milestone.placement)` (75 for second place at a DCMP, 25 at a district event), for an exact value it is 0; `points` is unchanged and is what the Playoffs cell prints. `settledElimBounds` returns `{ floor: 0, ceiling: settled.ceiling }` for a non exact value and `{ floor: settled.points, ceiling: 0 }` for an exact one, so the shipped ceiling test on the Champ Locks tab and the District Locks tab (`districtLedgerStatus.ts`, through the shared function, with no edit to that file) and the proof's extra (reading 5) all read the maximum. A placed team therefore needs no top up in the proof. The one remaining top up is a team with a non exact `settledElim` and NO routed placement in the proof's own routing of the facts: it gets the whole DCMP Playoffs ceiling (`maxEventPoints(year, "dcmp").elim`) minus the `settled.ceiling` already in its extra, floored at 0, so its total is the whole ceiling. Today the maxima differ from the table only for second place, and only live (every rewound stop over a finished event is exact). What the cell PRINTS stays the display question, todo 5a of 261008-26o.
