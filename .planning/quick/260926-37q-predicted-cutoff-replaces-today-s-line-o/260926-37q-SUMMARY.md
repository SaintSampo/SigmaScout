# Predicted cutoff replaces the line — summary

Five commits, `7f4a5e9c` through `e6412f2f`. Both Locks tabs now print ONE computed value where they previously printed two different quantities, neither of which sat between the teams the tab had just called In range and the teams it had just called Out of range. ## Task 1 — the predicted cutoff rule, pinned end to end against the fixture

Commit `7f4a5e9c`. `apps/web/src/components/districts/predictedCutoff.ts` is the one pure module both tabs call: no React, no Worker type, no lock rule of its own. `pointsRaceSlots` owns the pool and the slot count and `pointPercentiles` owns the percentile convention. `predictedCutoff({teams, capacity, qualifiers, reservedSlots})` returns a four-arm union (`capacityUnknown`, `absent`, `predicted`, `final`), the last two carrying `points` and the `boundary: {above, below}` pair they came from. It reads `pointsSlots`, never `lockSlots`, on `cutLinePointsWithQualifiers`' own stated reason. `simulatedCutoffRange(cutoffByRun, draws)` fills a counting histogram over the integer run cutoffs and calls the shipped `pointPercentiles`. `CutoffRankingTeam` is structural, so both tabs' team types satisfy it with no adapter.

The test is 19 pins against `data/fixtures/phase10/district-2026pnw.json` at an all-final position. Champ tab: `pointsSlots` 13, boundary pair 182 and 182, cutoff 182, equal to `insights.cmpCutLinePoints`. District tab: `pointsSlots` 42 over a pool of 118, boundary pair 60 and 57, cutoff 59, and explicitly NOT `insights.dcmpCutLinePoints` (82): the published insight ranks the all-tier `pointTotal` while this tab's grand total is district tier only, so the two describe different races. The between property is swept over 240 seeded random integer pools under BOTH In range rules, plus a second 240-pool sweep on continuous medians. The three refusals are pinned, as is that a reservation never moves the line.

## Deviations

1. Strict betweenness is impossible for two adjacent integers, so the sweep pins inclusive betweenness unconditionally and the strict form only where the boundary pair differs by 2 or more.
2. `midpointOf` clamps the rounded midpoint into the boundary pair whenever a whole number fits there, so a continuous pair like 59.6 and 60.4 cannot round outside its own interval.
3. `simulatedCutoffRange` also refuses a `draws` that disagrees with the array's length.
4. The likely range is refused entirely where the chance run excluded teams (task 5). Found by measurement, not by the plan. No dedicated automated pin; the three stat-line component tests assert the range's absence in their own arms only.
5. `ledgerCutoffDisplay` was added to `LedgerParts.tsx` as the single derivation of the label, the figure, the range, the marked position and the caption.
6. The structured-clone test asserts the object brand rather than `instanceof`: jsdom clones the typed array into another realm.
7. The e2e spec's retired-phrase literal is assembled from `String.fromCharCode`, because typing it would trip the gate grep.
8. `DISTRICT_CATEGORIES` left `districtLedgerRows.test.ts`'s imports with the stat-line tests that were its only user.
9. The methodology paragraph is three sentences rather than four, to keep its own voice test's cap.
10. `DistrictLedger.test.tsx`'s single stat-line test became three: settled, unpublished capacity, and the predicted arm on the open-cells fixture.
11. No screenshot carries a rendered likely range; no position reachable from the 2026-09-14 fixture produces one (settled at Now, excluded teams when rewound). Recorded in the README rather than faked.
