## The gaps to close (from Jacob's screenshot against the sketch)

1. **Cells are bare text; the sketch draws boxes.** Every category cell, event total and grand
   total is a filled box, centred, with a small radius: a FINAL cell is a grey filled box with a
   bold number in the muted ink (`.cell.fin` in the sketch: light grey fill, no ring); an OPEN
   cell is a sky-tinted filled box with an inset sky ring, the bold figure on the first line and
   the small label ("likely 19.9–22.3", "~30 if in") beneath, both centred, the whole box a
   button with a hover tint and a stronger ring when its drawer is open. The grand total box is
   taller (it spans the team's rows) with a slightly larger figure. Columns are centred, not
   right aligned. Use existing tokens only (the grey fill from the inset background token, the
   sky fill and ring from the locked-award status tokens or the tier "rare" sky tokens, muted
   ink for final numbers); no literal colours; `cn()` must not sit beside a `text-[var(...)]`
   role class.
2. **The controls card is three times taller than the sketch's.** Match the sketch's density:
   the "REWIND TO" label, the position readout and the hint on ONE line (label small caps,
   readout bold, hint muted right-aligned); the slider directly beneath with the tick labels
   under it (start, one per week, now) as small faint text; the jump chips as small pill
   buttons in one wrapping row directly under the ticks; the one-sentence rewind note in muted
   13px; the status chips row with the counts inside each chip and the cell key inline on the
   same wrapping row ("earned, final" swatch, "still open · click for the histogram" swatch,
   "likely = 8 of 10 runs land here · ~ = typical amount when it happens"); the five
   definitions as ONE wrapping row of small muted text with the status word in bold ink, not
   five separate lines; the team number search and the stat line (today's line, open cells) on
   one row. Card padding and gaps at the site's small spacing tokens, not large ones.
3. **Event cell.** Event name on the first line (drop the "FNC District" prefix only if the
   artifact's `eventName` carries it verbatim and the sketch's short form is derivable; if not,
   keep the full name), "wk N" as a faint small suffix, and the stage word ("final", "quals",
   "alliances picked", "playoffs done", "not started") as a faint small SECOND line, as the
   sketch does. Muted ink for the name.
4. **Team cell.** Bold team number (larger, as the sketch), the nickname beneath in muted small
   text truncated, then "#position · N earned" with " · median M" only when the team has an
   open category, in muted small text. Keep the width cap from cf809985.
5. **Rows.** Hairline separators: a light edge above each team's first row, a dashed hairline
   between a team's rows, no heavy rules. Keep the sticky first column and the shipped
   scroll-region wrapper untouched.
6. **Column headers** stay the site's uppercase letterspaced style; centre the numeric ones.
7. **Drawer** stays as is.
