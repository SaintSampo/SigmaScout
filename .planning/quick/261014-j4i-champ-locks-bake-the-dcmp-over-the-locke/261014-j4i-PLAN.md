The championship key reaches the artifact only through a team's dcmp tier row, so every Now window has at least one registered team. Task 2 lets such a team read the bake where the bake holds a record for it or is still pending, and keeps the estimate where the simulated field leaves it out (Locked out, Out of range or a refused field would contradict its registration).
