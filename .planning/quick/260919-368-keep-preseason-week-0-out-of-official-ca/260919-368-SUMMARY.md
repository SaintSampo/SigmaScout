# 260919-368: preseason Week 0 out of official ratings, predictions and accuracy — Summary

Jacob's rule (2026-09-14): unofficial Week 0 play must not feed an official calculation. It did, in
two ways, because the rating and scoring paths filtered on the corpus `is_offseason` column, which is
derived from event type 99 alone, while Week 0 is type 100.

## What was wrong, confirmed against the corpus before any change

Type 100 played matches per season: 16, 11, 32, 29, 29, 32, 37, 52, 31, 31 (2016 to 2020, 2022 to
2026). All but `2024nywz` (21 matches, during week 1) sort before the season's first official match.
They were folded through `predict` and `update` in every stream, so they moved EPA, SPR, OPR, Sigma
and ranking-point state before official week 1, and they were scored as official accuracy.

## What changed

1. **Scoring and loaders (`38a78c3e`).** `OFFICIAL_EVENT_SQL` in `packages/corpus/db.ts` is the SQL
   form of `isOfficialEventType`, built from the same two constants, and every `excludeOffseason`
   loader uses it. The publisher's scored-set flag reads `isOfficialEventType`.
2. **The no-fold rule (`ccdd3378`).** `foldsIntoRatings(eventType)` in `eventTypes.ts` is true for
   every type except 100. It is deliberately not `isOfficialEventType`: offseason play folds because
   `carryFrom: "last-official-match"` discards what it taught before the next season reads it, and
   nothing discards what preseason teaches, because preseason comes first. Gated inside each
   algorithm's `update` and inside `SigmaScoreAccumulator.foldMatch`, so no caller can bypass it, and
   at the three folds with no gate of their own (talent, RP mean shift, RP threshold fold) in
   `SigmaScoutLayer.foldPlayed`, the Worker tick and its probe mirror. A Week 0 match keeps its
   prediction, band and RP odds.
3. **Versions (`d64177d1`).** opr 5.0.0, epa 11.0.0, spr 6.0.0. The level-1 digest moved its version
   strings only; every recorded sha256 still reproduces bitwise, because that slice holds no Week 0
   match.
4. **The RP scorecard (`de54a3c9`).** Preseason events are RP-eligible, so they were in the published
   ranking-point scorecard. It now scores official play only and was re-measured as `-09f`.

## The measurement (`scripts/captureCompareSlices.ts`, method committed first in `2e11b76f`)

The real `publishSeasons` with `dryRun`, keeping the Compare page slices, so every arm is scored by
the published scorer. Arm A reproduced all nine live 2026 slices bit for bit before anything changed.
Three arms from three commits, qualification view, pooled over ten seasons.

**A to B, bookkeeping.** 159 Week 0 qualification matches leave the scored set (125,581 to 125,422).
Pooled accuracy rises by 0.00017 (EPA), 0.00027 (OPR), 0.00018 (SPR).

**B to C, the model effect, on the identical 125,422 matches.**

| | accuracy | Brier | seasons better / worse on accuracy | on Brier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SPR | 0.75580 to 0.75631 | 0.16176 to 0.16144 | 7 / 3 | 9 / 1 |
| EPA | 0.75127 to 0.75168 | 0.16993 to 0.16977 | 8 / 1 | 8 / 2 |
| OPR | unchanged in all ten | 0.20982 to 0.20984 | 0 / 0 | 3 / 7 |

Regressions as found: SPR accuracy in 2016 (-0.00075), 2017 (-0.00056) and 2022 (-0.00025), and SPR
Brier in 2016; EPA accuracy in 2022 (-0.00127) and EPA Brier in 2022 and 2026; OPR Brier in seven
seasons, by hundred-thousandths, with no winner call changed.

**The RP scorecard**, measured apart for the same reason (`docs/models/rp-layer-config-arms.md`): on
the identical population the no-fold rule is marginally WORSE (RPS 0.142366 to 0.142452, outcome
Brier 0.354918 to 0.355358). That measurement replays each season cold, so Week 0 was its only
warm-up. Restricting it to official play removes 298 total-RP and 170 outcome observations.

## Found along the way

- **Two Worker test stubs omitted the event detail's required `name` field**, so the parse threw and
  every tick in `liveAlgorithmTier.test.ts` and `scheduled.replay.test.ts` ran degraded at event type
  -1, including the replay-equivalence test that declares type 0. The new preseason test exposed it
  by seeing SPR's league scale step to 95. Both fixed; both files pass at their real types.
- Arm B is not perfectly prediction-neutral: the cold-start index is built through the same loader,
  so a few cold-start stamps move (270 to 267 SPR qualification exclusions). Too small to show in any
  pooled figure, recorded so the claim is not overstated.

## Known consequences, accepted

- Under OPR a preseason event page shows no team ratings and its live tick writes no metric rows:
  OPR is event-scoped and starts each season empty. EPA and SPR show carried ratings, flat across the
  event.
- **Out of scope, and still true:** type 99 play inside the official season still folds.
  `2026isrtp` (19 matches, 2026-04-03) is the one clear case; the large 2017 and 2026 counts are one
  late official event after the offseason had begun. Offseason folding was a separate, closed
  decision and was not reopened here.
