# 260919-368: keep preseason Week 0 play out of official ratings, predictions and accuracy

## The rule

Jacob, 2026-09-14: unofficial Week 0 data must not be used for official play calculations. TBA
`event_type` 100 is preseason. `isOfficialEventType` already excludes it, but the rating and scoring
paths read the corpus's `is_offseason` column instead, which is derived from type 99 only.

## Confirmed before changing anything (2026-09-19, `data/corpus.sqlite`)

Type 100 played matches per season: 16, 11, 32, 29, 29, 32, 37, 52, 31, 31 for 2016 to 2020 and 2022
to 2026. Every one sorts before that season's first official match except `2024nywz` (21 matches,
2024-03-03, during week 1). They are replayed through `predict` and `update` in every stream, so they
move EPA, SPR, OPR, Sigma and ranking-point state before official week 1, and they are scored.

Type 99 is OUT OF SCOPE here and deliberately so. The large type 99 counts "during official play" in
2017 and 2026 come from one late official event after the offseason has started, and offseason folding
is a separate, already closed decision. Noted for the record: `2026isrtp` (19 matches, 2026-04-03) is
type 99 inside the official season.

## The design

1. **One named rule, in `eventTypes.ts`:** a preseason match is PREDICTED and never FOLDED. Offseason
   matches keep folding, because `carryFrom: "last-official-match"` already discards what they teach
   before it can reach an official match. Nothing discards what preseason teaches, because preseason
   comes first. That asymmetry is the whole reason the fold rule is not `isOfficialEventType`.
2. **Applied at every fold site:** `WalkForwardSimulator.run` and `runAll` (level 1),
   `SigmaScoutLayer.foldPlayed` (Sigma, RP beliefs, RP mean shift), and the Worker tick's fold loop.
   A preseason match still gets a prediction, a band and RP odds, read from unchanged state, so its
   event page still renders.
3. **Scoring and loaders:** the scored-set exclusion and the `excludeOffseason` loaders treat type 100
   as unofficial, through `isOfficialEventType`, so backtests and published accuracy stop scoring it.
4. **Versions:** every algorithm whose published numbers move gets a new version. Decided by the
   level-1 digest, not by guess.
5. **Pinned:** tests that fail if a type 100 match moves state or reaches the scored set.

Known consequence, accepted up front: OPR is event-scoped and starts each season empty, so under OPR
a preseason event page shows predictions from no ratings and no team totals. EPA and SPR show their
carried ratings, flat across the event.

## The measurement, fixed before any number exists

Instrument: `scripts/captureCompareSlices.ts`. It runs the real `publishSeasons` with `dryRun` and
keeps the Compare page slices, so every arm is scored by the published scorer.

Three captures from three commits, so the two effects are separated rather than blended:

| Arm | Code | Isolates |
|---|---|---|
| A | before any change | the published numbers as they stand |
| B | scoring and loader change only | removing preseason matches from the SCORED SET. Official predictions are identical to A by construction |
| C | the no-fold rule on top | official predictions changing because preseason no longer teaches |

Read on the `qualification` view, per algorithm and season plus a pooled row weighted by each arm's
own scored count. **C against B is the model effect. B against A is bookkeeping.** Reported as found,
including any season that regresses.

This is a correctness rule Jacob asked for, not a tuning candidate, so there is no accept or reject
bar on accuracy. The numbers go to Jacob before anything is published, and he decides.
