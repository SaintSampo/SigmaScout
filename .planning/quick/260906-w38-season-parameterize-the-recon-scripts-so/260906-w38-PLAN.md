## TBA's ranking-point field naming is NOT stable across FRC eras

| season | total keys | booleans | booleans whose name carries an RP-shaped suffix |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2016 | 39 | 2 | **NONE** — the two booleans are `teleopTowerCaptured`, `teleopDefensesBreached` |
| 2017 | 34 | 8 | `kPaRankingPointAchieved`, `rotorRankingPointAchieved` |
| 2018 | 37 | 3 | `faceTheBossRankingPoint`, `autoQuestRankingPoint` |
| 2019 | 51 | 4 | `completeRocketRankingPoint`, `habDockingRankingPoint` |
| 2022 | 40 | 3 | `cargoBonusRankingPoint`, `hangarBonusRankingPoint`, **plus `quintetAchieved`** |
| 2023 | 37 | 4 | `sustainabilityBonusAchieved`, `activationBonusAchieved` |
| 2025 | 33 | 8 | `autoBonusAchieved`, `coralBonusAchieved`, `bargeBonusAchieved` |

A name-suffix heuristic fails in **both** directions:

- **False negatives.** 2016's actual bonus RPs (Breach and Capture) are named for the game action,
  not for the RP. A suffix filter finds ZERO RPs for 2016 and the script silently reports nothing.
  2016 is explicitly in this job's declared scope, so this is not hypothetical.
- **False positives.** `quintetAchieved` (2022) is a prerequisite, not an RP.
  `coopertitionCriteriaMet` (2023, 2025) is not an RP. 2025's four `g*Penalty` booleans are
  penalties. A suffix filter presents all of them as ranking points.

**Therefore the correct design is to enumerate EVERY boolean field in the breakdown and report
each one's base rate, rather than filtering by name.** The human reading the output identifies
which are RPs. This works identically for every era including 2016, cannot silently miss an RP,
and cannot invent one. Boolean counts are 2–8 per season, so enumerating all of them is cheap and
readable — there is no volume argument for filtering.
