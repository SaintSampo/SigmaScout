as playoffs go on? top four > finalist > winner. two awards should not be a predicted
possibility ever. dont touch quals or alliance selection. Im just trying to clean up

**Untouched, by instruction:** the Qualification and Alliance selection cells and their
drawers, the status rule, the tenets, the lock math, the reservation and the pooled

## Task 1 — the bracket knows what has been played

`packages/core/districts/bracket.ts`, on the ONE existing `BRACKET_SETS` topology and
with no second routing table:

- `PlayedBracketMatch` — TBA's `(compLevel, setNumber, matchNumber)` plus the winning
  alliance number; `bracketSetIdFor` maps those coordinates onto a set id;
  `bracketDecisionKey` / `bracketDecisionsFromPlayedMatches` build the decision map.
- `routePlayedBracket(decisions)` — the PARTIAL routing the played matches alone
  determine: per set, resolve both feeds or leave the set undecided.
- `allianceBracketMilestones(routing)` — per alliance: `decided` with its placement,
  `finals`, `topFour`, or `alive`. The double-elimination argument for `topFour` is the
  winner of sf7/sf8 (reaches sf11, worst 3rd) or of sf9/sf10 (reaches sf12, worst 4th).

Proven on a real event: `data/fixtures/phase10/event-2026wasam.json`'s 16 played
elimination rows route to the placement multiset `{1..8}` and reproduce
`district-2026pnw.json`'s own earned `elim` points for every team on those alliances.

**Commit:** `feat(260925-uf8): route the bracket from the matches already played`

## Task 2 — the simulation conditions on the played bracket

`packages/core/districts/ledgerSimulation.ts`:

- new optional input `playedElimMatches`; the decider returns the played winner and
  consumes NO randomness for a match already decided.
- new result field `playoffMilestones` (team key -> milestone), computed once outside
  the draw loop and only where the alliances are KNOWN.

**Commit:** `feat(260925-uf8): the playoff draw conditions on the matches already played`

## Task 3 — never a stacked award

`awardOrderingTables.ts` gains `foldStackedAwardPoints` / `foldStackedAwardPmf` (15 and
above -> Impact 10; 13 -> Rookie All Star 8). The base-rate path draws from the FOLDED
pmf; the ordering path takes the highest single award of the draw rather than a sum, so
`composeOrderedAwardPoints` goes with the stack it computed.

**Commit:** `feat(260925-uf8): an award draw is never a stack of two awards`

## Task 4 — the playoff headline advances with the bracket

`pointSummary.ts` gains a THRESHOLD-conditioned pair (`chanceOfAtLeast`,
`conditionalMedianGivenAtLeast`, `pointThresholdSummary`) beside the existing pair,
which is untouched. `districtLedgerRows.ts` maps the played rows onto alliance numbers,
passes them per event at the live position only, and carries the milestone plus its
threshold summary on the elim cell. `districtLedgerCopy.ts` prints
`top 4 ~66%` / `~13 if top 4`, `finalist ~40%` / `~20 if finalist`,
`winner ~55%` / `~30 if winner`, or `~7` / `4th place`.

**Commit:** `feat(260925-uf8): the playoff cell advances top 4 to finalist to winner`

## Task 5 — outcome lists, and the grand total drawer

`districtLedgerOutcomes.ts` (new, pure) builds the named outcome rows from the SAME
distribution the cell's chance comes from. The drawer renders an outcome list for the
Playoffs and Awards cells instead of a histogram, and the grand total drawer draws its
plot ONCE beside a per-event contribution list.

**Commits:** two, one per drawer.

## Task 6 — the missing chance lines

Reproduced locally at `?at=2026wasam:awards` against the real fixtures: production's
`v1/district/2026pnw.json` is generation 2026-09-14 and predates phase 10's `state`,
`awardProfile` and `bakedEvents` fields, so the tab holds NO distribution at a rewound
position and 90 of 126 grand totals are `unavailable`. `buildAdvancementChanceRun`
refuses all-or-nothing on the FIRST one, so the whole district prints no chance.

The over-broad refusal is narrowed: an unavailable grand total EXCLUDES that team and
is disclosed, and the run still refuses when the excluded set could fill the capacity.
A component test reproduces the failure first. The CAUSE is a republish and is a deploy
note, not a code change.

**Commit:** `fix(260925-uf8): one unavailable grand total no longer silences the district`
