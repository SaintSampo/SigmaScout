# 260912-2vg — Renumber the second section 20 to 21, and sweep every citation

## Why

`docs/models/statbotics-breakdown-reference.md` has two `## 20.` headings. The `avg.py`
section was appended 2026-09-11 and took a number already in use.

The renumber itself is cosmetic. The citation sweep is not: the section numbers are written
by hand, **nothing validates them**, and a renumber that leaves a citation pointing at the
old number converts a *visible* duplicate into an *invisible* wrong reference. That is why
the todo insists both happen in one change.

## The disambiguation that drives every edit

After this change:

- **§20 = "Residual gaps — named, not guessed"** (unchanged number)
- **§21 = "`backend/src/data/avg.py` — where every `Year` aggregate is WRITTEN"** (was the second 20)

So every existing "section 20" citation has to be read for *which* section it means. A blind
find-and-replace is wrong in both directions.
