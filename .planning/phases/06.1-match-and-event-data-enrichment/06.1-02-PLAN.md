<objective>
Get the per-bonus data the Monte Carlo already computes out of `rp/distribution.ts` instead of
discarding it, and surface it on `Prediction` — without moving the prediction-stream digest.

Purpose: F-06-1's predicted half. `rp/distribution.ts`'s per-draw loop already calls
`ruleModule.predictThresholds(...)` for both alliances on every draw and throws away everything
except the outcome-RP bucket. `redPrediction.bonusFlags` — a record keyed by `ruleModule.bonusNames`,
exactly what `BonusRpDots` needs — is available on every iteration and currently unread. Tallying it
costs one increment per bonus per draw: no new draws, no second RNG stream, no second Monte Carlo
pass.

Output: `RpPmfResult` widened with per-bonus probabilities, `Prediction` widened with the published
form of them, and a measured proof that neither move touched the D-15 digest.
</objective>

<planner_decisions>
- **PD-04 — Per-bonus data travels as a positional ARRAY aligned to `ruleModule.bonusNames`, not as
  a `Record<string, number>`.** RESEARCH.md and PATTERNS.md both sketched a record; this plan
  deliberately diverges, for two measured reasons. (1) **Consumer fit:** `BonusRpDots` already
  indexes `states?.[index]` against `bonusRpForSeason(season)`'s ordered array, so an array is the
  shape the only consumer actually wants, and `apps/web/src/lib/bonusRp.test.ts` already asserts
  that order matches `module.bonusNames` exactly for every registered season — the positional
  contract is pinned by an existing, passing test rather than by a comment. (2) **Payload budget:**
  on the budget-critical artifact (the 292-match `frc118/2024/sigma1` team page, 70,138 bytes of
  headroom under a 375,000-byte ceiling), the record form costs roughly 238 bytes per match row
  against the array form's roughly 124 — a difference of about 33,000 bytes on that one artifact,
  which is close to half the entire remaining headroom. Publishing a record would spend headroom
  this phase also needs for F-06-3's percentiles. **Reversibility: costly** — changing the wire
  format after publication needs another full ~22-minute republish plus a client change, but breaks
  no contract that cannot be re-satisfied and needs no migration. Not one-way, so no
  `checkpoint:decision`.
- **PD-05 — The predicted per-bonus probabilities are NOT put through `roundPmf`.** They are
  independent per-bonus marginals, not a distribution that must sum to 1; `roundPmf`'s residual
  redistribution would be actively wrong applied to them. Rounding happens once, at the publish
  boundary, in plan 06.1-05. **Reversibility: reversible.**
</planner_decisions>
