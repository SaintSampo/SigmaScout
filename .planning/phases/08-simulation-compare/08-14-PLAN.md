<objective>
The mock-before-build pass against real 1000-draw data, then the rank-distribution table itself.

This plan opens by discharging `chart-craft.md`'s mock-before-build obligation for real. `D-05`'s own
planner note names it as a planner obligation and UI-SPEC restates it as a contract: before this
component ships, mock the histogram **and** the interpolated-quantile calculation against a genuine
1000-draw distribution from a real event. Sketch 005 found the defects lived in the
edge-computation choice itself, not only in the rendering, so the mock exercises
`continuousQuantile()` against real draws rather than eyeballing finished bars. Two sampling rules
are binding and both come from mistakes that were actually made: **measure every row**, because
sketch 005's own session sampled the first 12 rows of a 39-row table and was wrong about the field
by a factor of three; and **sample a rewind event where `EventTeamSchema.rp` is absent**, so D-12's
summed-fallback baseline is exercised against reality rather than only against well-ranked events.

Then the table: four columns — Team # linking to `/team/{teamNumber}` and pinned, Nickname
truncating with a `title` and pinned, Median as a plain `numeric-cell` integer and the ascending
sort key, and Distribution. The distribution cell composes three layers on **one shared 1..N rank
axis drawn once in a header row**: histogram bars in `--sim-hist-bar`, one thin bar per occupied
rank with height proportional to the draws landing there and capped at `HIST_BAR_MAX_H`, which is
the layer that makes bimodality visible; the 10th–90th percentile band in `--sim-band-overlay`,
translucent, full `ROW_PLOT_H`, spanning `x(p10)` to `x(p90)` at the interpolated non-integer
quantiles and overlaid on top of the histogram so the reader sees the middle 80% of that same
distribution in one glance; and the median tick in `--sim-median-tick` at `MEDIAN_TICK_W`, drawn
last so it is never obscured. Every position derives from 08-04's single `x()` — no second
hand-tuned position is computed anywhere. The band's text label reads `10th–90th: {p10}–{p90}` to
one decimal and is never a plus-minus quantity. There is no probability-of-top-8 or captain column.

Purpose: this table is the Simulation tab's entire reason for existing, and it is the surface where
this product's honesty premise is most easily broken without anybody noticing. A snapped band edge,
a band drawn half a rank off its bars, a double-applied alpha, an invisible one-draw bar — every one
of them renders a confident-looking plot that is quietly wrong, and none of them is visible from a
passing unit test. Sketch 005 made two of those mistakes already, against real data, in a throwaway.
This plan measures first and builds second so the same mistakes cannot be made a third time.

Output: a runnable, credential-free measurement script with its committed record; a pure row builder
with its tests; the table component with its tests; the mount in 08-09's layout stack; and one new
tick-selection function in 08-04's axis module.
</objective>

## Decisions this plan makes (Claude's Discretion, recorded not assumed)

1. **The median tick is drawn at the CONTINUOUS 50th percentile from `continuousQuantile()`, and the
   Median column prints that same value rounded half-up and clamped into `[1, N]`.** This deviates
   from sketch 005's own renderer, which drew its tick at an integer median offset by half a slot.
   The deviation is deliberate and narrow: the sketch's recorded decision is `continuousQuantile()`
   and the interpolated band edges — its median handling appears nowhere in
   `simulation-and-compare.md` and is an implementation detail of the same class as the half-slot
   offsets 08-04 already corrected. Using one estimator for all three positioned marks is
   `chart-craft.md`'s coupled-geometry rule applied to the median, and it is materially more
   honest on the case that matters: a perfectly bimodal team with 500 draws at rank 3 and 500 at
   rank 4 has a continuous median of exactly 3.5, while an integer median asserts a preference for
   rank 3 that the draws do not support. UI-SPEC's requirement that the Median COLUMN print an
   integer is honoured — the column is a display rounding of the drawn quantity, which is the
   ordinary relationship between a printed number and a plotted one, not a drift, because both come
   from one function. The measured consequence (how often the printed integer and the drawn tick sit
   visibly apart) is reported by the mock rather than left unquantified.
   — <reversibility rating="reversible">One call site's `p` argument and one exported display helper, both unit-tested, with the mock's own report available to justify a reversal.</reversibility>

2. **Histogram bar heights are normalized to each ROW's own modal bin count, not to a table-wide
   maximum.** This is sketch 005's own validated choice, read directly from its renderer, and it is
   not the per-row-scale the user rejected: what was rejected was a per-row RANK axis, which
   destroys the cross-row comparison the table exists for. The vertical axis here carries no ticks,
   no labels and no cross-row comparison — the band and the printed 10th–90th range carry
   concentration, and the histogram carries SHAPE, which is the bimodality D-05 names as its whole
   reason for existing. A table-wide maximum would render a broadly-spread team's bars at roughly
   5px of 32 at a real event, which is the invisible-mark defect arriving by a third route. The
   accepted cost is stated rather than hidden: equal bar heights in two different rows do not mean
   equal draw counts. The falsification criterion is measured in Task 2, not assumed.
   — <reversibility rating="reversible">One divisor in one pure function with its own unit test; the mock reports the data needed to evaluate the alternative without a second run.</reversibility>

3. **`rankAxisTicks()` is added to 08-04's `apps/web/src/lib/simAxis.ts` rather than to a new
   module or to the component.** Every rank-to-pixel concern in this app then lives in one file, and

   site, which is precisely the shape `chart-craft.md` records as a defect and which
