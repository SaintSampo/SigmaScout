<objective>
The live half of SC-1: the cron tick learns which districts are live, asks TBA what moved, and
republishes the district artifact it reads back from R2 — without a corpus, without simulating,
and without ever being able to blank a published district.

Today the Worker has no district concept anywhere in its source. This plan adds the one pass that
closes SC-1, built entirely out of pieces that already exist:

1. **Liveness from the manifest the tick already reads.** 10-03 put `districtKey` on
   `LiveWindowEntrySchema`; `loadLiveEventsAt` already returns validated live entries. Grouping
   them by `districtKey` is the whole discovery mechanism — no second R2 object, no corpus
   (RESEARCH Open Questions 2 and 3).
2. **One conditional TBA request per live district per tick**, through `tbaPoll.ts` (the module
   whose header says a second TBA client is two politeness policies that agree until the day they
   do not, and that day is a live event).
3. **The four per-event state facts derived from data the tick already paid for** — the parsed
   match list — plus one conditional `/event/{key}/awards` request per live event once its
   playoffs are done (CONTEXT "The four state facts, sourced"; this is the fetch that resolves
   RESEARCH Pitfall 4 and Assumption A3 in favour of fetching).
4. **The merge and the write.** `applyDistrictRankings` / `applyDistrictEventState` (10-03) do
   the merge and the `locks.ts` recompute; a new district writer mirrors `writeArtifactObject`'s
   body — including the secret-leak refusal — and bypasses `PageKind` exactly as the offline
   publisher's district keys already do (RESEARCH Pattern 2, Security Domain).

Purpose: SC-1's success criterion is that a category finishing during a live weekend turns grey on
the district page inside the freshness window. Everything else in the phase renders or computes;
this is the only plan that makes the published numbers move.

Output: the artifacts in `must_haves.artifacts`. Nothing here deploys (`npx wrangler deploy` is
10-09's, and this plan needs no `wrangler.toml` change at all — DB and ARTIFACTS are already
bound), nothing here publishes, and no test in it touches the network.

**Known freshness limit to record, not to fix:** a district's awards that post after every member
event's live window has CLOSED are not picked up until the next offline republish. The window is
padded one hour past the last observed match (`LIVE_WINDOW_PAD_MS`); an award ceremony later that
night falls outside it. State this in the module header and the SUMMARY; 10-08 carries it into
`docs/worker-operations.md`.
</objective>
