`.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` were read in full for their OPR-relevant sections.

**Disposition key:** `re-issue` — a live document; its OPR figures/prose are replaced outright by
this plan (Task 3), no history preserved in place. `annotate` — a completed Phase 1-3 SUMMARY; gets
a dated "superseded by Phase 3.2" pointer note with its original numbers left intact (D-16); this is
plan **03.2-06's** job, not this plan's — this row only records that the location was found and
requires that follow-up. `no OPR figure found on re-check` — the planning sweep's floor candidate
was re-checked here and does not, in fact, carry a stale OPR figure or claim.
