<objective>
Measure what event-scoped OPR actually does early in an event, where its design matrix is
rank-deficient, and record it rather than assume it is benign (SC-5).

Purpose: D-02's literal-zero cold start has a cost that this phase committed to stating in its own
units. The first qualification match of every event predicts 0-0 with pRedWin exactly 0.500 — a flat
0.25 Brier on those matches — and the degradation decays at some rate nobody has measured. D-07
requires two halves and rejects either alone: an accuracy curve with no mechanism leaves a reader
with the shape but not the cause, and a rank diagnostic that never states the prediction-quality cost
is a numerics note.

Output: `packages/harness/eventScopeDiagnostic.ts`, a report at `reports/event-scope-diagnostic.json`
(gitignored) and a committed summary at `data/diagnostics/opr-event-scope-2026-08.json` that plan
03.2-05 writes up.

**Bucket boundaries and the warm cut are planner's discretion under 03.2-CONTEXT.md, and are fixed
here so they are not re-litigated during execution.** Checkpoint buckets on completed qualification
matches at the event: `0`, `1-6`, `7-12`, `13-24`, `25-48`, `49+`. Rank checkpoints at 0, 6, 12, 24,
48 completed quals and at event end. D-09's warm cut excludes records whose event had completed
fewer than 12 qualification matches. The corpus measures a median event at 38 teams and 74
qualification matches, so 6 quals is roughly one alliance appearance per team, 12 is roughly two, 24
roughly four, 48 roughly eight — the buckets are spaced in units of "how many times has the average
team been observed here," which is the quantity that actually governs the fit.
</objective>
